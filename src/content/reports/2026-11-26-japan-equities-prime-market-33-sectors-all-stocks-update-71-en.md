## Key points

- **A re-rating market.** PER expansion (+20.9%) contributed roughly twice the EPS factor (+10.7%) on the Prime weighted average.
- **Breadth was real; leadership was narrow.** 1,227 of 1,536 stocks rose, but only 560 (36%) beat TOPIX.
- **Earnings-led winners were the durable ones.** Nonferrous Metals gained +79.0% on an EPS factor of +92.5% with the PER down; the median bank stock gained +99.4%.
- **The last three months unwound the AI trade.** TOPIX −7.5%; Nonferrous Metals −49.7% and Electric Appliances −17.2% (total return), while Insurance +12.6% and Pharmaceutical +14.8% led.
- **The extremes were single-stock stories.** Kioxia Holdings +887.1% at the top; GMO Internet −87.5% at the bottom.

## Part 1 — The market frame

## 1. Market frame — a re-rating market, not an earnings market

**Multiples, not earnings, drove the 18 months.** TOPIX returned +38.6% including dividends (price +32.8%). The Prime weighted-average PER rose from 15.7x to 19.0x (+20.9%), while the implied EPS factor added +10.7% — roughly two-thirds of the price gain was re-rating. PBR rose from 1.37x to 1.60x. The Nikkei gained +50.2% in price. The last three months have begun to unwind it: TOPIX −7.5% total return.

## Part 2 — The 33 sectors

## 2. Sector returns and valuation — dispersion was the story

In Banks, 97% of stocks beat TOPIX and the median stock gained +99.4%. Financials and resources led on earnings; Information & Communication and Machinery lagged in the middle of a capex boom. The last three months flipped the leadership: Insurance (+12.6%) and Pharmaceutical (+14.8%) led, and Nonferrous Metals halved.

Electric Power & Gas (EPS factor −38.0%, PER contribution +83.9 log pts) and Marine Transportation (EPS factor −71.7%, PER contribution +100.2 log pts) show the two forces pulling hard in opposite directions. ## 6. Methodology, data notes and discrepancies

**Method.** The window is the close on 30 May 2025 to the close on 25 Nov 2026 (18 months); the last three months run from 25 Aug 2026. TOPIX total return is proxied by ETF 1306. Price returns use adjusted prices from Yahoo Finance. Sector PER and PBR are from the JPX size- and sector-level tables; the May 2025 values are price-adjusted from the Jun 2025 and Oct 2026 tables. Sector aggregates are weighted by current share counts, so they differ slightly from the official sector indices. The EPS factor is implied, decomposing each sector’s price change into EPS and PER log contributions. Excess returns vs TOPIX are geometric.

**Data note.** The per-stock PER and EPS columns are empty for every stock in the source data (0 of 1,547 rows). The stock tables therefore show returns, market cap, size class and analyst counts only.

**Discrepancies found on republication (stated, not smoothed over).** The stocks file carries 1,547 company rows; 1,536 have an 18-month return and form the headline universe (the other 11 are mostly recent listings). Machinery shows 109 rows in the stocks file against 107 companies in the sector table; the two extra rows (544A GMS Group, 575A Maezawa Holdings) have no 18-month return.
