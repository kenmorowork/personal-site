Edge-AI cycle, record orders: Advantech is the world's largest industrial-PC maker (an estimated ~34% share of the embedded IPC market, third-party estimates) and is repositioning as an edge-AI platform provider. After two down years (revenue −6.1% in FY2023 and −7.4% in FY2024), FY2025 revenue recovered 18.6% to NT$70.9bn and **3Q 2026 produced the strongest quarter in the company's history: revenue of NT$32.44bn (+83% YoY), a record 20.9% operating margin and a book-to-bill ratio of 1.64x**. Strengths: a design-in model with long product lifecycles and high customer stickiness, unmatched breadth across verticals and regions, a lightly levered balance sheet and 26+ years of uninterrupted dividends. Weaknesses: **gross margin is being squeezed by memory and component costs (36.7% in 3Q26 against a 40% long-term target)**, the order surge is concentrated in cyclical semiconductor equipment, and founder succession is an open item after management flagged a CEO-transition announcement on the 3Q26 call.

## Key points

- **Record 3Q26.** **Revenue NT$32.44bn (+83% YoY, +24% QoQ) crossed US$1bn in a single quarter for the first time; operating profit NT$6.80bn at a record 20.9% margin**; net income to parent NT$6.22bn (+125% YoY); EPS NT$7.15. Nine-month revenue reached NT$78.95bn (+49% YoY) with EPS of NT$16.21 — already above FY2025's full-year NT$12.14 (company results, 29 Oct / 4 Nov 2026).
- **Orders are the story.** **Book-to-bill hit 1.64x in 3Q26 (North America 2.12x, Europe 1.52x, China 1.50x) and 1.60x in October; semiconductor-equipment book-to-bill reached 2.62x**. Orders have grown for five consecutive quarters and the backlog covering November, December and 2027 deliveries stands at about US$2.5bn (company, 4 Nov 2026).
- **Guidance labelled conservative.** **Company guidance for 4Q26 is revenue of US$970–990mn (at NT$31.8/US$), gross margin 36–38% and operating margin 18–20%**. Management called the guide conservative — October revenue set a record at about US$352mn — and expects high double-digit revenue and profit growth in 2027 (company commentary, not formal guidance).
- **Margin is the tension.** **Gross margin fell to 36.7% in 3Q26 from 38.9% a year earlier as SSD and DDR4 costs rose faster than pass-through pricing**; price contributed 20.5pt of the 49.1% nine-month revenue growth. Management's long-term target is a return to a 40% gross margin (3Q26 conference).
- **AI mix rising fast.** **AI-related revenue grew 109% YoY in 3Q26 to 24.5% of sales**, just below the company's 30% annual ambition; US design wins reached 161 projects with an estimated annual revenue of US$687mn (+262% YoY) (company, 4 Nov 2026).

## Latest update — 3Q 2026 results (29 Oct / 4 Nov 2026)

3Q 2026 was a record quarter on every headline line. Revenue of NT$32.44bn rose 83% YoY and 24% QoQ, the first quarter above US$1bn. Gross profit was NT$11.90bn; the 36.7% gross margin was down 2.2pt YoY on sharp increases in key component costs, particularly SSD and DDR4 memory. Operating leverage more than offset this: the opex ratio fell to 15.7% of sales and **operating profit reached NT$6.80bn, a record 20.9% operating margin**. Non-operating income of NT$1.11bn included dividend income and a one-off gain on disposal of a Japan office building. Net income to parent was NT$6.22bn (+125% YoY) and EPS NT$7.15, both quarterly records (company 3Q26 results and investor-conference transcript, 4 Nov 2026).

Regionally, North America, China and Taiwan led order growth; semiconductor equipment was the standout vertical (book-to-bill 2.62x), with smart healthcare also strong. Management noted some customers are ordering early to secure allocation on components with lead times beyond 20 weeks, and that deferral/cancellation rates remain on its watch list — the first qualification to attach to the backlog quality.

## Guidance and outlook commentary

Advantech guides one quarter ahead. For 4Q 2026 the company guided revenue of US$970–990mn at an assumed NT$31.8/US$, gross margin of 36.0–38.0% and operating margin of 18.0–20.0% — a sequential revenue decline of roughly 3–5% in NT$ terms against 3Q26, which management attributed to the year-end holiday season, fewer working days and an ERP system upgrade in November, while stating that October's record shipments (about US$352mn of revenue) leave 4Q revenue with a chance to exceed 3Q26. For 2027, management expects high double-digit growth in both revenue and profit, supported by the US$2.5bn backlog; this is directional commentary from the 4 Nov 2026 conference, not formal guidance, and is labelled as such throughout this report.

## Business description

Founded in Taipei in 1983 by KC Liu and two fellow Hewlett-Packard engineers, Advantech built its position in industrial PCs (IPCs) — ruggedised computers, embedded boards and systems designed for factory, medical, retail, transport and energy environments, sold on long lifecycles (typically 5–7+ years of supply commitment) rather than consumer-style refresh cycles. The portfolio spans embedded computing (boards, modules, edge systems), industrial automation (controllers, I/O, HMI), intelligent systems (medical and retail terminals, in-vehicle and railway systems) and service/solutions. The WISE-IoT software platform and the sector-driven 'AIoT co-creation' model — building solutions with ecosystem partners rather than selling boxes alone — are the company's stated route from hardware to platform economics, under the vision of 'Enabling an Intelligent Planet'.

Production is centred on Taiwan (including the Linkou AIoT campus) and Kunshan, China, supplemented by regional assembly and configuration centres; the new North America headquarters in Tustin, California, integrating local assembly, technical service and customer collaboration, began production in 2026 — a direct response to North America's order surge and to customers' localisation requirements. Advantech employs more than 9,000 people globally (company material; 9,478 per data vendors, Nov 2026).

## Products, technology and the design-in model

The economic engine is design-in: Advantech products are specified into customers' equipment designs, after which the customer buys the same configuration for the life of the machine — often a decade. **This produces sticky, annuity-like revenue, high switching costs and gross margins (high-30s to ~40%) well above commodity PC levels**, at the cost of long qualification cycles and exposure to customers' capex cycles. The current technology push is edge AI: GPU-accelerated edge systems and AI inference boxes that run models on factory floors, in hospitals and in semiconductor tools, working with the silicon ecosystem (Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, MediaTek, Arm, Microsoft and Canonical all feature in the company's partner material). AI-related revenue reached 24.5% of sales in 3Q26, up 7.3pt YoY.

## Market and value chain

Advantech sits between component suppliers (CPUs, memory, displays, power) and system integrators / end-equipment makers across dozens of verticals. The industrial-PC market is a niche of the broader computing industry — third-party estimates put Advantech's share of the embedded IPC segment at roughly one-third (~34%, Verified Market Research 2026 estimate; the company has historically cited a 34%+ global IPC share), several times the next competitor. Named competitors include Kontron, ADLINK and AAEON in embedded computing, Beckhoff and Siemens in automation-adjacent computing, and the edge-server lines of the large IT vendors at the high end.

==Entry barriers are the installed design-in base, breadth of catalogue (1,000+ products), global service coverage in 26+ countries==, and the longevity/reliability record that regulated verticals (medical, rail, semiconductor tools) require. Substitutes are real but bounded: customers can design in-house (only economical at large scale), buy white-box industrial PCs at lower specification, or shift workloads to cloud/standard servers where environment and latency permit — the edge-AI trend cuts the other way, pushing compute back to the machine. The main structural risk to the model is the one visible in 3Q26: component cost inflation (memory) that cannot be passed through immediately, compressing the gross margin the model is built on.

## Segments and regions

Since the FY2025 financial statements, Advantech reports five segments. **Embedded Design-In is the largest (FY2025 revenue NT$25.6bn, 36.1% of sales; segment margin 20.0%), followed by Intelligent Systems (NT$24.0bn, 33.9%; margin 23.1%)** — the fastest-growing, +28.3% in FY2025 on semiconductor-equipment and AI-infrastructure demand. IoT Automation is small (NT$5.1bn) but the most profitable (38.5% segment margin); Intelligent Service (NT$7.5bn, 8.7%) and Advantech Service Plus & Others (NT$8.7bn, 7.1%) are lower-margin, service- and channel-weighted. Segment profit is profit before tax by segment before central costs (company definition; segment note, FY2025 statements). Earlier years were reported under two superseded structures; the PL/segment sheet preserves those on their own bases rather than restating them, and this report follows the same discipline.

By customer location (FY2025): Asia NT$24.2bn (34.2%), USA NT$22.6bn (31.9%), Europe NT$14.0bn (19.7%), Taiwan NT$4.8bn (6.8%), Others NT$5.3bn (7.5%). The US was the growth engine of the last cycle (revenue +27.0% in FY2022, +13.0% in FY2023, −10.2% in FY2024, +19.0% in FY2025) and leads the current one (3Q26 book-to-bill 2.12x). Regional profit is not disclosed.

## Financial statements summary

Profit and loss (audited, via PL/segment sheet): FY2025 revenue NT$70,882mn; gross profit NT$28,198mn (39.8%); operating expenses NT$16,631mn, of which R&D NT$5,816mn (8.2% of sales); operating profit NT$11,567mn (16.3%); non-operating income NT$1,123mn (interest income NT$341mn, share of associates NT$362mn); pre-tax profit NT$12,690mn; net income to parent NT$10,593mn; diluted EPS NT$12.14. The balance sheet is lightly levered: total assets of NT$75.7bn and shareholders' equity of NT$54.9bn at end-FY2025 (data-vendor compilation of company filings), with interest-bearing debt minimal against cash — finance costs were just NT$89mn in FY2025. Working capital is the swing factor: inventories ended FY2025 at NT$11.8bn (about 3.1 months of cost of sales, calculated) after the FY2022 peak of NT$14.0bn, and capex stepped up to NT$2.8bn in FY2025 (FY2024: NT$1.5bn) as capacity and the Tustin site were built out.

Cash conversion was weak in the FY2022–24 inventory build and recovered as inventories normalised; the 3Q26 commentary (allocation-driven early ordering by customers) argues for watching receivables and inventory months as the first quality check on the current upcycle.

## Strategy and mid-term targets

Advantech's strategy is sector-driven: organise around verticals (semiconductor equipment, medical, retail, energy, transport), sell platforms and co-created solutions rather than components, and make edge AI the growth engine. Stated ambitions from company material: **AI revenue at 30% of sales (24.5% in 3Q26), a 2030 vision of becoming the global leader in edge-AI platform solutions**, and a long-term gross-margin recovery to 40% (management, 3Q26 conference). There is no published multi-year numerical mid-term plan of the Japanese type; the quantified commitments are the quarterly guidance, the AI-mix ambition and the margin target, and this report treats them as such. On the 4 Nov 2026 call, CEO Eric Chen also flagged that a formal announcement on the founder-CEO transition would follow the next day — succession at a founder-led company (KC Liu, chairman since 1983) is a governance item to track alongside the numbers.
