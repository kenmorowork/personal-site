## Key points

- **A strong year, decided in November.** TOPIX +14.40% (12M), Nikkei +20.52%; record close 72,366.34 on 25 Jun, trough −22.0% on 20 Nov, +3.91% rebound on 24 Nov.
- **Financials and transport led.** Marine Transportation +63.3%, Services +60.2%, Insurance +51.3%, Banks +48.0% (12M to 30 Oct); Construction and Other Products lagged.
- **Guidance revisions wrote the leaderboard.** The biggest OP upgrades top the coverage table (Sodick's guide doubled; Harmonic Drive +64.5%); one of only two cuts, Yaskawa, sits near the bottom.
- **The bottom is not an earnings-collapse list.** MHI, Kawasaki Heavy and IHI raised guidance and still fell −14% to −27% — expectations, dilution and earnings quality, not demand.
- **Japan beat the US; Asia split.** S&P 500 +6.43% (12M) with defensive leadership; TAIEX +49.09%, KOSPI +29.76%, Hang Seng −14.19%; USD/JPY +1.32% on the year.

## Part 1 — The year in one story

## 1. Market overview — a policy-and-AI year, decided in November

**A policy-and-earnings year, decided by a positioning accident in November.** TOPIX +14.40% and the Nikkei +20.52% over twelve months. The path ran from the Takaichi policy trade and the LDP's February landslide (316 seats) into the Iran-war break — TOPIX more than 10% below its record by 23 Mar — then the year's fastest recovery after the 8 Apr ceasefire. The Nikkei's record close was 72,366.34 on 25 Jun. Summer brought the yen crisis (USD/JPY 163.988 intraday on 22 Jul) and ¥15.39tn of intervention (30 Jul–3 Aug). September tightened in sync: the BOJ to 1.25%, the Fed back up to 3.75–4.00%. November re-priced the AI complex in nine sessions — Huang's resignation (12 Nov), Tokyo −5.06% on 20 Nov (trough 56,436.65, −22.0%), reinstatement on 23 Nov, a +3.91% rebound. No guidance cut or capex cut drove the fall.

## 2. The year month by month — rhythm, breadth, drawdowns and flows

**Three regimes, two tiers.** The year's gains were made by August — best month February (TOPIX +10.44%), worst March (−11.19%) — defended into October, then partly surrendered in November. Breadth was narrow at the top: 38 of 54 coverage names rose over 12M, but the median (+12.8%) lagged TOPIX and only 24 of 52 comparable names beat it; sectors were healthier (28 of 33 positive, median +12.5%). Foreign investors supplied the bid: +¥10,490bn net in 2026 to 13 Nov, 92% of it in H1. The 10-year JGB yield rose ~100bp to a 3.111% high on 7 Oct, then rallied to ≈2.7–2.8%.

## 6. Top movers — the reasons

**Earnings revisions, not momentum.** Advantech (+106.7%): record 3Q26 — revenue +83% YoY, book-to-bill 1.64.
