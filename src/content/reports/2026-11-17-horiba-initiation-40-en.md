Measure what matters — and right now what matters is the semiconductor fab: Horiba, Ltd. (6856, TSE Prime) is the Kyoto-based measurement and analysis group whose analysers sit inside car-engine test cells, environmental monitoring networks, hospital laboratories and — decisively for the current P&L — semiconductor fabs, where its mass-flow controllers and process-measurement tools meter the gases and liquids from which chips are made. The initiation question is whether Horiba is still the diversified analyser conglomerate its history describes, or has become a semiconductor process-measurement company with two smaller fields attached. The nine months to September 2026 answer it numerically: group revenue was ¥281,659mn (+22.3% YoY) and operating profit ¥50,673mn (+51.2%), of which **the Advanced Materials & Semiconductor field alone contributed ¥161,189mn of revenue (+40.4%) and ¥50,224mn of operating profit (+53.1%) — 99% of the group total (calculated)**. Full-year FY2026 company guidance was raised for the third time on 13 November 2026, to revenue of ¥392,500mn and operating profit of ¥73,000mn (company guidance). Strengths: (1) a genuine niche moat in measurement — a decade of reported figures shows the old Semiconductor segment earning a 36.7% segment margin in FY2024 (¥44,178mn on ¥120,466mn) and the new semiconductor field printing a 31.2% margin in 9M FY2026 (calculated), on products (mass-flow control, process measurement) that are specified into fab tools and hard to displace; (2) a fortress balance sheet — owners' equity ratio 67.1% at December 2025, cash of ¥162,471mn against interest-bearing debt of ¥66,483mn (sheet definition, corrected — see Basis of preparation), and operating cash flow of ¥54,383mn in FY2025; (3) guidance momentum with unusual candour about its mix — **three upward revisions in FY2026 (February ¥56,000mn operating profit → November ¥73,000mn)**, each attributed by the company to semiconductor demand and recent order trends, with the offsetting cuts (Energy & Environment, Bio & Healthcare) disclosed in the same documents. Weaknesses: (1) concentration is now the risk, not the remedy — one field is effectively the whole profit pool, Bio & Healthcare has lost money for two consecutive years (operating loss of ¥894mn in FY2025 and ¥1,263mn in 9M FY2026), and Energy & Environment operating profit fell 15.4% in 9M FY2026 on European hydrogen-market stagnation; (2) the guidance is back-end loaded — 9M delivered 69.4% of guided operating profit, so Q4 must print ¥22,327mn (calculated), above any quarter of 9M, on semiconductor order conversion the company does not quantify (order intake and backlog are not disclosed in the tanshin — 未取得); (3) comparability and capital-return optics need care — the FY2025 segment reorganisation (five segments into three fields) breaks the segment series (FY2024 quarters were never restated onto the new basis), and the FY2026 dividend forecast of ¥790 includes ¥430 of special and commemorative dividends that are explicitly one-off in character, ahead of a 1-for-5 stock split effective 1 January 2027. All historical figures are J-GAAP as reported by the company via the PL/segment sheet built from tanshin in the JPX disclosure archive (FY2016–FY2025, ten years); the only forward figures are company guidance and the company's MLMAP2028 plan targets, labelled as such. No rating or price target is assigned at initiation (Under Review).

- **9M FY2026: semiconductor did all of it.** For the nine months to 30 September 2026 (J-GAAP), revenue was ¥281,659mn (+22.3% YoY), operating profit ¥50,673mn (+51.2%; 18.0% margin), ordinary profit ¥52,172mn (+51.8%) and net income attributable to owners ¥36,345mn (+50.8%); basic EPS was ¥865.18. Source: Q3 FY2026 tanshin, 13 November 2026.
- **Guidance raised three times — and Q4 has to be the biggest quarter of the year.** Full-year FY2026 company guidance (会社計画) was raised on 13 November 2026 to revenue ¥392,500mn, operating profit ¥73,000mn, ordinary profit ¥74,000mn and owners' net income ¥51,500mn (EPS ¥1,225.56), from an initial ¥345,000mn / ¥56,000mn / ¥56,500mn / ¥40,500mn on 12 February 2026, via ¥373,000mn / ¥68,000mn on 14 May and ¥382,000mn / ¥70,000mn on 7 August 2026 (guidance tracker, verified against the tanshin chain). The FX assumptions were left unchanged at USD/JPY 155 and EUR/JPY 180. With 9M operating profit at ¥50,673mn, **the guide implies Q4 operating profit of ¥22,327mn (calculated) — above the strongest 9M quarter (Q3: ¥20,185mn, tanshin supplement)** — predicated on semiconductor shipments the company supports by reference to 'recent order trends' it does not disclose numerically. Source: Q3 FY2026 tanshin, 13 November 2026; guidance tracker 2026-11-17.
- **Strength — the moat shows up in the segment margin, for a decade.** On the old basis in force through FY2024, **the Semiconductor segment's margin rose from 24.9% in FY2016 (¥9,678mn on ¥38,828mn) to 36.7% in FY2024 (¥44,178mn on ¥120,466mn)**, and it was the group's largest profit contributor in every one of the ten years in Exhibit 2. On the new basis, Advanced Materials & Semiconductor earned ¥44,517mn on ¥156,500mn in FY2025 (28.4% margin) and ¥50,224mn on ¥161,189mn in 9M FY2026 (31.2%, calculated). Full-year FY2026 segment guidance is revenue ¥215,500mn and operating profit ¥66,000mn (company guidance, revised 13 November 2026) — a 30.6% guided margin. Source: company results via PL/segment sheet (tanshin, JPX archive); Q3 FY2026 tanshin segment guidance table.
- **Weakness — the other two fields do not currently pay for themselves.** **Bio & Healthcare has printed an operating loss in FY2024 (restated new basis: −¥890mn), FY2025 (−¥894mn) and 9M FY2026 (−¥1,263mn)**, and its full-year FY2026 guided loss was widened on 13 November 2026 from −¥500mn to −¥1,000mn, which the company attributes to investment in the life-science field amid intensifying competition. Source: Q3 FY2026 tanshin and supplement, 13 November 2026; PL/segment sheet.

## 1. Key indicators and financial history

Horiba reports with a December year-end under J-GAAP. The history in this chapter and in Exhibit 1 is as reported in each year's own annual tanshin, compiled in the PL/segment sheet for 6856: the main window covers FY2021–FY2025 and a supplementary history block adds FY2016–FY2020, giving the ten annual years the Shared Research depth standard calls for (all template checks equal zero; register S1–S19). Figures are ¥mn unless stated.

Revenue describes a company that compounded quietly, then stepped up twice. Sales were ¥170,093mn in FY2016, rose through the semiconductor and emissions-regulation cycle to ¥210,570mn in FY2018, fell for two years (¥200,241mn in FY2019, ¥187,080mn in FY2020) as semiconductor capex corrected and COVID hit the automotive and medical lines, then inflected: ¥224,314mn in FY2021 (+19.9%), ¥270,133mn in FY2022 (+20.4%), ¥290,558mn in FY2023 (+7.6%), ¥317,369mn in FY2024 (+9.2%) and ¥333,081mn in FY2025 (+5.0%) — a ten-year revenue CAGR of 7.7% (calculated). Operating profit is more cyclical than revenue: ¥18,499mn (10.9% margin) in FY2016, ¥26,834mn in FY2017, a then-peak ¥28,838mn (13.7%) in FY2018, down to ¥20,916mn and ¥19,694mn in FY2019–20, then ¥32,046mn (14.3%) in FY2021, ¥45,843mn (17.0%) in FY2022, ¥47,296mn (16.3%) in FY2023, ¥48,340mn (15.2%) in FY2024 and ¥53,040mn (15.9%) in FY2025. The margin structure is the story: gross margin expanded from 41.0% in FY2016 (calculated) to 43.9% in FY2025 as the mix shifted toward semiconductor process measurement, while the SG&A ratio was held in a 27–31% band (30.1% in FY2016; 28.0% in FY2025, calculated from the sheet).

## 3. Business description

Horiba is headquartered in Kyoto and organises itself, since the FY2025 reorganisation under the MLMAP2028 plan, into three reportable fields — Energy & Environment (エネルギー・環境), Bio & Healthcare (バイオ・ヘルスケア) and Advanced Materials & Semiconductor (先端材料・半導体) — replacing the five segments (Automotive, Process & Environmental, Medical-Diagnostic, Semiconductor, Scientific) in force through FY2024. The field definitions below follow the company's own presentation (FY2025 and Q3 FY2026 tanshin qualitative sections and segment notes): the fields are application groupings that cut across the old product segments — Scientific (analytical instruments) products, for example, are distributed across all three fields — which is precisely why the old and new bases are never spliced in this report. Segment profit is operating profit, and on the new basis the disclosed adjustment column is nil, so field profits sum to consolidated operating profit. In FY2025 the mix was Advanced Materials & Semiconductor 47.0% of external revenue, Energy & Environment 40.4% and Bio & Healthcare 12.7%; in 9M FY2026 the semiconductor field reached 57.2% of revenue (calculated).

## Advanced Materials & Semiconductor: the mass-flow and process-measurement core

This field sells the measurement and control layer of the semiconductor fab and of advanced-materials production: mass-flow controllers that meter process gases with precision, liquid and gas concentration monitors, wafer and reticle inspection and measurement, and related process-measurement instruments sold to semiconductor-equipment makers and device makers, principally in Asia. Demand is derived from wafer-fab equipment (WFE) spending with an advanced-node tilt — the company attributes FY2025 and 9M FY2026 growth to generative-AI-driven advanced-semiconductor demand and to higher sales to equipment makers in Asia. The financial profile is that of a high-share niche supplier: on the old basis the Semiconductor segment (the closest ancestor, though not identical in scope) grew revenue from ¥38,828mn in FY2016 to ¥120,466mn in FY2024 — a 15.2% CAGR (calculated) — at segment margins rising from 24.9% to 36.7%; **on the new basis the field printed ¥156,500mn / ¥44,517mn in FY2025 (28.4% margin**; the company notes profit fell 1.0% YoY despite +4.5% revenue growth because new-product and technology development investment was accelerated) and ¥161,189mn / ¥50,224mn in 9M FY2026 (31.2% margin, calculated). Full-year FY2026 guidance is ¥215,500mn / ¥66,000mn (company guidance). The open product question — how much of the growth is mass-flow controllers versus other process measurement, and whose share is being taken — is question 3 of the interview pack and is not answered in the tanshin disclosure. Source: company results via PL/segment sheet (tanshin, JPX archive); FY2025 tanshin qualitative section; Q3 FY2026 tanshin.

## Energy & Environment: automotive test, process and environmental measurement

Energy & Environment combines the group's original automotive franchise (engine and driveline test systems, emissions measurement, combustion measurement for hybrid-vehicle development), process measurement for industry, and environmental monitoring (air, water) — the businesses carried in the old Automotive, Process & Environmental and parts of Scientific segments. FY2025 revenue was ¥134,407mn (+5.5% YoY on the restated FY2024 figure of ¥127,422mn) and operating profit ¥9,417mn (+120.9% on ¥4,262mn), as a slower EV shift lifted hybrid-development combustion-measurement demand in Europe and the Americas (company text). The field's earnings are, however, the group's most seasonal and most policy-exposed: quarterly operating profit in FY2025 ran ¥754mn / −¥243mn / ¥1,513mn / ¥7,393mn (tanshin supplement) — Q4 delivers the year — and in 9M FY2026 revenue grew only 2.7% to ¥88,319mn while operating profit fell 15.4% to ¥1,712mn, with European destination sales in the field down 13.5% YoY in 9M (supplement destination table) as the hydrogen-related market stagnated; the company cut the field's FY2026 operating-profit guidance to ¥8,000mn (from ¥9,000mn) on 13 November 2026 while holding revenue at ¥134,000mn — i.e. guiding the field to a full-year revenue decline of 0.3% versus FY2025. Whether the drag is hydrogen timing, automotive measurement or price is interview-pack question 5; the tanshin names hydrogen-market stagnation in Europe as the guidance-cut reason. Source: FY2025 tanshin qualitative section; Q3 FY2026 tanshin and supplement.

## Bio & Healthcare: diagnostics with a life-science investment drag

Bio & Healthcare is the medical-diagnostics business (blood-cell counters / haematology analysers, clinical chemistry and related diagnostic instruments, centred on Europe) extended toward life-science applications — the old Medical-Diagnostic segment plus the life-science parts of Scientific. It is the smallest field and currently loss-making: revenue of ¥40,155mn (FY2024, restated) and ¥42,173mn (FY2025, +5.0%) with operating losses of ¥890mn and ¥894mn in both years; 9M FY2026 revenue rose 9.0% to ¥32,150mn on higher blood-cell-counter sales mainly in Europe, but the operating loss was ¥1,263mn and the full-year guided loss was widened to ¥1,000mn (from ¥500mn) on 13 November 2026, which the company attributes to the cost burden of increased investment in the life-science field amid intensifying competition. On the old basis, Medical-Diagnostic was profitable in most years (segment profit of ¥2,806mn in FY2016 on ¥26,564mn; ¥638mn in FY2023; −¥150mn in FY2024), so the field's current losses are a mix of scope change (life-science investment now sits inside the field) and competitive pressure — the two cannot be separated from tanshin disclosure alone, and the interview pack carries the field's MLMAP2028 target arithmetic as a live debate. ## 4. Market and value chain

Horiba sells instruments, not commodities: in each field it sits at the measurement layer of a customer's process — the fab tool, the engine test cell, the emissions-monitoring station, the hospital laboratory — between component suppliers (precision fluidics, optics, detectors, electronics) and equipment makers, laboratories and regulators who specify its products into their own systems. That position explains both the moat and the cyclicality. ==Entry barriers are qualification and installed-base trust: a mass-flow controller or emissions analyser written into a customer's tool or regulatory method is re-ordered and serviced for years, and switching requires re-qualification==; application know-how accumulated over decades of measurement science is the company's stated core competence. The substitute risk is integration — equipment makers internalising measurement, or general-purpose sensing commoditising a niche — which is why the product split of semiconductor-field growth (interview-pack question 3) matters more than the growth rate itself.

Demand drivers differ by field and are currently moving in opposite directions. Advanced Materials & Semiconductor rides WFE and advanced-node capex: the company cites generative-AI-driven advanced-semiconductor demand and Asian equipment-maker sales as its 9M FY2026 driver, and FIELDNOTES' Measurement & Inspection sector report (2026-11-16) identifies Horiba's Q3 as the sector's strongest print, driven by mass-flow controllers and semiconductor process measurement. Energy & Environment rides vehicle-development spending (currently hybrid-led as the EV shift slows), industrial process investment and environmental regulation — with hydrogen-related demand, an expected growth leg, stagnating in Europe on the company's own account. Bio & Healthcare rides hospital and laboratory diagnostics demand in Europe, with a competitive pricing environment the company flags explicitly. No company-disclosed market-size or market-share figure for Horiba's categories is carried in the sources used here, and none is substituted: the verifiable demand evidence is Horiba's own reported field growth, its destination sales (Chapter 5) and the guidance language on order trends. The two sensitivities that follow for the equity: first, Asia (including China) was 41.7% of FY2025 destination sales (supplement basis, calculated) and is the semiconductor field's centre of gravity, so China exposure inside that field — revenue share and export-control sensitivity — is a first-order unknown (interview-pack question 8); second, with no order-intake or backlog disclosure in the tanshin, the earliest observable of a semiconductor pause is the quarterly field print itself, one quarter late. ## 7. Strategy and mid-term plan — MLMAP2028

Horiba's mid- to long-term management plan 'MLMAP2028' (announced 14 February 2024) reorganised the group around the three fields that became the FY2025 reportable segments, and set FY2028 targets of revenue ¥450,000mn, operating profit ¥80,000mn and ROE of at least 12% (company plan targets, as summarised in the interview pack from the company's plan materials). The 'MLMAP2028 Update' briefing of 10 August 2026 raised the FY2028 targets to revenue ¥460,000mn, operating profit ¥90,000mn, net income ¥62,000mn and ROE of 14.0%, with new-business sales of ¥35,000mn (company plan targets, company briefing material via the interview pack) — while cutting field-level targets for Energy (on the changed hydrogen market) and partly for semiconductor solutions and life science in the same update, so the group raise leans on mix rather than uniform growth. That internal tension is the plan's central credibility question, and it is interview-pack question 6: **the FY2028 group operating-profit target of ¥90,000mn sits 23.3% above even the raised FY2026 guidance of ¥73,000mn (calculated)**, and on the FY2026 segment guide the semiconductor field alone would supply ¥66,000mn of it. The FY2026 trajectory check is therefore mechanical: a FY2027 initial guide (due February 2027) with operating profit well below roughly ¥80,000mn would break the MLMAP2028 path (interview-pack yardstick); the plan's own arithmetic requires the semiconductor field to keep compounding while Energy & Environment recovers from a guided revenue decline and Bio & Healthcare returns from losses to its field target.

Capital allocation under the plan, as evidenced rather than as promised: the stated dividend policy targets a payout of about 30% of consolidated net income, applied flexibly with special dividends and buybacks (FY2025 tanshin); delivery has run ahead of the target — payout ratios of 33.8% (FY2024) and 50.9% (FY2025) as reported, and a FY2026 forecast of ¥790 per share including ¥430 of special/commemorative dividends, alongside a 1-for-5 stock split effective 1 January 2027 that the company frames in its separate split notice (referenced in the Q3 tanshin). Investment is rising into the cycle: CF-basis capex of ¥26,142mn in FY2025 (from ¥9,171mn in FY2023), company-defined capex of ¥27,451mn, and the HORIBA World Headquarters Project — a new global headquarters on the current Kyoto head-office site, construction begun December 2025 with completion planned for January 2028 (company announcement, February 2026).

The monitoring set, in the order the data arrives: Q4 FY2026 standalone operating profit against the ¥22,327mn implied by guidance (calculated) — the raise lives or dies there; the semiconductor field's full-year revenue/operating profit against its ¥215,500mn / ¥66,000mn guide and any first disclosure of order intake or backlog by field; Energy & Environment's Q4 (its seasonally decisive quarter) against the cut ¥8,000mn guide; Bio & Healthcare's loss trajectory against the widened −¥1,000mn guide; FY2027 initial guidance against the MLMAP2028 path; and working capital — receivables and inventory growth versus the +22.3% 9M revenue growth (interview-pack question 9).
