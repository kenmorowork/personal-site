The Nikkei 225 fell for a second day on Tuesday 17 Nov, closing the morning session down 705.35 points (-1.18%) at 58,956.67 after dipping below 59,000 for the first time since 9 Nov, and stood at 58,809.84 (-852.18) at 13:00 JST with TOPIX at 3,837.40 (-63.97). Selling centred on AI and semiconductor names after the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 5.28% in the US on 16 Nov following the Commerce Department's tighter AI chip export rules: Advantest was the largest drag on the index, SoftBank Group hit a year-to-date low, and Tokyo Electron, Ibiden, Kioxia and Fujikura also fell, while Fast Retailing, KDDI, Terumo and Otsuka rose. Sources: Nikkei, 17 Nov 2026 (morning close); Kyodo via Okinawa Times, 17 Nov 2026, 13:00 JST.

**Why it matters:** Day two of the de-rating is broader than day one — the drag now runs from test equipment through cables (Fujikura) — so tonight's Nvidia print has to stabilise the whole JP AI chain, not just the equipment names.
