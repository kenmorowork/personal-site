Mitsubishi Logistics (9301) will buy back up to 13,000,000 shares, 3.9% of shares outstanding, for up to ¥10bn from 1 December 2026 to 31 May 2027, and cancel all shares acquired on 15 June 2027. The shares closed up 2.0% at ¥1,277.5 on the announcement day, and the previous programme bought 6,716,500 shares for ¥9,999,969,100, cancelled on 13 November 2026.

**Why it matters:** Same size, second time in a row, with full cancellation — the ¥10bn is a programme, not a signal event.
