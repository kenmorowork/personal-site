Taiwan's Liberty Times (22 Nov) reports Nomura Securities arguing the selloff confused cash flow with profitability: Amazon, Meta, Microsoft and Alphabet's data-centre capex creates a J-curve, while AWS, Google Cloud and Azure each grew revenue more than 39% year on year in Q3 with operating margins above 36% and large order backlogs. Former fund manager Steve Eisman warned hyperscaler AI infrastructure capex reaches $850bn this year and a 1 GW data centre costs about $30bn to build. Figures: Liberty Times, 22 Nov 2026, citing Nomura Securities and Eisman.

**Why it matters:** Nomura's J-curve framing separates the cash-flow squeeze from profitability: cloud revenue growth above 39% with operating margins above 36% and large order backlogs is the counterweight to the $850bn capex figure and the roughly $30bn cost of a 1 GW data centre.

**野村称半导体遭错杀：超大规模云厂商2026年AI资本支出达8500亿美元，云业务三季度营收增逾39%、营业利润率逾36%**



台湾《自由时报》11月22日报道，野村证券认为市场混淆了现金流与盈利，数据中心支出先流出、收入在5至20年间确认，形成J曲线效应；AWS、Google Cloud与Azure三季度营收同比均增逾39%，营业利润率均逾36%，未履约订单庞大。前基金经理艾斯曼警告，超大规模云厂商今年AI基础设施资本支出达8500亿美元，一座1吉瓦数据中心造价约300亿美元。数据来源：《自由时报》，2026年11月22日，引野村证券与艾斯曼。



野村的J曲线框架把现金流压力与盈利能力分开看待：云业务营收增逾39%、营业利润率逾36%且未履约订单庞大，是对8500亿美元资本支出与单座1吉瓦数据中心约300亿美元造价的另一侧砝码。
