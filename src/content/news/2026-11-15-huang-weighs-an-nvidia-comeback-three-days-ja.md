Jensen Huang has discussed returning as Nvidia chief executive with confidants, the Financial Times reported on Sunday 15 Nov (via Reuters), three days after his 12 Nov resignation. One ally is assembling a 'war room' of Silicon Valley executives to lobby the board and seek White House support, and an open letter backing reinstatement passed 152,000 signatures by Sunday. The board says it is choosing a search firm and has not formally engaged candidates, though the FT reported it approached Arm CEO Rene Haas; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia needs a 'dramatic shake-up' and an outside CEO. Nvidia reports Q3 on Tuesday 17 Nov under interim CEO Colette Kress.

**Why it matters:** The succession fight, not the quarter, is now the priced variable into Tuesday's print: a comeback bid, a board search and a Treasury demand for an outsider cannot all win.

**黄仁勋辞职三天后考虑重返英伟达——盟友组建“作战室”，贝森特要求外聘CEO**



据英国《金融时报》11月15日（周日）经路透社报道，黄仁勋在11月12日辞职仅三天后，已与亲信讨论重返英伟达CEO一事。一名盟友正组建由硅谷高管组成的“作战室”，游说董事会并寻求白宫支持；支持其复职的公开信到周日已获逾15.2万个签名。董事会称正在遴选猎头公司、尚未正式接触候选人，但《金融时报》称其已接触Arm首席执行官Rene Haas；财长贝森特周日表示英伟达需要“大刀阔斧的改革”并应外聘CEO。英伟达将于11月17日（周二）在临时CEO Colette Kress任内发布三季度业绩。



进入周二业绩发布的定价变量已是接班之争而非季度本身：复职游说、董事会遴选与财长要求外聘，三者不可能同时获胜。
