Tokyo core consumer prices rose 2.9% year on year in November, the fastest pace since July 2025, with headline CPI up 2.8% and the core-core index up 2.5%. Electricity rose 9.5% and city gas almost 14% after government utility subsidies ended, service prices rose 2.1%, and rice prices fell almost 34%, the biggest decline in data going back to 1971.

**Why it matters:** The acceleration was energy-led while the core-core measure eased — headline and underlying inflation moved in opposite directions into the December Bank of Japan meeting.
