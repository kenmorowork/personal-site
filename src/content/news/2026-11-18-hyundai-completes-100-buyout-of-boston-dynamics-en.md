Hyundai Motor Group affiliates and Chairman Chung Euisun now hold 100% of Boston Dynamics after SoftBank exercised its put on its remaining 9.65% stake, disclosed in Hyundai Glovis's Q3 filing on 16 Nov (Edaily, 18 Nov). Boston Dynamics' revenue for Jan–Sep 2026 was ₩136.0bn with a net loss of ₩626.6bn; the group targets Atlas capacity of about 30,000 units a year by 2028, and GlobalFoundries will field-test Atlas from early 2027 as the humanoid's first customer outside autos and the first semiconductor maker in the early-adopter programme.

**Why it matters:** Full ownership removes the SoftBank overhang just as Atlas moves from car plants into chip fabs — the GlobalFoundries test is the first semiconductor-fab validation for a humanoid, directly relevant to fab automation names.
