No production timetable or customer was disclosed. Source: Digitimes, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Panel makers testing glass-core substrates would widen the advanced-packaging substrate contest beyond incumbent suppliers; with no timetable or customer disclosed, prototypes are the only verified step.
