Micron has begun tool installation at its US$7 billion HBM advanced-packaging plant in Singapore, targeting production in early 2027 and about 1,400 semiconductor jobs, according to Semiconductor Projects' 20 Nov project news roundup.

**Why it matters:** Tool installation is the milestone that moves the $7bn Singapore HBM packaging plant from construction toward its early-2027 production target — added advanced-packaging capacity for the memory layer of the AI stack.

Source: Semiconductor Projects · 20 Nov 2026 — [original article](https://semiconductor-projects.com/news)

**美光在新加坡70亿美元HBM封装厂开始设备安装**



据Semiconductor Projects 11月20日报道，美光已在其新加坡70亿美元HBM先进封装厂开始设备安装，目标2027年初投产，创造约1,400个半导体岗位。



设备安装是70亿美元新加坡HBM封装厂从建设走向2027年初投产目标的里程碑——为AI供应链的存储环节再添先进封装产能。
