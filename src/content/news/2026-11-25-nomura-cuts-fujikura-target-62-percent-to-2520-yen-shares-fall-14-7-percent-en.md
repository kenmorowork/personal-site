Nomura Securities cut its rating on Fujikura (5803) from Buy to Neutral and its target price from ¥6,700 to ¥2,520 in a report dated 24 November. The shares fell 14.70% to close at ¥2,062.5 on 25 November. Nomura expects strong results for the year ending March 2027, but judges that the high profitability of data-centre optical components for Southeast Asia will not continue from the year ending March 2028.

**Why it matters:** A core data-centre optics name has been repriced on how long its margins last, not on its near-term earnings.
