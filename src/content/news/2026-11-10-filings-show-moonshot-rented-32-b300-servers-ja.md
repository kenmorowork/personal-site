Bloomberg reported on 10 Nov that Beijing regulatory filings show Moonshot AI signed a contract in August to rent 32 Super Micro servers equipped with Nvidia B300 GPUs, valued at RMB 432m (about $64.8m) and located in China — a possible breach of US rules if no export licence was obtained, which Bloomberg could not confirm. Moonshot said it has no business relationship with the server owner Lingmeixin and disputed the documents; after Bloomberg sought comment, references to Moonshot were removed from the filings. Figures: Bloomberg review of PBOC credit-database filings, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Renting, not buying, at a reported premium is the workaround channel to watch — it links straight to the Nvidia board-probe story already on this dashboard.

**文件显示月之暗面在中国境内租用32台B300服务器——月之暗面否认文件内容**



彭博11月10日报道，北京监管文件显示月之暗面于8月签约租用32台搭载英伟达B300 GPU的超微服务器，估值4.32亿元人民币（约6480万美元），服务器位于中国境内——若未获出口许可，可能违反美国规定，彭博未能确认是否取得许可。月之暗面称与服务器所有方凌美芯无业务关系并质疑文件真实性；彭博寻求置评后，文件中月之暗面的相关表述被删除。数据来源：彭博查阅央行征信数据库文件，2026年11月10日。
