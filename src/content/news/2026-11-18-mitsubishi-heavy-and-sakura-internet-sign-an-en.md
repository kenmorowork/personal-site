Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and Sakura Internet announced on 18 Nov an MOU to study a strategic alliance for domestic digital infrastructure in the sovereign AI and sovereign cloud era. The two areas are joint construction of a secure, high-performance domestic infrastructure base with vertically integrated services, and a highly efficient, rapidly buildable modular AI data centre, including a reference architecture and modular construction methods for electromechanical equipment. Sakura Internet was selected by the Digital Agency as a fiscal 2026 government-cloud provider, the first domestic cloud operator so chosen. Source: Sakura Internet news release, 18 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Sovereign AI is becoming a construction story in Japan: a heavy-industry builder plus the first domestic government-cloud operator studying modular AI data centres together links the heavy-equipment and AIDC coverage in one dated MOU.
