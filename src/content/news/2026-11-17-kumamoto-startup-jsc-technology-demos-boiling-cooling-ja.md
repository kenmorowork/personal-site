JSC Technology, a Kumamoto University spin-out, announced on 16 Nov its 'Silent Jet' boiling-cooling technology for AI servers, developed with Professor Yasushi Koito's group: refrigerant boiling drives a jet at the heat-receiving surface with no pump on the secondary loop. Lab tests confirmed cooling of heat loads above 2kW and heat flux above 110W/cm², with development targeting up to 3.6kW. Server integration and long-run reliability testing are still to come, and no productisation timing was given — the result is lab-stage. Source: AI-CHI News summary of the JSC Technology announcement, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Pump-free boiling cooling at 2kW+ per device speaks directly to the power constraint in AI data centres — but with no product timing, it is a technology watch item, not a supply-chain event.

**熊本初创JSC Technology实验室演示单器件2千瓦以上沸腾冷却**



熊本大学衍生企业JSC Technology于11月16日发布面向AI服务器的“Silent Jet”沸腾冷却技术，与小糸康志教授团队共同开发：冷媒沸腾在受热面形成喷流，二次侧循环无需泵。实验室测试确认可冷却2千瓦以上热负荷、110W/cm²以上热流密度，开发目标为最高3.6kW。服务器集成与长时间可靠性测试尚待进行，产品化时间未公布，现阶段为实验室成果。来源：AI-CHI News对JSC Technology发布的摘要，2026年11月17日。



单器件2千瓦以上的无泵沸腾冷却直指AI数据中心的电力约束，但由于没有产品化时间表，它属于技术观察项，而非供应链事件。
