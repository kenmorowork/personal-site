Hitachi is in on-site testing of UBTECH's Walker S2 industrial humanoid for material-handling tasks in elevator manufacturing workshops, under a strategic partnership announced on 11 May 2026. The two sides say the work could extend beyond elevators to healthcare, industrial equipment and semiconductor manufacturing equipment. (AI123 News Hub, updated 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Humanoid deployment is moving through factory validation first — Hitachi is using its own plants as the test bed before any wider rollout.
