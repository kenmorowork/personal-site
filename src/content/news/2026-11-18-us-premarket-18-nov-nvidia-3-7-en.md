Nvidia rose 3.7% in premarket trading on 18 Nov, with Nasdaq 100 futures up 1.17% and S&P 500 futures up 0.62%, while gold fell 2.1% to $3,769.68 and the 10-year Treasury yield was 5.095% (Reuters). The FOMC minutes of the 27-28 Oct meeting, when the Fed held at 3.75-4.00% by 10-2, are due at 14:00 ET; Lowe's reported comparable sales up 1.8% and raised its full-year sales outlook to about $92.5bn.

**Why it matters:** Premarket says the Nvidia beat is being bought — Nasdaq futures up 1.17% — but the 14:00 ET FOMC minutes and a 5.095% 10-year yield are the rates check the cash session has to pass before the rebound is confirmed.
