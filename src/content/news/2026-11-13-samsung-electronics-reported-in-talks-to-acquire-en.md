Per CLS on 13 Nov, Samsung is negotiating to buy Modine Manufacturing (market cap about $5.6bn; shares down about 15% since its disappointing 3 Nov results), with a deal possible within weeks, though talks could still fail or draw rival bidders. Daikin, LG Electronics and Vertiv are also competing for data-centre cooling opportunities.

**Why it matters:** Cooling is becoming an M&A battleground: a Samsung move on Modine would put it directly against Daikin, LG Electronics and Vertiv in data-centre cooling — a direct read-across for Japan's thermal and AIDC supply names.
