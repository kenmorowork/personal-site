JSR reported Apr-Sep 2026 net profit of ¥15.6 billion, 4.6 times the year-earlier level, on revenue of ¥265.1 billion, up 28%, and operating profit of ¥42.0 billion, 2.2 times higher (Nikkei, 16 Nov 2026). Advanced lithography materials for Korea, Taiwan and Asia drove growth on AI demand; the digital solutions margin reached 23.5%.

**Why it matters:** Advanced lithography materials are showing operating leverage to AI demand across Korea, Taiwan and Asia, with digital solutions margin at 23.5% as the quality check.

**JSR 4至9月净利润达上年同期的4.6倍，先进半导体材料表现强劲**



JSR公布2026年4至9月净利润为156亿日元，达到上年同期的4.6倍，营收为2651亿日元，同比增长28%，营业利润为420亿日元，达到上年同期的2.2倍（日经，2026年11月16日）。面向韩国、台湾和亚洲的先进光刻材料在AI需求带动下增长，数字解决方案部门利润率达到23.5%。



先进光刻材料正显示出对韩国、台湾与亚洲AI需求的经营杠杆，数字解决方案部门23.5%的利润率是质量检验点。
