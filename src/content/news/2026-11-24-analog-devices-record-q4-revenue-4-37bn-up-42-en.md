Analog Devices reported fiscal Q4 2026 (ended 31 Oct 2026) revenue of $4.37 billion, up 42% year on year, and fiscal 2026 revenue of $15.2 billion, up 38%, a record. For Q1 fiscal 2027 it guided revenue of $4.4 billion, plus or minus $150 million. (PR Newswire, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** A 42% jump led by data centre demand is a direct read-across for power-management and analogue exposure to AI infrastructure spending.
