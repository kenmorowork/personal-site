SMIC's Q3 2026 revenue was US$3.19 billion, up 33.9% year on year and a record, with net profit of US$428.4 million, 2.2 times higher, gross margin of 29.9% (22.0% a year earlier, 25.3% in Q2) and utilisation of 97.3% (TECH+, 16 Nov 2026). China accounted for 90.8% of revenue; Q4 guidance is revenue up 4 to 6% quarter on quarter with gross margin of 26 to 28%.

**Why it matters:** Record revenue at 97.3% utilisation with China at 90.8% of sales shows SMIC running full on domestic demand; Q4 guidance implies margin normalising from Q3's 29.9%.
