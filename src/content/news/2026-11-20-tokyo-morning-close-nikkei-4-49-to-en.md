TOPIX fell 3.12% to 3,703.30 and the intraday Nikkei drop topped 2,800 points to a roughly seven-month low; TSE Prime turnover was ¥4.19tn with decliners outnumbering advancers 1,402 to 127. Advantest was the largest single drag (about −631 points) and SoftBank Group fell more than 16% intraday, below ¥3,000 for the first time since August 2025.

**Why it matters:** Breadth confirms the move: 1,402 decliners against 127 advancers on Prime, with the two biggest AI-linked index weights — Advantest and SoftBank Group — doing the damage; the seven-month low sets the tone into the US session.
