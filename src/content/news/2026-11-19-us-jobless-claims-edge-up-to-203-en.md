Seasonally adjusted initial claims rose 1,000 to 203,000 in the week ended 14 Nov 2026, above the Reuters-polled consensus of 197,000; the prior week was revised up to 202,000, the four-week average rose to 197,750, and continuing claims rose 70,000 to 1.763m in the week ended 7 Nov (US Department of Labor, released 19 Nov 08:30 ET).

**Why it matters:** Claims remain low in absolute terms but printed above consensus with continuing claims up 70,000 — a softening edge to the labour data rather than a break, and one more input for the December Fed and BOJ reads.
