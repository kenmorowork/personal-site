In the Federal Reserve's November 2026 Financial Stability Report (13 Nov), 87% of the 23 market contacts surveyed in September–October named AI a salient risk to US financial stability over the next 12–18 months, up from 50% in the spring survey and 30% in fall 2025; the next most-cited risks were a risk-asset correction (52%) and higher long-term rates (48%). Fed staff put debt issuance tied to advanced computing above $680bn by October 2026, about five times the $130bn issued in all of 2023, at about 31% of nonfinancial investment-grade issuance versus under 6% in 2023; outstanding related public debt exceeds $1.1tn, and for seven large firms signed-but-not-yet-started leases exceed $1tn with other commitments above $2tn. The survey records contacts' views, not a Fed forecast, and it closed before Huang's 12 Nov resignation.

**Why it matters:** The regulator now sizes the build-out's off-balance-sheet layer itself: leases and guarantees above $3tn combined are the number Tuesday's Nvidia guidance will be read against.

**美联储调查：AI以87%成为头号金融稳定风险——先进计算相关债务发行突破6,800亿美元**



在美联储2026年11月金融稳定报告（11月13日发布）中，9–10月受访的23位市场联系人中有87%将AI列为未来12–18个月美国金融稳定的显著风险，高于春季调查的50%和2025年秋季的30%；其次是风险资产回调（52%）与长期利率上行（48%）。美联储工作人员测算，与先进计算相关的债务发行到2026年10月已超过6,800亿美元，约为2023年全年1,300亿美元的五倍，占非金融投资级债券发行的约31%，而2023年不足6%；相关存量公开债务超过1.1万亿美元，七家大型企业已签约未开工的租赁超过1万亿美元、其他承诺超过2万亿美元。该调查记录的是联系人观点而非美联储预测，且在黄仁勋11月12日辞职之前已结束。



监管者已亲自测算建设潮的表外层规模：租赁与担保承诺合计逾3万亿美元——周二英伟达的指引将被放在这个数字下审视。
