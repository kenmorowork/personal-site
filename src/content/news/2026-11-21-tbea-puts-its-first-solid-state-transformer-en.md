At a new power systems conference in Tianjin on 21 Nov, TBEA unveiled its Ruiyu-series solid-state transformer and said it has entered mass production at its Tianjin base with annual capacity of 200 units, targeting AI computing centres, heavy-truck supercharging and new-energy sites; 29 TBEA products passed a national appraisal the same day, 18 rated internationally leading.

**Why it matters:** Mass production — 200 units a year at Tianjin — moves TBEA's solid-state transformer from unveiling to supply, with AI computing centres among the named target markets as the SST field widens beyond the established names.
