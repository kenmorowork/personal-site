ASUS introduced Gallop, the first humanoid from its Physical AI Solutions group, on 13 Nov: it is learning a real workstation at a group EMS plant, packing motherboard accessories (15 small parts, many in soft bags), mostly by watching skilled workers, and runs on a single NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor computer (ASUS, 13 Nov). ASUS plans its own dexterous hand and a wheeled version over the coming year, a Computex 2027 showcase, customer pilots in 2027 and market availability in 2H 2028.

**Why it matters:** A top-tier ODM is entering humanoids through its own factory floor rather than a demo stage — the same factory-first route as Schaeffler's Taicang plant, and a new Taiwan name in the physical-AI supply chain.

**华硕发布首款人形机器人Gallop——已在集团工厂从事主板配件包装；计划2027年客户试点，目标2028年下半年上市**



华硕11月13日发布其Physical AI事业群的首款人形机器人Gallop：它正在集团EMS工厂的真实工位上学习主板配件包装（15个小零件，多为软包装），主要通过观察熟练工人来学习，并由一台NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor计算机驱动（华硕，11月13日）。华硕计划在未来一年推出自研灵巧手与轮式版本，在Computex 2027展示，2027年开展客户试点，目标2028年下半年上市。



一线ODM厂商不是从演示舞台、而是从自家工厂产线切入人形机器人——与舍弗勒太仓工厂相同的工厂先行路线，也为台湾物理AI供应链添了新名字。
