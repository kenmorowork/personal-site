The Nikkei 225 rose 672.88 points, or 1.17%, to close the morning session at 58,172.04 on Friday 27 November, extending the previous day's gain. TOPIX rose 1.56% to 3,809.02, 29 of the Tokyo Stock Exchange's 33 sectors advanced, and TSE Prime turnover reached ¥3,440.8bn, with Kioxia Holdings and Lasertec among the sharp gainers.

**Why it matters:** TOPIX outperformed the Nikkei by 0.39 percentage points with 29 sectors up — the rebound broadened beyond the AI names that led it.
