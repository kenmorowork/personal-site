Filings reviewed by Bloomberg show a GB300 NVL72 rack with 72 Blackwell Ultra chips pledged as collateral for a 96 million yuan (about $14m) loan, and a GB200 server listed at the same Shanghai facility, which was designed to house as many as 800,000 servers. Megaspeed, linked to the site, was added to a US trade blacklist this month.

**Why it matters:** The first documentary evidence of Blackwell in China arrived via loan filings, not shipping records.

Source: Bloomberg via Daily Caller · 27 Nov 2026 — [original article](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/27/nvidia-chips-us-exports-megaspeed-alice-huang-shanghai-data-center)

**英伟达Blackwell系统现身上海数据中心，一台GB300机柜被用于质押9600万元贷款**

彭博审阅的文件显示，一台装有72块Blackwell Ultra芯片的GB300 NVL72机柜被用作9600万元人民币（约1400万美元）贷款的抵押品，同一上海设施还登记了一台GB200服务器，该设施设计容量高达80万台服务器。与该设施相关的Megaspeed本月已被列入美国贸易黑名单。
