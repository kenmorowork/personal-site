South Korea's Newsworks reported on 16 Nov (via The Robotics Media, updated 22 Nov) that Samsung Electronics and its wholly owned Rainbow Robotics are building a humanoid hand with five fingers, each driven by its own motor, sensing grip force through motor current rather than a separate touch sensor. The project sits in Samsung's Future Robot Promotion Group under Oh Jun-ho, with Seoul National University and Ajou University input; Samsung plans to show industrial and home-use applications at CES 2027 in January. Grip strength, battery life and pricing are not disclosed, and the report is based on sourcing rather than an official Samsung release.

**Why it matters:** A five-finger, direct-drive hand that senses grip through motor current would remove a separate touch sensor from the design; the CES 2027 prototype is the checkpoint, with grip strength, battery life and pricing still undisclosed and the report based on sourcing rather than an official Samsung release.
