Daikin Applied Americas unveiled the D60-W cooling reference architecture, a 60MW-class liquid-cooling design for AI factories built around Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin NVL72 system (Digitimes, 16 Nov 2026; announcement dated 11 Nov). Further detail is paywalled; the 60MW capacity figure is the verified number.

**Why it matters:** Cooling is being sold as a 60MW reference block for Vera Rubin NVL72 factories: the design win to watch is adoption, as detail beyond capacity is paywalled.

**大金发布面向英伟达AI数据中心的60MW液冷参考设计**



大金美国子公司发布了D60-W冷却参考架构，这是一套面向采用英伟达下一代Vera Rubin NVL72系统的AI工厂的60MW级液冷设计（Digitimes，2026年11月16日；发布于11月11日）。更多细节位于付费墙后，已核实的数字为60MW这一容量。



冷却正以60MW参考模块的形式面向Vera Rubin NVL72工厂销售：值得关注的是采用情况，因为容量之外的细节位于付费墙后。
