CG Power and Industrial Solutions said on 11 Nov it has won a Rs 900 crore (₹9.0bn) order from Tallgrass Integrated Logistics Solutions to supply power transformers for a large hyperscale data-centre project in the United States. The transformers will be designed, manufactured and tested at CG Power's facilities in India, with execution over 12 to 20 months. CEO Amar Kaul described the contract as the company's entry into the global data-centre segment. No site, MVA capacity or end-customer name was disclosed. Source: CG Power disclosure, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** An Indian maker winning US hyperscale transformer work from Indian factories is the supply-base widening Ken's transformer coverage is tracking — lead time, not demand, is the constraint.
