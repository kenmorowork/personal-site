UBTech (9880 HK) signed a strategic deal (announced 11 Nov) with luxury auto dealer Yongda Auto (3669 HK) for a first batch of 400 U1 Pro ultra-bionic humanoids, delivery planned within 2026 — its first publicly disclosed bulk order for a consumer-grade bionic robot. It follows a 9 Nov deal with a China Telecom subsidiary targeting 100,000+ humanoid terminals over three years. Figures: 21jingji, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Two channel deals in three days, including its first disclosed bulk order for a consumer bionic humanoid, move UBTech from demonstrations toward deliveries — volumes are still small next to the 100,000-terminal telecom target.
