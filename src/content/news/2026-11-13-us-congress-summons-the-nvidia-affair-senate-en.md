Senate Banking Committee chair Tim Scott wrote on 12 Nov asking resigned Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to testify on the company's recent developments, saying he would consider a subpoena if Huang does not volunteer (Reuters, 12 Nov). House Foreign Affairs chair Brian Mast said Huang will testify within the month; scrutiny centres on alleged chip smuggling to China via Singapore-based Megaspeed and on Commerce's refusal to blacklist dozens of Chinese firms, and Reuters reported White House officials discussing Lutnick's future.

**Why it matters:** The story has moved from personnel to export-control policy: testimony this month could tighten enforcement on third-country transshipment, which is the channel Japan's equipment and materials exporters also ship through.
