Intel slid over 2% in pre-market trading on Tuesday 17 Nov after a regulatory filing revealed that Nvidia reduced its stake in the chipmaker by 25% in the third quarter. The disclosure landed on the morning of Nvidia's own Q3 results, due after Tuesday's close, with consensus at about $109bn of revenue and adjusted EPS of about $2.48. Source: Barchart pre-market report, 17 Nov 2026.
