Preliminary closes on 18 Nov: the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.39% to 24,429.89, the S&P 500 fell 0.24% to 7,322.53 and the Dow fell 1.12% to 50,193.03 (Reuters). Nvidia fell even after beating estimates and guiding revenue higher, as uncertainty over Jensen Huang's departure persisted, and the semiconductor index closed slightly lower; minutes of the Fed's 27–28 Oct meeting kept the focus on inflation, with a few policymakers open to a 50bp hike if data worsen, and CME FedWatch pricing a December 25bp hike as near-certain.

**Why it matters:** The beat did not lift the sector: with the Fed minutes hawkish and a December hike near-fully priced, AI names now need delivery, not just guidance — tonight's Anthropic debut and the Palo Alto reaction are the next reads.
