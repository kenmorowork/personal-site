Economists expect headline PPI to rise 0.4% m/m in October (September: +1.3%) and 6.6% y/y, the highest since November 2022, with core PPI +0.3% m/m (September: +0.7%) and +5.0% y/y. CME FedWatch priced about a 90% chance of a December quarter-point hike as of 11 Nov; the 10-year Treasury yield closed at about 5.10% on 12 Nov.

**Why it matters:** With a December hike priced at about 90% and the 10-year already at about 5.10%, an upside PPI surprise tonight would pressure both US multiples and Tokyo's chip-heavy open on Monday.
