US-Armenian venture Firebird is building a large AI data centre at Hrasdan, Armenia, with investment of almost $5bn — about 20% of the country's GDP — according to NZZ am Sonntag (15 Nov 2026). The site is to run more than 70,000 GPUs at full build-out, among the world's five largest clusters, with Nvidia supplying chips and Dell the servers; AI search firm Perplexity is named as first customer. Power demand is to reach 300MW at full operation from end-2027, about 35% of Armenia's current national electricity consumption; a first section opened in August 2026.

**Why it matters:** Chip diplomacy is now placing near-GW-class AI capacity in small, power-constrained states — the binding constraint is again electricity (35% of national demand), not chips.
