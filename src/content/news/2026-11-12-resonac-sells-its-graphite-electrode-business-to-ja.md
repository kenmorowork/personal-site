Resonac Holdings (4004) said on 12 Nov it will transfer its graphite electrode business in Japan, the Americas and Europe to European private-equity fund Aurelius; the price was not disclosed. The transfer runs in three steps — a company split in Japan, a business transfer in Germany and share transfers of 13 companies — and is due to complete by April–June 2027. Resonac expects to book a loss of about ¥105bn as a non-recurring item in its FY December 2026 consolidated results, affecting IFRS operating profit. The same morning it also announced its exit from lithium-ion battery anode materials. This follows the 11 Nov guidance revision already on this dashboard: FY sales raised ¥70bn to ¥1,235bn and core operating profit raised ¥54bn to ¥250bn on advanced-semiconductor demand, with net profit guidance cut ¥37.5bn to ¥75bn. Figures: Reuters, 12 Nov 2026; Minkabu market wrap, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The graphite business that dragged Resonac's net guide is now formally leaving — the portfolio pivot to semiconductor materials is the story, and the ¥105bn one-off is its price tag.

**Resonac将石墨电极业务出售给Aurelius——计提约1050亿日元损失，聚焦半导体材料**



Resonac控股（4004）11月12日宣布，将把日本、美洲和欧洲的石墨电极业务转让给欧洲私募基金Aurelius，转让价格未披露。转让分三步实施——日本公司分割、德国业务转让、13家公司股份转让——预计2027年4至6月完成。Resonac预计在2026年12月期合并业绩中就此计提约1050亿日元非经常性损失，影响IFRS营业利润。同日早间，该公司还宣布退出锂离子电池负极材料业务。此前11日的业绩修正已在本看板记录：因先进半导体需求强劲，全年销售额上调700亿日元至1.235万亿日元，核心营业利润上调540亿日元至2500亿日元，净利润预期下调375亿日元至750亿日元。数据来源：路透，2026年11月12日；Minkabu，2026年11月12日。



拖累Resonac净利润预期的石墨业务如今正式剥离——向半导体材料转型的组合调整才是主线，1050亿日元的一次性损失是其代价。
