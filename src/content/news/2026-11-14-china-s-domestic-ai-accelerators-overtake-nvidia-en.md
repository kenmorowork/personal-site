The weekly briefing (14 Nov, synthesising reports to 13 Nov) puts Huawei Ascend first with over 570,000 units and a 28% share, Alibaba's T-Head third at 7%, and domestic accelerators combined ahead of Nvidia for the first time; it also reports SMIC's Q3 utilisation at 97.3%, up from 93.7% in Q2.

**Why it matters:** If domestic accelerators now outsell Nvidia inside China, the export-control debate in Washington (see the Bessent item above) and SMIC's near-full utilisation become two sides of the same substitution story.
