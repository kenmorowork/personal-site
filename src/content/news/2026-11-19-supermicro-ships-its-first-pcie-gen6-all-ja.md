Supermicro announced on 19 Nov at Supercomputing 2026 in Chicago that its first AMD EPYC 9006-based Petascale all-flash servers are now shipping, delivering PCIe Gen6 performance with up to 100% greater sequential read bandwidth than Gen5; the 2U system supports up to 32 E3.S NVMe SSDs for as much as 3.93 PB with 122 TB drives.

**Why it matters:** Storage bandwidth is part of the AI bottleneck alongside compute and power: Gen6 all-flash systems now shipping give the training and inference build-out a dated hardware checkpoint.

**Supermicro出货首批PCIe Gen6全闪存Petascale存储服务器，2U最高3.93PB，面向AI训练与推理**



Supermicro于11月19日在芝加哥Supercomputing 2026上宣布，首批基于AMD EPYC 9006的Petascale全闪存服务器开始出货，采用PCIe Gen6，顺序读取带宽较Gen5最高提升100%；2U系统最多支持32块E3.S NVMe SSD，使用122TB硬盘时容量最高达3.93PB。



存储带宽与算力、电力同属AI瓶颈：Gen6全闪存系统开始出货，为训练与推理建设提供了一个有日期的硬件检验点。
