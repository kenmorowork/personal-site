Wei Shaojun of the China Semiconductor Industry Association's IC design branch forecast at the 2026 IC Development Forum in Beijing on 19 Nov that China's 2026 chip industry sales will reach ¥1,582.53bn, +89.4% year on year, across about 4,324 companies in 20 cities, with 947 companies expected to exceed ¥100m in sales (Beijing Daily).

**Why it matters:** An 89.4% growth forecast for 2026 frames the scale of China's domestic chip build-out behind the buybacks and the licence fight — demand inside China is not the constraint; advanced-node access is.

**今年国产芯片销售额预计超1.58万亿元，同比增长89.4%**



中国半导体行业协会集成电路设计分会魏少军11月19日在北京举行的2026集成电路发展论坛上预计，今年中国芯片产业销售额将达到15,825.3亿元，同比增长89.4%，涉及20个城市约4,324家企业，其中947家企业销售额预计超过1亿元（北京日报／京报网）。



2026年89.4%的增速预期，勾勒出回购与许可之争背后中国国产芯片建设的规模——中国国内需求不是约束，先进制程的可获得性才是。
