The US EIA forecasts US electricity sales of 4.135 trillion kWh in 2026 and 4.211 trillion kWh in 2027, with the commercial sector taking 63% of the increase; large AIDC transformers now run at 150–200MVA and above with lead times of two to three years. Taiwan makers such as TECO and Tatung are shifting from equipment supply toward power-system integration and microgrids. Figures are EIA forecasts (Sep 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook), via CacheNews, 22 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The shift from transformer supply toward microgrid integration is the value-capture story: with US electricity sales forecast at 4.211 trillion kWh in 2027 and large AIDC transformers at 150–200MVA and above on two-to-three-year lead times, integration work takes a larger share of grid spend than equipment alone.
