Türkiye's Industry and Technology Minister Mehmet Fatih Kacır announced on 23 Nov a Humanoid Robot Development for Industrial Applications Call offering grants covering 50% of project budgets, with a 31 Dec 2026 deadline. The strategy prioritises domestic support for robot software, reducers, motors and motor drivers. Figures: minister's announcement, 23 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Governments are now subsidising the humanoid component chain — reducers and motors included — which is where the supply base Ken covers sits.
