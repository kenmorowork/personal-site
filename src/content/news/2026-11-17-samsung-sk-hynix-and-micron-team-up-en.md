Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron have reportedly formed an early-stage consultative body, alongside SEMI and other stakeholders, to standardise charging for mobile robots in semiconductor fabs, Seoul Economic Daily reported on 17 Nov. The work covers battery specifications, charging-station equipment, connectors, communication protocols and battery management systems for AMRs, mobile manipulators and humanoids, aiming to cut the cost and complexity of robot fleets and lay groundwork for fully autonomous fabs; Samsung and SK hynix have both outlined autonomous-fab plans by 2030. Source: Seoul Economic Daily via Tom's Hardware, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Charging standards are a prerequisite for mixed robot fleets inside fabs; the three memory leaders aligning with SEMI this early points to autonomous-fab spending broadening beyond process tools toward fab infrastructure.
