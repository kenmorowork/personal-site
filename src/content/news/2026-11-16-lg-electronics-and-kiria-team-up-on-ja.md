LG Electronics said on 16 November it signed an MOU with the Korea Institute for Robot Industry Advancement (KIRIA) on 13 November to jointly build a safety and usability evaluation system for humanoid robots, contribute to KIRIA's Humanoid Robot Safety Certification Center, and help develop national and international standards. LG will pursue a domestic pilot safety certification for its in-house humanoid LG CLOiD, and is developing a bipedal robot using Nvidia robotics technology for unveiling in the first quarter of next year.

**Why it matters:** Certification is becoming the gate for humanoid deployment in Korea: a CLOiD pilot plus a Q1 2027 bipedal reveal gives LG two dated checkpoints.

**LG电子与KIRIA合作推进人形机器人安全认证，CLOiD将试点认证，双足机器人计划于2027年一季度亮相**



LG电子11月16日表示，已于11月13日与韩国机器人产业振兴院（KIRIA）签署备忘录，共同建立人形机器人的安全性与易用性评估体系，参与KIRIA人形机器人安全认证中心的体系建设及国内外标准制定。LG还将为其自研人形机器人LG CLOiD申请国内试点安全认证；其采用英伟达机器人技术的双足机器人计划于明年第一季度亮相。



认证正成为韩国人形机器人落地的门槛：CLOiD试点认证与2027年一季度双足机器人亮相为LG提供了两个有日期的检验点。
