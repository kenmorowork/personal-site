Taiwan's Liberty Times (14 Nov 2026, citing Bloomberg / The Motley Fool) puts Rapidus's 2nm wafer price at about $21,000 against TSMC's about $30,000 — a discount of roughly 30% — while TSMC holds about 73% of the global third-party foundry market. TSMC's Q3 revenue was $46.7bn, up 41.2% y/y, at a 66.4% gross margin, with 2nm already 8% of wafer revenue, up from 3% in Q2; Rapidus is in talks with more than 60 potential customers but has yet to report a fully functional SRAM cell or commercial 2nm production.

**Why it matters:** A clean check on the Japan-foundry challenge thesis: at 2nm the contest is yield, SRAM functionality and ecosystem, not headline wafer price — a 30% discount has so far bought talks, not share.
