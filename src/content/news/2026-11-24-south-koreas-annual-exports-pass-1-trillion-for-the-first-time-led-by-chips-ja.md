Korea's trade ministry said cumulative 2026 exports topped $1 trillion on 19 Nov, making Korea the fourth country to reach that level after the US, China and Germany. Chip exports nearly tripled in January-October to $396.8bn, or 42.7% of all exports. Figures: Korea trade ministry, as reported 24 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** One industry now carries more than two-fifths of a major exporter's shipments — the demand side of the AI memory cycle, and its concentration risk, in a single number.

**韩国年度出口首次突破1万亿美元，半导体达3968亿美元、占1至10月出口的42.7%**

韩国产业通商部称，2026年累计出口于11月19日突破1万亿美元，韩国成为继美国、中国、德国之后第四个达到这一规模的国家。1至10月芯片出口增至3968亿美元，接近三倍，占出口总额的42.7%。
