Hitachi Energy expects sales at its Thai transformer plant to double year on year to about $200m (about ¥31.7bn) in 2026 as AI data-centre expansion lifts transformer demand. The plant is investing THB 455m (about $14m) to lift production capacity by 60%, with the expanded lines due to start in February 2027. (NNA Asia, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Transformer supply stays the long pole in the AI buildout — a Southeast Asian plant doubling sales shows the capacity race moving beyond the US and Japan.

**日立能源泰国变压器工厂2026年销售额预计翻倍至约2亿美元，数据中心需求是主因**

日立能源预计其泰国变压器工厂2026年销售额同比翻倍至约2亿美元（约317亿日元），AI数据中心扩张推高了变压器需求。该厂正投资4.55亿泰铢（约1400万美元）将产能提升60%，扩产产线计划于2027年2月投产。（NNA Asia，2026年11月24日）
