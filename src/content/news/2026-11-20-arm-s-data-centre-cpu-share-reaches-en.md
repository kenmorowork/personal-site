Cumulative shipments of CPU cores using Arm's Neoverse data-centre designs reached 1.5 billion, of which 500 million shipped in the most recent nine months, as AI workloads shift from chat-style queries toward agent-style tasks that raise the CPU's role alongside GPUs.

**Why it matters:** Half of major-cloud data-centre CPUs now on Arm designs, with 500m cores shipped in nine months, quantifies the CPU-side shift as agent workloads grow — the read-across runs to Arm licensees and the x86 incumbents alike.
