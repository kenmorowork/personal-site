Malaysia's Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani said on 18 Nov that all data-centre operators in the country must comply with the new US export restrictions on advanced AI chips, which take effect on 19 Nov (Bernama via Crypto Briefing). The government will notify all 117 approved data-centre projects, of which 55 were operational as of mid-November; CGS International called the rules a speed bump rather than a derailment for ASEAN data-centre spending, while noting possible delays.

**Why it matters:** This turns the BIS remote-access rule from a US filing into on-the-ground compliance in ASEAN's fastest-growing data-centre market — licensing timelines for non-US operators are now a schedule risk for 2027 capacity.
