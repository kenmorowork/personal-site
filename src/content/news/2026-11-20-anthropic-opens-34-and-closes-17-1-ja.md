ANT opened at $111.50 (+34% vs the $83 IPO price), traded as high as $115.10 — a valuation of about $2tn intraday (Reuters) — and closed at $97.17, +17.07% on the day (Finnhub, 19 Nov). Nikkei Asia reports a concurrent $26bn private placement including TSMC, Nvidia, Google and Microsoft took total funds raised to $107bn.

**Why it matters:** A +17% close after a +34% open leaves the debut a success on price but not on momentum: the stock faded from its intraday high into a falling market, and the $107bn total raise underlines how much capital the AI complex is now absorbing.

**Anthropic纳斯达克首日开盘涨34%，收涨17.1%报97.17美元；260亿美元私募使募资总额达1070亿美元**



ANT以111.50美元开盘（较83美元的IPO定价高34%），盘中最高触及115.10美元，盘中估值约为2万亿美元（路透），收盘报97.17美元，全日上涨17.07%（Finnhub，11月19日）。日经亚洲报道，同期一项260亿美元的私募配售（参与方包括台积电、英伟达、谷歌和微软）使募资总额达到1,070亿美元。



开盘涨34%后收涨17%，上市首日在价格上成功但动能上有所回落：股价自盘中高点回落、且大盘下跌；而1,070亿美元的募资总额凸显了AI板块如今吸收资本的规模。
