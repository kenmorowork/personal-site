Hitachi Energy India Q2 FY27 (Jul-Sep 2026, reported 12 Nov): revenue from operations ₹2,996.5 crore, +63.6% y/y; profit after tax ₹319 crore, +20.5% y/y, as margins shrank. Orders received were ₹4,120.8 crore, +85.9% y/y, led by utility transmission orders for power transformers, STATCOM, cooling systems, AIS and GIS — with data centres the second-largest contributor to the order breakup. Exports grew +41% y/y in the quarter and +184% in H1. The order backlog stood at a record ₹33,335.4 crore at end-Sep 2026. Source: Hitachi Energy India results via CNBC TV18, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Cross-check for the transformer thesis: orders +85.9% with data centres already No.2 — the same demand mix Ken tracks at Hitachi Energy globally, GE Vernova and Siemens Energy.
