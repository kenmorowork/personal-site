Speaking after his Asia trip on Friday, President Trump set the bar at older chips — the H200 generation — and said he would accept none going at all, while noting China would then make its own. Treasury Secretary Bessent told CNBC the same day that existing curbs, including the new remote-access limits, are sufficient; Nvidia has said it is no longer shipping H200s to China despite the earlier licence approvals (Reuters, 2026-11-20).

**Why it matters:** The President's bar at older chips and the Treasury Secretary's line that no further curbs are needed together cap the near-term policy risk on chip sales to China, while Nvidia's halted H200 shipments show that licence approvals and actual deliveries remain separate gates.

**特朗普称对华只应出售旧世代芯片；贝森特称无需追加限制、政策不会改变**



特朗普总统周五在亚洲之行后表示，对华芯片门槛为以H200为代表的旧世代产品，并称即使一颗不卖也可以接受，但指出中国将因此自行制造。财政部长贝森特同日对CNBC表示，现有包括远程访问新规在内的限制已经足够；英伟达称尽管此前获得许可，目前已停止向中国出货H200（路透，2026-11-20）。



总统划定的旧世代芯片门槛与财长“无需追加限制”的表态，共同封顶了对华芯片销售的短期政策风险；而英伟达已停止H200出货则显示，许可批准与实际交付仍是两道独立的闸门。
