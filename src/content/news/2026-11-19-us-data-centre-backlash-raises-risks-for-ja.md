The Korea Times reported on 19 Nov that growing US local opposition to power-hungry AI data centres, plus Democratic midterm gains, raises delay risk for Korean suppliers; about 80% of US data-centre capacity under construction or in planning is now in states set to be governed by Democrats, up from 35% before the elections, Texas accounts for about 40% of planned additions and its incoming governor has pledged a moratorium on approvals, and MSCI counted at least 75 US data-centre projects delayed or cancelled in Q1 2026, the same number as in all of 2025.

**Why it matters:** US permitting politics is now a quantified schedule risk for the Korean supply chain — memory, transformers, cables and ESS batteries all sit behind the same approval bottleneck.

**美国数据中心反对声浪升温，韩国存储、变压器、电缆与储能供应商面临订单延迟风险**



《韩国时报》11月19日报道，美国地方对高耗电AI数据中心的反对及民主党在中期选举中的 gains 使韩国供应商面临延迟风险；美国在建及规划数据中心容量约80%位于将由民主党执政的州（选前为35%），得州占规划新增约40%且当选州长承诺暂停审批，MSCI统计2026年一季度至少75个美国数据中心项目被延迟或取消，与2025年全年持平。



美国的审批政治如今已成为韩国供应链可量化的进度风险——存储、变压器、电缆与储能电池都卡在同一个审批瓶颈之后。
