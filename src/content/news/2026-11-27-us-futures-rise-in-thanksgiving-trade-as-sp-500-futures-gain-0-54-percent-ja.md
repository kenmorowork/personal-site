With the NYSE, Nasdaq and bond markets closed for Thanksgiving on 26 November, equity futures traded higher in thin holiday conditions. S&P 500 futures closed up 38.75 points (+0.54%), and Dow Jones reporting put Nasdaq futures up 0.65% and Dow futures up 0.4% earlier in the session. The move extended Wednesday's reversal, in which the Dow erased a loss of more than 1,100 points to close up 0.33% at 50,182.95.

**Why it matters:** Friday's shortened session, closing at 13:00 ET, is the first full test of the reversal that began on Wednesday.
