TSMC approved investments of up to US$500 million each in Anthropic and OpenAI, plus another US$500 million to establish an investment company focused on semiconductor and related ecosystem opportunities — a US$1.5 billion programme aimed at AI demand including GPUs, ASICs, HBM and advanced packaging. The board also approved approximately US$40.93 billion in capital spending for advanced, mature and specialty manufacturing, packaging, facilities and research.

**Why it matters:** TSMC is buying proximity to its two largest AI customers while committing $40.93bn more to capacity — customer equity and capex now move together.
