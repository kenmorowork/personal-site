Nippon Steel's interim results (announced 5 Nov) show its three non-steel units expanding on AI and semiconductor demand, with combined underlying business profit for FY2026 now seen at ¥105bn — up ¥14.4bn from ¥90.6bn a year earlier and raised ¥5bn from the ¥100bn August forecast; all three units expect record profits. Nippon Steel Chemical &amp; Material's resin for rigid circuit-board materials for AI servers is forecast to rise 80% in sales volume in FY2026 versus FY2025, with a new plant under construction; semiconductor bonding wire volumes at Nippon Steel Micrometals are seen up 15%. Chemical &amp; Material's underlying business profit is seen at ¥33bn, up about 36% from ¥24.2bn. Figures: Nippon Steel interim results materials, reported 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** An 80% volume jump in AI-server substrate resin from a steelmaker's materials arm is another read on how far the AI bill of materials reaches beyond chips — into resins and bonding wire.

**日本制铁材料部门：AI服务器基板树脂销量预计增长80%，键合线增长15%**



日本制铁11月5日公布的中期业绩显示，其三家非钢子公司受益于AI与半导体需求扩张，2026年度实际业务利润合计预计1050亿日元，较上年度的906亿日元增加144亿日元，并较8月预测的1000亿日元上调50亿日元，三家公司均预计创历史最高利润。日铁化学与材料的AI服务器刚性电路基板材料用树脂2026年度销量预计同比增长80%，新工厂正在建设；日铁微金属的半导体键合线销量预计增长15%。化学与材料的实际业务利润预计330亿日元，较242亿日元增长约36%。数据来源：日本制铁中期业绩资料，2026年11月12日报道。



一家钢铁企业材料部门的AI服务器基板树脂销量增长80%，说明AI物料清单的辐射范围远超芯片本身——延伸到树脂和键合线。
