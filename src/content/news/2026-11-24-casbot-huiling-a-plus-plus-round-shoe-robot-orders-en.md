Beijing humanoid maker Zhongke Huiling (CASBOT) has closed an A++ round of several hundred million yuan to fund mass production and delivery. Its embodied shoe-making robots entered a leading footwear maker's Putian plant in August 2025, and its 2026 order book for the line exceeds RMB 400m with production planned in the thousands of units; it is also testing robots in underground mining with Shandong partners. (Robot Belt, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Humanoid commercialisation is arriving through unglamorous factory tasks first — shoe-making orders give the sector a rare hard order number.
