SANY Group announced on 16 Nov it will mark its 40th anniversary with its largest-ever exhibition at bauma CHINA 2026 (23–27 Nov, Shanghai, two venues), presenting more than 40 machines — predominantly electrified, including multiple new launches — across 9,797 sq m, configured as five integrated solutions for mining, road construction, port operations and other applications. For the first time SANY will dedicate a full hall to intelligent technologies, with AI agents and robotic machinery operating as an integrated system; the full lineup is unveiled when the stand opens on 24 Nov. Source: SANY Group via PR Newswire / Kyodo News, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** China's largest construction-machinery maker is framing its next decade around AI agents and robotic machines — the competitive backdrop for Komatsu and Caterpillar autonomy programmes into the 24 Nov reveal.

**三重工将在其史上最大规模bauma CHINA展会上首发AI智能体与机器人机械**



三重工于11月16日宣布，将以其史上最大规模参展bauma CHINA 2026（11月23–27日，上海，双场馆）庆祝成立40周年：在9,797平方米展位上展出40多台设备，以电动化为主、含多款新品，组成面向矿山、道路施工、港口作业等应用的五大一体化解决方案。三重工将首次设立整馆智能技术展区，展示作为一体化系统运行的AI智能体与机器人机械；完整阵容将于11月24日展位开放时揭晓。来源：三重工（经PR Newswire／共同社），2026年11月16日。



中国最大的工程机械制造商将下一个十年定位于AI智能体与机器人机械，这构成了小松与卡特彼勒自动驾驶计划在11月24日揭晓前的竞争背景。
