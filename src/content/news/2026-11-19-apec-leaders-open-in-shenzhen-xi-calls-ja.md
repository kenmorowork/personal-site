The APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting opened in Shenzhen on 19 Nov; in his opening address Xi Jinping called for cooperation on AI and supply chains and said China will provide $20 million to support cooperation in areas such as digital technology and clean energy, while President Trump said the US opposes any form of robust regulation of AI; a Xi-Trump bilateral and a Xi-Trump-Putin trilateral are scheduled later today, with advanced-chip export controls expected on the agenda.

**Why it matters:** With advanced-chip export controls expected on the agenda of both the bilateral and the trilateral later today, the Shenzhen meetings are a direct policy checkpoint for the chip and AIDC coverage.

**APEC峰会在深圳开幕：习近平呼吁AI与供应链合作，特朗普反对强力AI监管，中美及中美俄会谈在即**



APEC经济领导人会议11月19日在深圳开幕，习近平在开幕讲话中呼吁加强AI与供应链合作，并表示中国将提供2,000万美元支持数字技术与清洁能源等领域合作；特朗普表示美国反对任何形式的强力AI监管；习近平与特朗普的双边会谈及中美俄三边会谈定于今日晚些时候举行，先进芯片出口管制预计在议程之中。



先进芯片出口管制预计将进入今日晚些时候双边与三边会谈的议程，深圳会谈是芯片与AIDC覆盖的直接政策检验点。
