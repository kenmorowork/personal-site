A checkpoint-forensics report by Unemployment Labs (released 12 Nov, validated across the industry 13–14 Nov; summarised in Kimkj's 15 Nov briefing) identifies weight-initialisation fingerprints of export-controlled Nvidia Blackwell GPUs in six open-weight models from four Chinese labs released since April 2026: DeepSeek's V4-Flash and MiniMax's M3 match a 148-core B200/B300 configuration, while DeepSeek V4-Pro, Moonshot's Kimi K3/K3.1-Pro and Ant Group's Ling-3.1-flash match a 152-core GB200/GB300 configuration. The researchers note the method cannot distinguish chips physically in China from capacity rented in overseas cloud data centres.

**Why it matters:** If enforcement agencies adopt forensic evidence of this kind, the Megaspeed and entity-list fight tracked on this board gains a technical evidence channel — directly relevant to Nvidia's China exposure into its 17 Nov print.
