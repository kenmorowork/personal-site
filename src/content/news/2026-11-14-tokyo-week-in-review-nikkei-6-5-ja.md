The weekly wrap (Kabutan, 14 Nov) records the Nikkei's path over 9–13 Nov: +2.33% on Monday, +1.31% on Tuesday, -0.70% on Wednesday as Nvidia concerns hit, and +0.53% on Friday, with Friday turnover of ¥8.41tn on 2.54bn shares.

**Why it matters:** The weekly frame matters for Monday's open: Tokyo absorbed the Huang resignation, a hot US PPI and the chip unwind and still closed the week up 6.5%, so the starting point for next week is resilience, not capitulation.

**东京一周回顾：日经平均本周涨6.5%（+3707日元）至61142.35点——两周来首次大幅反弹并收复6万点关口**



该周报（株探，11月14日）记录了日经平均11月9—13日的路径：周一+2.33%、周二+1.31%、周三因英伟达担忧-0.70%、周五+0.53%，周五成交额为8.41万亿日元、成交量25.4亿股。



周度框架对周一开盘很重要：东京市场消化了黄仁勋辞任、美国PPI火热和芯片股反转，本周仍收涨6.5%，因此下周的起点是韧性而非 capitulation。
