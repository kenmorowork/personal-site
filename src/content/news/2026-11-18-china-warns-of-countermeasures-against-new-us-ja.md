China's commerce ministry warned it could take countermeasures against new US rules, effective 19 Nov, that require licences for advanced chips and servers used in overseas data centres above 12 million aggregate TPP serving designated entities. The rules target Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil as diversion risks, with a 90-day compliance window.

**Why it matters:** This is Beijing's answer to the 16 Nov BIS offshore-data-centre rule: it takes effect 19 Nov with a 90-day compliance window, and the countermeasure warning puts the Southeast Asian AIDC build-out — Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia — in the middle of the licensing fight.

**中国警告将对美国针对离岸数据中心的新AI芯片限制采取反制**



中国商务部警告，可能对美国新规采取反制；新规自11月19日起生效，对在超过1,200万总处理性能的海外数据中心为指定实体提供算力的先进芯片与服务器要求许可证。新规将新加坡、马来西亚、泰国、印度尼西亚和巴西列为转运风险地区，并设90天合规期。



这是北京对11月16日BIS离岸数据中心规则的回应：新规11月19日生效并设90天合规期，反制警告使新加坡、马来西亚、泰国、印尼等东南亚AIDC建设被置于许可之争的中心。
