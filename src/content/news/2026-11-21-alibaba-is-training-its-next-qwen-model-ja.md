CEO Eddie Wu said on the 19 November earnings call that the Zhenwu M890 (144 GB memory, 800 GB/s interconnect) is in large-scale sales and that most new AI chip supply after 2027 is expected to come from T-Head, with other Chinese chips used mainly for inference. Alibaba's July–September revenue was 271.5 billion yuan, up 10% year on year, with AI cloud revenue of about 61 billion yuan, up 53% (Asia Daily, 2026-11-21).

**Why it matters:** Training the next Qwen model on T-Head chips, with most new AI chip supply after 2027 expected to come from T-Head, is the clearest statement yet that a top Chinese cloud provider is building its roadmap around domestic silicon.
