The Nikkei 225 closed the morning of 18 Nov down 0.28% (163.89 points) at 58,696.54, after rising as much as 1.31% to 59,630.95 in early trade as Nvidia's results beat expectations. TOPIX fell 0.73% to 3,811.98, with 1,183 of Prime Market issues down versus 326 up. Reuters cited fading buying ahead of the Anthropic listing, softness in Korea's KOSPI, and lingering concern over Nvidia's leadership and data-centre regulation, quoting Nomura Securities strategist Wataru Akiyama. Advantest and SoftBank Group held gains but trimmed them; Toyota and Sony were soft. Source: Reuters (Japanese), 18 Nov midday.

**Why it matters:** Tokyo faded a +1.31% Nvidia-driven spike into a down midday close — the beat alone was not enough to hold the AI chain with the Anthropic debut ahead, which makes tonight's US reaction the real test of the rebound.

**东京11月18日午盘：日经指数在英伟达利好带动下早盘一度涨1.31%后回落，收跌0.28%至58,696.54点**



日经225指数11月18日上午收盘下跌0.28%（163.89点）至58,696.54点，早盘曾因英伟达业绩超预期一度上涨1.31%至59,630.95点。TOPIX下跌0.73%至3,811.98点，主板市场1,183只股票下跌、326只上涨。路透社援引野村证券策略师秋山涉称，Anthropic上市前买盘消退、韩国KOSPI走软，以及对英伟达领导层与数据中心监管的持续担忧压制了市场。爱德万测试与软银集团守住涨幅但有所收窄，丰田与索尼走软。来源：路透社（日文），2026年11月18日午盘。



东京将英伟达带动的+1.31%涨幅回吐至午盘收跌——仅靠业绩超预期不足以在Anthropic上市前托住AI链，今晚美股的反应才是反弹的真正检验。
