At Baidu World on 12 Nov, Baidu said the inference-focused Kunlun M100 has launched as scheduled and the next training/inference flagship M300 is planned for 2027. The new Tianchi supernode links 1,024 AI cards with all-optical interconnect, claiming 2.5x training and 3x inference performance vs the prior generation, scalable to 10,000-card clusters. Baidu has cumulatively sold 30+ Kunlun-chip clusters. Figures: TechNews Taiwan, 12 Nov 2026; performance figures are Baidu claims.

**Why it matters:** A 1,024-card all-optical supernode and a 2027 flagship chip are Baidu's bid to keep China's domestic AI stack credible under export controls — the scale figures are company claims, not independent benchmarks.
