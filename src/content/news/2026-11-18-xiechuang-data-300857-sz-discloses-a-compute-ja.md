Shenzhen-listed XieChuang Data said its wholly owned subsidiary signed a compute cloud services agreement in October with an unnamed customer, with a total amount estimated at no more than ¥37.49bn based on the service term, capacity and monthly prices, subject to actual settlement (company announcement, reported 18 Nov). As of 18 Nov the company had only placed a formal server purchase order with a qualified supplier, which confirmed a production schedule; no revenue or profit has been recognised, and the customer is not named.

**Why it matters:** A ¥37.49bn ceiling against no recognised revenue yet is the China version of the circular-financing question already flagged for TSMC's clients — the gap between announced compute deals and delivered, paid capacity is the number to track.

**协创数据（300857.SZ）披露算力云大单，测算上限374.9亿元——为测算值而非已确认收入**



深交所上市公司协创数据公告称，其全资子公司已于10月与一家未披露名称的客户签署算力云服务协议，按服务期限、算力数量及月度价格测算的总金额不超过374.9亿元，最终以实际结算为准（公司公告，11月18日报道）。截至11月18日，公司仅完成向合格供应商下达服务器正式采购订单、供应商确认排产这一步，尚未产生任何收入或利润，客户名称未予披露。



374.9亿元的测算上限与尚未确认的收入并存，这是台积电客户议题的中国版循环融资问题——已宣布算力交易与实际交付并付款的产能之间的差距，是需要跟踪的数字。
