Nvidia's 17 November announcement named six partners — Amkor, Foxconn, Kinsus, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, SPIL and TSMC — for substrate, packaging and testing for Vera Rubin NVL72, which per Nvidia's spec sheet pairs 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs with 20.7TB of HBM4. Kinsus production starts in the second half of 2027, with the dedicated capacity targeted for Q3 2028 (Pandromeda, reporting the 17 Nov announcement).

**Why it matters:** A more than 3.5x lift in dedicated ABF substrate capacity and close to $3bn of Amkor packaging deals show Nvidia locking in the substrate and packaging layers for Rubin well ahead of the 2H 2027 production start — capacity, not demand, remains the pacing item.
