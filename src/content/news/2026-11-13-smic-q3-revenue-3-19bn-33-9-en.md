China's largest foundry posted record Q3 revenue of $3.19bn (+33.9% y/y, +6.1% q/q) with total profit +106.3% to $650.9m; utilisation reached 97.3% and gross margin 29.9%. Q4 guidance is revenue +4–6% q/q with gross margin of 26–28%; co-CEO Zhao Haijun said AI-related supply chains stay tight through 2027 and, per CLS, full-year 2026 revenue should exceed $12bn, while Q3 capex fell to $1.09bn from $1.84bn in Q2.

**Why it matters:** Utilisation at 97.3% with AI supply tight into 2027 is the China-foundry counterpart to TSMC and Samsung capacity signals — and the capex step-down to $1.09bn in Q3 is the number to test against equipment orders.
