Allegro MicroSystems expanded its humanoid portfolio with the A81481 48V power-management IC (6×6mm, dual buck regulators, five LDO outputs, triple-watchdog safety diagnostics) plus inductive position and magnetic angle sensors for joints and grippers. Sarcomere Dynamics is integrating Allegro motion controllers, sensors and power ICs into its Scorpion robotic gripper. Source: Allegro via EDN Asia, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** For the sensor work: 48V architecture + joint-level sensing in one vendor stack is the per-unit content to price — cross-check against the TDK / MinebeaMitsumi / Nitto Denko map.

Source: EDN Asia / Allegro MicroSystems · 10 Nov — [original article](https://www.ednasia.com/allegro-develops-48v-power-and-precision-sensing-ics-for-humanoid-robots/)

**Allegro推出面向人形机器人的48V电源与精密传感IC**



Allegro MicroSystems扩展人形机器人产品线，推出A81481 48V电源管理IC（6×6mm，双路降压、五路LDO输出、三重看门狗安全诊断），以及用于关节与夹爪的电感式位置传感器和磁角度传感器。Sarcomere Dynamics正把Allegro的运动控制器、传感器与电源IC集成到其Scorpion机器人夹爪中。来源：Allegro（EDN Asia），2026年11月10日。



对传感器调研：48V架构+关节级传感由单一供应商打包，正是需要定价的单机价值量——可与TDK/美蓓亚三美/日东电工的图谱交叉核对。
