The Financial Times (15 Nov) reports Samsung is assembling a one-stop AI-infrastructure offer — Samsung C&T builds the data centres, Samsung SDS operates them, Samsung SDI supplies energy storage and Samsung Electronics supplies memory — and that SDS is in early-stage talks to sign Anthropic and OpenAI as data-centre tenants; no terms or capacity have been agreed. Analysts cited by the FT expect SDS's annual data-centre revenue to reach ₩6.6tn ($4.9bn) by 2031, roughly six times current levels, and Korean press reports put SDS's AI capacity plan at about 110MW rising to over 800MW by 2031. SDS broke ground on 2 Nov on a 60MW AI-only data centre at Gumi, expandable to 120MW, and Samsung group companies committed $1bn to KKR-backed Helix Digital Infrastructure in September.

**Why it matters:** A memory supplier moving into tenancy competes with its own customers' landlords: the test is a signed tenant, and none exists yet — talks and analyst estimates are all that is disclosed.
