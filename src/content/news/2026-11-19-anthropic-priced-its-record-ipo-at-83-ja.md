Anthropic said on 18 Nov it priced 980 million Class A shares at $83, above the $70-78 range, for a record $81.3bn offering valuing it at about $1.5 trillion; 956,351,354 shares are sold by the company and 23,648,646 by selling holders; trading starts on Nasdaq on Thursday 19 Nov under ANT and the offering closes 20 Nov.

**Why it matters:** Pricing above the marketed range sets the reference for the whole AI complex on debut day — the first trade on Nasdaq later today is the next checkpoint, with the offering closing on 20 Nov.

**Anthropic以每股83美元定价史上最大IPO，募资813亿美元，今日以ANT登陆纳斯达克**



Anthropic于11月18日将9.8亿股A类股定价为每股83美元，高于70-78美元区间，募资813亿美元创IPO纪录，估值约1.5万亿美元；股票将于11月19日在纳斯达克以代码ANT开始交易，发行于11月20日交割。



定价高于推介区间，为整个AI板块在上市首日设定了参照——今日晚些时候纳斯达克的首笔交易是下一个检验点，发行将于11月20日交割。
