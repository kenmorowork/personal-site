Bessent told CNBC on 13 Nov that alleged smuggling of Nvidia chips to China via Singapore-based Megaspeed is 'infuriating' because it advantages China in the AI race (Sankei, 14 Nov); he said he had argued for adding Megaspeed to the export-control list, a step Commerce Secretary Lutnick is reported to have delayed.

**Why it matters:** The Megaspeed affair escalates from a corporate-governance story to an export-control decision, directly relevant to how much AI compute can still reach China and to Nvidia's 17 Nov earnings questions.
