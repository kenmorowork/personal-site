Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation's net profit rose to ¥903.5bn in the six months to September 2026, with net business profit up ¥202.2bn to ¥1.17tn. Domestic funds income rose ¥293.6bn to ¥606.5bn as the average domestic loan yield reached 1.86%, while credit costs returned at ¥29.4bn and the CET1 ratio fell to 11.77%.

**Why it matters:** Profit rose on loan pricing rather than loan growth: the yield rose 0.35 points to 1.86% while consolidated loans fell ¥409.0bn from March.

三井住友银行截至2026年9月的上半年净利润增至9035亿日元，业务纯益增加2022亿日元至1.17万亿日元。国内资金收益增加2936亿日元至6065亿日元，国内贷款平均收益率升至1.86%，而信用成本回升至294亿日元，CET1比率降至11.77%。
