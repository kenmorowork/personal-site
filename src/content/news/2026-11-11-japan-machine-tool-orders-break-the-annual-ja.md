The JMTBA October flash, released 11 Nov, puts October orders at ¥204.4bn — 142.5% of a year earlier and the second-highest month on record after September's ¥223.2bn (91.6% m/m). Domestic orders were ¥52.3bn (+46.5% y/y) and foreign orders ¥152.1bn (+41.1%). The January–October total reached ¥1.8737tn, already ¥57.9bn above the full-year 2018 record of ¥1.8158tn, with two months still to run. The end-September order backlog stood at ¥1.0982tn. Figures: JMTBA flash report, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The JMTBA chairman attributes this cycle to AI and data centres, semiconductor equipment, energy and aerospace — not autos, which drove the 2018 record. This is the demand backdrop for Ken's machine-tool and FA coverage.

**日本工作机械订单十个月打破全年纪录——10月2044亿日元，同比+42.5%**



日本工作机械工业会（JMTBA）11月11日发布的10月速报：订单额2044亿日元，为上年同期的142.5%，是仅次于9月2232亿日元纪录的历史第二高单月（环比91.6%）。国内订单523亿日元（同比+46.5%），海外订单1521亿日元（同比+41.1%）。1–10月累计达1.8737万亿日元，已比2018年全年纪录1.8158万亿日元高出579亿日元，且还剩两个月。9月末订单积压为1.0982万亿日元。数据来源：JMTBA速报，2026年11月11日。



JMTBA会长称本轮周期的驱动来自AI与数据中心、半导体设备、能源与航空航天，而非推动2018年纪录的汽车行业。这正是工作机械与工厂自动化板块的需求背景。
