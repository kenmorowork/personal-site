The Nikkei 225 closed down 801.59 points (-1.34%) at 58,860.43 on Tuesday 17 Nov, below 59,000 for the first time since 9 Nov, with TOPIX down 61.35 points (-1.57%) to 3,840.02. Advantest was the largest drag, subtracting about 257 points as it closed at ¥26,010, down ¥1,195; Kioxia fell 5.08%, SoftBank Group 3.95% and Fujikura fell ¥109.5 (-4.06%) to ¥2,590.5. About 80% of Prime Market issues declined, with trading value of about ¥7.92tn, as investors waited for Nvidia's results after the US close. Source: BigGo Finance summary of the Tokyo close, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The close confirms the midday pattern held to the bell — the selloff is still concentrated in the AI/semi chain, with Fujikura (5803), a coverage name, now down for a second straight session into Nvidia's print tonight.
