Infineon Technologies will supply its 900V gallium nitride (GaN) monolithic bidirectional switches (BDS) to Enphase Energy for the power modules of Enphase's IQ Solid-State Transformer (SST), extending GaN's operating range to 900V while keeping high-frequency switching (Electronic Specifier, 14 Nov 2026). The devices have been tested and are now being integrated into IQ SST product development, with production qualification expected to follow; the same technology is also aimed at three-phase commercial solar inverters (480V in the US, 400V elsewhere).

**Why it matters:** Solid-state transformers are emerging as a core building block of next-generation AI data-centre power distribution: a named device win at 900V ties the power-semiconductor chain (Infineon) directly to the SST architecture Enphase is qualifying for AI data centres.
