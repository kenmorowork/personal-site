At its 12 Nov earnings briefing, Pegatron said 2026 will be its first year of server shipments, with servers contributing about 10% of consolidated revenue and growing severalfold in 2027 alongside entry into hyperscale CSP customers. Shipments of Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform are set for the first half of 2027. (Pegatron October revenue was NT$188.08bn, +59.6% y/y, already on this dashboard.) Figures: DigiTimes, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Pegatron naming 2026 as year one for servers, with Rubin shipments dated to 1H27, gives the Taiwan EMS server ramp a timetable — and a 2027 severalfold growth claim to test against monthly revenue.
