Core CPI (ex-fresh food) rose 2.2% y/y in October versus +2.1% in September — a second straight month above the BOJ's 2% target — as scaled-back subsidies pushed electricity to +1.6% y/y and energy contributed +0.2pt; rice prices fell 29.7% y/y, the largest drop since 1971.

**Why it matters:** A second straight month above the BOJ's 2% target, with energy turning positive as subsidies fade, keeps the 17–18 Dec BOJ meeting in play; the small miss versus consensus limits the immediate policy read.

Source: Nikkei · 20 Nov 2026, 08:31 JST — [original article](https://www.nikkei.com/)

**日本10月核心CPI同比上涨2.2%，略低于市场预期的2.3%**



10月核心CPI（除生鲜食品）同比上涨2.2%，9月为2.1%，连续第二个月高于日本央行2%的目标；补贴削减使电费同比转为上涨1.6%，能源贡献+0.2个百分点；大米价格同比下跌29.7%，为1971年以来最大跌幅。



核心CPI连续第二个月高于日银2%目标，且补贴削减使能源转为正贡献，12月17–18日的日银会议仍在议程上；略低于预期使即时政策解读空间有限。
