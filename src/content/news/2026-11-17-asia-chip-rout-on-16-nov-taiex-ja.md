Asian chip shares tumbled on Monday 16 Nov ahead of Nvidia's earnings: Taiwan's Taiex plunged 6.03% (down 2,635 points) to 41,040.08 with TSMC down 5.74% to NT$2,215; Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 2.42% to 59,662.02, with Advantest down 11.1% and Tokyo Electron down 7.1%; South Korea's Kospi fell 3.6% to 5,100.50, with SK Hynix down 7.89% and Samsung Electronics down 4.47%. TSMC's October revenue rose 59.7% y/y to NT$586.9bn, underscoring that the move was sentiment, not reported demand. Sources: Associated Press and market data via CryptoCompass / KIMKJ, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The selloff was sentiment-led, not demand-led: TSMC's October revenue up 59.7% y/y sits against a 6.03% Taiex fall, so the read-through is multiple compression across the Asia chip chain into Nvidia's results.

**11月16日亚洲芯片股重挫：台股跌6.03%、日经跌2.42%，台积电跌5.74%、SK海力士跌7.89%**



11月16日（周一），在英伟达财报前，亚洲芯片股大跌：台湾加权指数暴跌6.03%（跌2,635点）至41,040.08点，台积电跌5.74%至2,215新台币；日经225跌2.42%至59,662.02点，爱德万测试跌11.1%、东京电子跌7.1%；韩国Kospi跌3.6%至5,100.50点，SK海力士跌7.89%、三星电子跌4.47%。台积电10月营收同比增长59.7%至5,869亿新台币，显示此次下跌源于情绪而非已报告的需求。来源：美联社及市场数据（经CryptoCompass／KIMKJ），2026年11月16日。



此次下跌由情绪而非需求驱动：台积电10月营收同比增长59.7%，与台股6.03%的跌幅形成对照，亚洲芯片链在英伟达财报前面临的是估值压缩。
