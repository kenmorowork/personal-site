High-priced AI and semiconductor stocks including SoftBank Group and Tokyo Electron lifted the Nikkei ahead of Anthropic's US debut, while selling prevailed across the broader market and TOPIX fell for a fourth straight session. Prime-market turnover was about ¥7,719.0bn (BigGo Finance, 19 Nov).

**Why it matters:** Breadth again lagged the headline index: a second straight Nikkei rise on narrow chip leadership alongside a fourth TOPIX decline keeps the narrow-leadership pattern in focus into the US session.

**东京收盘（11月19日）：日经指数涨0.71%至59,444.21点，半导体股领涨，TOPIX跌0.04%至3,822.47点**



软银集团、东京电子等高价AI与半导体股在Anthropic美国上市前推动日经指数上涨，但更广泛市场卖压占优，TOPIX连续第四个交易日下跌。主板市场成交额约为77,190亿日元（BigGo Finance，11月19日）。



市场广度再次落后于指数本身：日经靠狭窄的芯片股领涨连续第二日上涨，而TOPIX连续第四日下跌，狭窄领涨的格局在美股时段前仍是焦点。
