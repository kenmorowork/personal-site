Siemens FY Q4 (to 30 Sep): industrial profit €3.89bn (+22% y/y, record) vs €3.71bn company consensus; revenue €23.68bn (+11%); orders €34.77bn (+58%), an all-time high, led by Mobility and Smart Infrastructure (data-centre electrical gear). Mid-term comparable revenue growth guidance raised to 7-11% from 6-9%. Figures: Reuters (Siemens AG release), 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Record orders led by data-centre electrical gear, plus a raised mid-term growth guide, confirm Smart Infrastructure as a direct AI-power beneficiary — the European read-across for transformer and grid names.
