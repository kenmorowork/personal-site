Cisco reports fiscal Q1 after the close: consensus EPS $1.33 on $18.11bn revenue, inside its $18.0–$18.2bn guide. Applied Materials reports fiscal Q4 after the close: consensus EPS $4.06 on $10.30bn, against its guide of about $10.25bn ±$0.5bn. All figures are consensus expectations or company guidance, not results. Source: Tickeron earnings preview, Nov 2026.
