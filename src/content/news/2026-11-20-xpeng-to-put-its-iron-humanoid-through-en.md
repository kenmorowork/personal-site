XPeng will showcase its IRON general-purpose humanoid at its annual AI Day on 25 Nov in Guangzhou; the latest IRON, which rolled off a newly commissioned production line on 8 Sep 2026, stands about 170cm tall, has 76 degrees of freedom in the body plus 21 per hand, and runs on three in-house Turing AI chips with up to 2,250 TOPS (XPeng release via GlobeNewswire).

**Why it matters:** AI Day on 25 Nov is a dated checkpoint for China's humanoid push: IRON's 76 body degrees of freedom and in-house Turing chips put XPeng's physical-AI claims up for direct demonstration.
