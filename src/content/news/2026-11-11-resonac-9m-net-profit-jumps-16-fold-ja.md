Resonac (4004) reported Jan–Sep (IFRS) after the Tokyo close on 11 Nov: net profit ¥94.6bn, about 16 times a year earlier, and core operating profit ¥164.1bn, 2.36 times higher, as AI demand lifted semiconductor and electronic materials including copper-clad laminates. For the full year it raised core operating profit guidance from ¥196.0bn to ¥250.0bn (2.39 times the prior year) but cut net profit guidance from ¥112.5bn to ¥75.0bn after booking a ¥99.0bn graphite-electrode-related loss; the revised net figure sits below the ¥109.3bn IBES consensus (14 analysts). Figures: Resonac Q3 results via Reuters, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A clean split print: the AI materials engine is strong enough to lift the core guide by ¥54bn, while a legacy graphite charge takes the headline the other way.

Source: Resonac Q3 via Reuters · 11 Nov (after Tokyo close) — [original article](https://www.reuters.com/jp/economy/KXQRV7UR5NML7FBPEXBWF444NY-2026-11-11/)

**Resonac前三季度净利润增至16倍、达946亿日元——AI材料强劲，核心利润指引上调，净利润因石墨损失下调**



Resonac（4004）于11月11日东京收盘后公布1–9月业绩（IFRS）：净利润946亿日元，约为上年同期的16倍；核心营业利润1641亿日元，为上年同期的2.36倍，AI需求带动覆铜板等半导体与电子材料增长。公司将全年核心营业利润指引从1960亿日元上调至2500亿日元（为上年的2.39倍），但因计入石墨电极相关损失990亿日元，将净利润指引从1125亿日元下调至750亿日元；下调后的数字低于IBES汇总的1093亿日元市场预期（14位分析师）。数据来源：Resonac三季度业绩（路透），2026年11月11日。



一份分化鲜明的财报：AI材料引擎强劲到足以把核心指引上调540亿日元，而传统石墨业务的一次性损失把净利润 headline 拉向相反方向。
