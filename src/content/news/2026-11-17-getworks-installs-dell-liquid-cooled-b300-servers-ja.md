Getworks has completed installing Dell PowerEdge XE9780L servers (NVIDIA HGX B300, eight GPUs per server) in the IR7044 liquid-cooled rack solution at its Yuzawa GX container data centre in Niigata, forming a cluster of 128 B300 GPUs with direct liquid cooling. The rack uses Schneider Electric's Motivair MCDU-10 coolant distribution unit and a Dell PowerSwitch Z9864F + SONiC network. Getworks had built more than 380 container data centres as of September 2026. Source: Jiji, Getworks press release, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** 128 liquid-cooled B300s running in a container data centre in Niigata shows Japan's GPU capacity being built outside the hyperscale campuses too, with Dell servers and a Schneider CDU inside the rack.

**Getworks在汤泽集装箱数据中心导入戴尔液冷B300服务器，建成128块GPU集群**



Getworks已在新潟汤泽GX集装箱数据中心完成戴尔PowerEdge XE9780L服务器（NVIDIA HGX B300，每台8块GPU）的IR7044液冷机架方案导入，采用直接液冷，建成128块B300 GPU集群。机架采用施耐德电气Motivair MCDU-10冷却液分配单元与戴尔PowerSwitch Z9864F＋SONiC网络。截至2026年9月，Getworks已建成380台以上集装箱数据中心。来源：时事通信，Getworks新闻稿，2026年11月17日。



128块液冷B300在新潟的集装箱数据中心运行，显示日本的GPU算力也在超大规模园区之外建设，机架内是戴尔服务器与施耐德的CDU。
