TSMC is facing unprecedented demand for its advanced chips as AI infrastructure investment accelerates, with advanced packaging and emerging physical AI applications expected to create additional growth opportunities, Digitimes reported on 12 Nov. The same day's Digitimes focus list also flags TSMC breaking its silence on Musk's Terafab — talks underway, but fab control non-negotiable — and CoPoS packaging advancing at the Taoyuan Longtan site. Source: Digitimes, 12 Nov 2026.

**台积电视AI芯片需求空前——先进封装与物理AI被点名为下一增长点**



Digitimes 11月12日报道，随着AI基础设施投资加速，台积电正面临对其先进芯片的空前需求，先进封装与新兴的物理AI应用有望带来额外增长机会。Digitimes同日焦点列表还提到，台积电就马斯克的Terafab打破沉默——谈判进行中，但工厂控制权不可谈判——以及CoPoS封装在桃园龙潭园区推进。来源：Digitimes，2026年11月12日。



一条公司串起Ken的两条线索：封装产能作为绑定约束，以及Terafab是否会成为日本设备厂商真正的第二需求极。
