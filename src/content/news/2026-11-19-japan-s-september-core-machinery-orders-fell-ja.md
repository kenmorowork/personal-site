Cabinet Office data released 19 Nov at 08:50 JST: core private-sector orders ex ships and electric power fell 0.3% m/m in September to ¥1,066.2bn (+13.4% y/y), the first fall in two months; manufacturing rose 3.9% m/m to ¥547.6bn and non-manufacturing ex ships/power rose 1.1%; the Cabinet Office kept its assessment at "showing signs of picking up" and the Oct-Dec forecast is +4.3% q/q to ¥3,287.0bn.

**Why it matters:** Machinery orders are the leading capex gauge for Japan's machine-tool and FA names: a smaller-than-forecast September dip alongside a firmer Oct-Dec outlook keeps the domestic capex recovery story intact into year-end.

**日本9月核心机械订单环比下降0.3%至1.0662万亿日元，10-12月展望环比增长4.3%**



内阁府11月19日8:50公布：9月除船舶电力外民间核心订单环比-0.3%至10,662亿日元，同比+13.4%，为两个月来首次下降；制造业环比+3.9%，10-12月展望环比+4.3%至32,870亿日元，基调判断维持"出现回暖动向"。



机械订单是日本工作机械与FA厂商的先行资本开支指标：9月降幅小于预期、10-12月展望走强，意味着国内资本开支复苏逻辑在年末前保持完好。
