Ebara's July–September operating profit rose 69.0% y/y, helped by semiconductor-related demand (CMP and dry vacuum pumps), while the full-year guide was left unchanged and a buyback of up to ¥5bn was announced. Hirata (6258) also raised its full-year ordinary profit guide by 23.6% the same day.

**Why it matters:** Semiconductor process demand (CMP and dry vacuum pumps) drove a +69.0% single-quarter profit rise while the full-year guide was held — that conservatism, plus Hirata's same-day raise, marks the breadth of equipment demand.
