Hon Hai (Foxconn) Q3 2026: net profit NT$77.52bn (+34% y/y, +29% q/q), beating the LSEG consensus of NT$69.6bn; revenue NT$3.02tn (+46% y/y), EPS NT$5.51. Cloud &amp; networking products including AI servers reached 54% of revenue, up from 51% in Q2; gross margin 6.02%. Figures: Focus Taiwan (CNA), 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** AI servers passing half of revenue at the largest ODM reframes Hon Hai from assembler to AI infrastructure — and the beat versus consensus sets the tone for Taiwan EMS peers reporting this week.
