Digital Realty Trust will invest an additional $10bn in Japanese data centres over the next decade, in partnership with Mitsubishi Corporation, its CEO told Nikkei Asia (15 Nov 2026). Japan capacity is currently 193MW and is targeted to reach roughly 700MW, against about 3,000MW operating globally and a further 9,000MW planned.

**Why it matters:** A US hyperscale landlord adding $10bn to Japan is direct demand evidence for the domestic chain Ken covers — transformers, switchgear, cooling and construction — and it lands ahead of Nvidia's 17 Nov earnings.

**Digital Realty拟再向日本数据中心投资100亿美元——容量从193MW迈向约700MW**



Digital Realty Trust首席执行官向日经亚洲表示，公司将在未来十年与三菱商事合作、再向日本数据中心投资100亿美元（2026年11月15日）。其日本容量目前为193MW，目标提升至约700MW；全球在运营容量约3,000MW，另有9,000MW在规划中。



美国超大规模数据中心运营商再向日本投入100亿美元，直接印证了Ken所覆盖的国内产业链需求——变压器、开关设备、冷却与建筑——且时点正值英伟达11月17日财报之前。
