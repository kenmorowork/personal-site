Ministry of Finance data released 18 Nov showed October exports rose 20.5% year on year to ¥11.768tn, a record monthly value and the 14th straight monthly rise, versus a median forecast of +20.0%; exports to the US rose 23.9% and to China 13.3%. Imports rose 21.6% to a record ¥12.245tn on energy costs, leaving a trade deficit of ¥476.5bn, well below the market estimate of about ¥956bn and below the morning Watch-next QUICK consensus of a ¥927.1bn deficit. Source: Reuters / Ministry of Finance, 18 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A record export month with the deficit at less than half the consensus estimate is hard trade data behind the chip-equipment names — and it lands the same morning as Nvidia's beat, reinforcing the semiconductor demand read for Japan's exporters.

**日本10月出口创纪录达11.77万亿日元，同比增长20.5%，半导体相关需求强劲；贸易逆差收窄至4,765亿日元**



财务省11月18日公布的数据显示，10月出口同比增长20.5%至11.768万亿日元，创单月纪录且连续第14个月增长，高于市场预估中值+20.0%；对美出口增长23.9%，对华增长13.3%。进口因能源成本增长21.6%至创纪录的12.245万亿日元，贸易逆差为4,765亿日元，远低于市场预估的约9,560亿日元，也低于早间Watch next中QUICK一致预期的9,271亿日元逆差。来源：路透社／财务省，2026年11月18日。



出口创纪录、逆差不到一致预期一半，这是芯片设备股背后的硬贸易数据——且与英伟达超预期同日出现，强化了对日本出口商的半导体需求解读。
