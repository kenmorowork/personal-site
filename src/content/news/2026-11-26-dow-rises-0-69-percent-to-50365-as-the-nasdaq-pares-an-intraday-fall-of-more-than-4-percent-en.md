The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 346.96 points, or 0.69%, to close at 50,365.85 on Wednesday 25 November. The S&P 500 lost 6.03 points, or 0.08%, to 7,195.06, and the Nasdaq Composite lost 121.93 points, or 0.52%, to 23,611.61. The Nasdaq had been more than 4% lower earlier in the session as AI-related stocks sold off and new US tariff proposals weighed on sentiment, before bargain hunters stepped in ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday. US markets are closed on Thursday and close early on Friday.

**Why it matters:** A 4% intraday Nasdaq fall that closes at minus 0.52% reads as a positioning event rather than a confirmed trend break.
