Anthropic fell further in Friday premarket trading after its +17% debut close; Reuters notes the US SEC is investigating Nvidia, per the Wall Street Journal, even as Nvidia raised its fiscal 2028 revenue growth outlook to 75% y/y. US October retail sales rose 0.2% m/m (after +0.8% in September) and Brent crude held above $90 a barrel on Friday morning.

**Why it matters:** An 8.5% weekly fall in Asia-Pacific ex-Japan — the worst since March 2020 — despite Nvidia's beat frames this sell-off as positioning rather than fundamentals; the reported SEC investigation into Nvidia and Anthropic's premarket fade are the threads to track into the US close.
