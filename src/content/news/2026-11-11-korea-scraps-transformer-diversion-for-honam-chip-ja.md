South Korea's climate minister Kim Sung-hwan said on 11 Nov the government will drop its plan to divert transformers earmarked for substations in Daegu, Geoje, Yeosu and Busan to the Honam semiconductor cluster in Gwangju, and will order new transformers instead. The plan had aimed to bring the cluster's first-phase transmission line forward by a year; the five substations whose transformers were considered were already delayed. The government still plans to start power-line construction in January 2027 and supply 4GW to the cluster by 2029. Ultra-high-voltage transformers now take about three years from order to delivery, up to four to five years, amid grid replacement and AI data-centre demand. Figures: Maeil Business / Yonhap, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Another data point that transformer lead time — not chips — is the binding constraint on new semiconductor capacity, and that governments are now ordering new units rather than raiding other substations.

**韩国取消湖南半导体产业园变压器调拨方案——改为新订购，2029年前供电4GW**



韩国气候能源环境部长官金星焕11月11日表示，政府将取消把大邱、巨济、丽水和釜山变电站变压器调拨至光州湖南半导体产业园的方案，改为新订购变压器。该方案原本旨在把产业园一期输电线路提前一年建成；被考虑调拨的五座变电站本身已延期。政府仍计划2027年1月开工建设供电线路，2029年前向产业园供电4GW。在电网更新与AI数据中心需求推动下，超高压变压器从订购到交付目前平均约需三年，最长四至五年。数据来源：每日经济新闻/韩联社，2026年11月11日。



又一个证据表明，制约新建半导体产能的瓶颈是变压器交货期，而非芯片本身；政府现在选择新订购，而不是挪用其他变电站的设备。
