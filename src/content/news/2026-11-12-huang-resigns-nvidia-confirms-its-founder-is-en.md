Nvidia announced on 12 Nov that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, with no reason given in the release. CFO Colette Kress (in post since Sep 2013) is interim CEO and remains CFO; Harvey C. Jones becomes the board's first chair, and the search for a permanent CEO will consider internal and external candidates. Operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap continue, and the fiscal Q3 report stays on Tue 17 Nov at about 13:20 PT, call at 14:00 PT. The stock opened at $150.99 (-13.1% vs the $173.73 close on 11 Nov; intraday low $148.80) and closed at $166.19, -4.34% on the day. Sources: Nvidia announcement via FinanceFeeds, 12 Nov 2026; Finnhub market data, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** This converts last night's 'reported ouster debate' into a confirmed leadership change five days before earnings — the question for 17 Nov is now guidance and succession, not demand.
