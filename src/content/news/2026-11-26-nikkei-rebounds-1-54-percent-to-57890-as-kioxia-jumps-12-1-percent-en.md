The Nikkei 225 opened up 417.92 points at 57,429.54 and closed the morning at 57,886.95, up 875.33 points. TOPIX was 3,777.85 at 10:57, up 0.84%, and the dollar was at 159.09 yen at 11:02. Kioxia ended the morning at ¥7,732, up 12.14%, after touching its limit-up price of ¥7,895. Sumitomo Metal Mining rose 4.9% and Murata rose nearly 7%. US markets are closed on November 26 for Thanksgiving.

**Why it matters:** Semiconductors rising 6.28% against an index up 1.84% at the same time shows the rebound was a buyback of the sold-off names, not yet a broad recovery.
