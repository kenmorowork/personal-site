The Semiconductor Industry Association reported Q3 2026 sales of $517.7bn on 3 Nov (WSTS data; September is a three-month moving average of $172.6bn); the same article notes WSTS's August full-year 2026 calculation of $1.655tn would imply Q4 sales of about $435.3bn, roughly -16% q/q, a figure WSTS itself described as carrying forward June growth rates rather than a new forecast.

**Why it matters:** The industry's own tally says demand is still accelerating into Q4, while the stale full-year arithmetic implies a drop — a useful base-rate check before Nvidia's 17 Nov report and Japan's equipment orders.

**全球芯片销售额第三季度达5177亿美元、环比+24.1%——连续第三个季度环比增幅超20%，9月为1726亿美元、同比+146.8%**



半导体行业协会11月3日发布2026年第三季度销售额5177亿美元（WSTS数据；9月为1726亿美元的三个月移动平均）；该文指出，WSTS在8月测算的2026年全年1.655万亿美元将意味着第四季度销售额约4353亿美元、环比约-16%，而WSTS本身称该数字是延续6月增速的测算、并非新预测。



行业自身的统计显示需求进入第四季度仍在加速，而过时的全年测算却隐含下滑——这是英伟达11月17日财报和日本设备订单之前一个有用的 base-rate 参照。
