THINE Electronics said it has started offering a solution that transmits data contactlessly between boards rotating through 360 degrees, combining its IOHA:B signal-aggregation technology with a rotation-capable antenna over the 60GHz band. Aimed at humanoid and industrial robots, security equipment, medical and precision instruments, it replaces slip rings and brushes at rotating joints, removing wear-related degradation, failure and maintenance cost. Figures: EDN Japan, 12 Nov 2026; THINE announcement, 9 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A small, concrete component-level fix for a real humanoid bottleneck — wiring across rotating joints — from a listed Japanese chip name.

Source: EDN Japan · 12 Nov 2026 12:30 JST — [original article](https://edn.itmedia.co.jp/edn/articles/2611/12/news043.html)

**THINE推出面向机器人关节的60GHz非接触通信方案，可360度旋转传输**



ザインエレクトロニクス（THINE Electronics）开始提供一种在360度旋转的电路板之间非接触传输数据的方案，将其IOHA:B信号聚合技术与可旋转天线结合，使用60GHz频段。面向人形与工业机器人、安防设备、医疗和精密仪器，替代旋转关节处的滑环和电刷，消除磨损导致的劣化、故障和维护成本。数据来源：EDN Japan，2026年11月12日；THINE公告，2026年11月9日。



针对人形机器人真实瓶颈——旋转关节布线——的一个具体零部件级解决方案，出自一家日本上市芯片企业。
