Leveraged Korean retail buying is concentrated in the two mega-cap chip names: margin balances stood at ₩3.6358tn for Samsung Electronics and ₩3.3311tn for SK hynix as of 12 Nov 2026 — a combined ₩6.9669tn, or 27.2% of the ₩25.5976tn total across KOSPI and KOSDAQ and 36.5% of KOSPI-only credit loans of ₩19.0935tn (FnGuide, released 15 Nov, via BigGo). The two names' share averaged 22.35% from January to 12 Nov, versus 3–7% averages in 2020–2025, and regulators have suspended the 140% collateral-maintenance requirement from 9 Nov to 31 Jan.

**Why it matters:** With the KOSPI down about 42% from its June high, this concentration is the forced-selling overhang hanging over the two names that anchor Asia's chip tape — the same correction this board's week-in-review is tracking.

**三星与SK海力士两只股票背负7.0万亿韩元融资余额——占KOSPI信用贷款的36.5%**



韩国杠杆资金高度集中于两大芯片股：FnGuide 11月15日发布的数据显示，截至2026年11月12日，三星电子融资余额为3.6358万亿韩元、SK海力士为3.3311万亿韩元，合计6.9669万亿韩元，占KOSPI与KOSDAQ融资总额25.5976万亿韩元的27.2%，占KOSPI信用贷款19.0935万亿韩元的36.5%（经BigGo报道）。两只股票的占比在今年1月至11月12日平均为22.35%，而2020–2025年平均仅为3–7%；监管机构已于11月9日至1月31日暂停140%的担保维持率要求。



KOSPI已较6月高点下跌约42%，这种集中度是悬在两大亚洲芯片龙头之上的强平压力——与本看板周度回顾正在追踪的这轮调整直接相关。
