The 17 Nov expansion puts Spot inspection routes at Malta (NY), Burlington, Dresden and Singapore; in Singapore two Spot units have covered nearly 900km and logged more than 90,000 sub-fab inspections since April at a 99% mission-success rate. GlobalFoundries is the first non-automotive customer in Boston Dynamics' Atlas early-adopter programme, with Atlas testing due from early 2027 in sub-fab utility areas; neither company gives a timeline for cleanroom-floor work. Source: RobotAIGeek, 22 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Spot's 99% mission-success rate across more than 90,000 sub-fab inspections is the operating proof point for fab robotics; Atlas testing from early 2027 stays in sub-fab utility areas, so the cleanroom floor — the larger prize — remains unaddressed, with no timeline from either company.

**波士顿动力将Spot晶圆厂巡检扩大至格芯四座工厂，Atlas人形机器人2027年初开始测试——地点在子厂房而非洁净室**



11月17日的扩展使Spot巡检路线覆盖马耳他（纽约）、伯灵顿、德累斯顿与新加坡四座格芯工厂；新加坡两台Spot自4月以来已在子厂房累计行驶近900公里、完成逾9万次巡检，任务成功率99%。格芯是波士顿动力Atlas早期采用计划中首家非汽车行业客户，Atlas测试将于2027年初在子厂房公用设施区域开始；两家公司均未给出进入洁净室的时间表。来源：RobotAIGeek，2026年11月22日。



Spot逾9万次子厂房巡检、99%的任务成功率，是晶圆厂机器人应用的运营实证；Atlas自2027年初的测试仍限于子厂房公用设施区域，规模更大的洁净室场景尚未触及，两家公司也均未给出时间表。
