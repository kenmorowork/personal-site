Institutional enthusiasm for data-centre stocks waned in Q3 2026 (quarter ended 30 Sep): at least 138 funds completely sold their data-centre positions versus 119 that initiated new ones, and holders of eight large data-centre stocks fell to 3,392 from 3,411, according to a Reuters analysis of nearly 6,400 SEC 13F filings. The retreat was sharpest in utilities and power stocks, with 212 funds exiting versus 96 initiating. Semiconductor buying stayed positive but concentrated: 2,951 funds (51% of holders) increased positions versus 2,584 (44.7%) reducing, with 77 initiating and 69 exiting. Third Point dissolved its entire stakes in Amazon and ASML as of 30 Sep. Source: Reuters, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The 13F tape says the AIDC trade was already thinning before the November selloff — exits concentrated in power/utilities, the same leg Tokyo sold on 17 Nov.

**三季度机构对数据中心股降温：13F显示138家基金退出、119家新入**



2026年第三季度（截至9月30日）机构对数据中心股的热情减退：据路透社对近6,400份SEC 13F文件的分析，至少138家基金完全卖出数据中心持仓，而新入场的为119家；八只大型数据中心股的机构持有人从3,411家降至3,392家。公用事业与电力股撤退最明显，212家基金退出、仅96家新入。半导体买盘仍为正但趋于集中：2,951家基金（占持有人51%）增持，2,584家（44.7%）减持，新入77家、退出69家。Third Point已于9月30日清仓亚马逊与阿斯麦的全部持仓。来源：路透社，2026年11月16日。



13F数据表明，AIDC交易在11月抛售之前就已变薄，退出集中在电力/公用事业一环，与东京市场17日抛售的是同一条腿。
