The Dow fell 2.20% (−1,102.71 points) to 49,095.46, the S&P 500 fell 2.03% to 7,175.24 and the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.23% to 23,885.88 on Thu 19 Nov, the S&P 500's sixth straight loss (Reuters, preliminary close data). Nvidia closed down 3.30% at $145.98 (Finnhub, 19 Nov close), while WTI crude rose 0.7% to $83.84 as tanker disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz continued (Reuters).

**Why it matters:** A sixth straight fall, with Nvidia down on Anthropic's debut day, shows the AI trade failing to find support even from a record listing — Anthropic's second trading day is the next test.
