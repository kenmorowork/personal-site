Malaysia is drafting a law requiring data centre operators to disclose AI accelerator import volumes, power use, facility locations and large cloud customers, per Trade Minister Johari (TechNews, 16 Nov 2026). Malaysia has approved 117 data centre projects, 55 already operating; operators would pay for all reserved power capacity even if unused.

**Why it matters:** Transshipment scrutiny is moving from chips to the facilities that house them: reporting GPU imports, power use and large customers would give regulators a direct audit trail over AI capacity in Malaysia.
