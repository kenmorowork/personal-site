SUMCO (3436) reported Jan–Sep on 11 Nov: sales ¥332.5bn, +9.2% y/y, as strong AI and data-centre demand lifted wafer shipments — yet the operating result swung to a ¥4.40bn loss from a ¥5.87bn profit a year earlier, with an ordinary loss of ¥9.89bn and a net loss of ¥7.42bn (from ¥1.00bn). The revised full-year forecast is sales of ¥439.5bn (+7.3%), an operating loss of ¥3.3bn and a net loss of ¥3.4bn; the ¥20 annual dividend forecast is unchanged. The shares had closed up 9.31% at ¥2,089 on 11 Nov, before the release. Figures: SUMCO Q3 filing summary, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Volume is not the constraint — price and cost are. SUMCO is the wafer-side counterpoint to TSMC's record October revenue on this same page.

**SUMCO前三季度仍亏损——尽管AI晶圆出货增长，销售额3325亿日元、营业亏损44亿日元**



SUMCO（3436）11月11日公布1–9月业绩：销售额3325亿日元，同比+9.2%，AI与数据中心需求强劲带动晶圆出货增加；但营业损益从上年同期盈利58.7亿日元转为亏损44.0亿日元，经常亏损98.9亿日元，净利润亏损74.2亿日元（上年同期亏损10.0亿日元）。修订后的全年预测：销售额4395亿日元（+7.3%）、营业亏损33亿日元、净亏损34亿日元；全年每股股息预测20日元不变。发布前，该股11月11日收盘上涨9.31%至2089日元。数据来源：SUMCO三季度决算短信摘要，2026年11月11日。



约束不在出货量，而在价格与成本。SUMCO与本页台积电10月营收纪录形成晶圆供需两侧的对照。
