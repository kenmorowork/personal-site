Adjusted EPS was $1.43, above the $1.28 FactSet consensus (Barron's, 24 Nov 2026). Fiscal 2027 revenue guidance was raised to growth of 27-35% from 13-17% previously, adjusted EPS guidance is $5.05-5.35, and the buyback authorisation was increased by $5bn; the shares still fell about 14% in early trading on 24 Nov (Investopedia).

**Why it matters:** AI server demand is not the debate for HPE — the fiscal 2027 segment margin path is, with Cloud & AI guided to about 14% from 18.4% this quarter.

**HPE第四财季营收145.5亿美元，同比增长50%，并将2027财年营收指引上调至594亿至631亿美元**

调整后每股收益为1.43美元，高于FactSet一致预期的1.28美元（Barron's，2026年11月24日）。2027财年营收增速指引由13%至17%上调至27%至35%，调整后每股收益指引为5.05至5.35美元，回购授权增加50亿美元；但股价在11月24日早盘仍下跌约14%（Investopedia）。
