ams-OSRAM reported Q3 2026 revenue of €829m (prior year: €853m) with adjusted EBITDA of €135m, down 19%, for a 16.3% margin; reported net profit was €319m while the adjusted result was a €35m net loss, shaped by the sale of the non-optical sensor business to Infineon (ad-hoc-news, 14 Nov 2026). Design wins were about €1.3bn in Q3, taking the year-to-date total to about €3.8bn; Opto Semiconductors revenue rose 9% to €399m. Q4 guidance is revenue of €750–850m and an adjusted EBITDA margin of 16.0% ±1.5pp.

**Why it matters:** A €3.8bn design-win backlog against a below-consensus Q4 guide is the European opto-semiconductor version of the week's theme: AI photonics and data-centre optics are the growth leg, while the legacy portfolio drags the reported numbers.
