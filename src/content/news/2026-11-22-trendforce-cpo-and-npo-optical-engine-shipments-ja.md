UDN (22 Nov) cites TrendForce: as AI clusters scale across racks, optical interconnect reaches nearly 20% of AI chip-to-chip (scale-up) bandwidth in 2028, lifting CPO/NPO optical-engine shipments about 18-fold. Certified silicon-photonics (PIC) wafer supply in 2027 is estimated at only 82,800 wafers against demand of about 152,000, and because a failed optical engine can scrap a co-packaged GPU, testing moves to wafer level and test time multiplies. Taiwan's FitTech (6706) has launched lens/FAU inspection, coupling and PIC test equipment, with spot inspection verified beyond 80 channels. Figures: TrendForce via UDN, 22 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** An 18-fold jump in CPO/NPO optical-engine shipments to 62.86m units in 2028, against certified PIC wafer supply of only 82,800 wafers versus demand of about 152,000 in 2027, puts wafer-level testing — and equipment suppliers such as FitTech — on the critical path for AI cluster scale-up.

Source: UDN · 22 Nov 2026, 02:18 (citing TrendForce) — [original article](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9893865)

**集邦：CPO光引擎出货量2028年将达6286万颗、较2027年增约18倍，检测成量产瓶颈，惠特（6706）切入硅光子检测**



联合新闻网11月22日引集邦科技（TrendForce）指出，2028年硅光子晶圆需求接近2027年已认证产能的两倍，AI芯片间光互连（Scale-up）带宽占比升至近两成，CPO与近封装光学（NPO）光引擎出货将由2027年的354万颗增至2028年的6286万颗；因光引擎与芯片高度整合、单一通道失效恐致整颗芯片报废，测试前移至晶圆级、时间倍增。检测设备厂惠特（6706）已推出Lens及FAU光形检测、耦光贴合与PIC芯片测试设备，光斑检测已验证超过80通道。数据来源：集邦科技，经联合新闻网2026年11月22日报道。



CPO/NPO光引擎出货量2028年增至6286万颗、约为2027年的18倍，而2027年已认证PIC晶圆产能仅82,800片、需求约152,000片，晶圆级检测——以及惠特等设备供应商——成为AI集群扩容的关键环节。
