TSMC Arizona Corporation has applied for a Texas school property tax limitation for a proposed $22.6bn campus in Brazos County, comprising about $8.6bn of buildings and $14bn of equipment on more than 3,200 acres, making 2nm or more advanced logic chips. The project would create at least 2,099 direct jobs at an average salary of $133,000, with construction from 2027 to 2031 if approved, and the filing states the tax limit will decide Texas versus expanding in Arizona.

**Why it matters:** Site economics, in the form of an estimated $354mn of school-tax savings over 20 years, are stated in the filing as the deciding factor for $22.6bn of leading-edge capacity.
