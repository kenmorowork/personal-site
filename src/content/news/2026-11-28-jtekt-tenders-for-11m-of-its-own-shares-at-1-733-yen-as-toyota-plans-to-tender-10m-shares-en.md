JTEKT (6473) will tender for up to 11,000,000 of its own shares at ¥1,733 per share, a 10% discount to the 24 November close of ¥1,925, from 26 November to 23 December, for an expected ¥19.1bn within a ¥20bn cap. Toyota Motor, which holds 24.26%, intends to tender 10,000,000 shares, and JTEKT plans to cancel 9,500,000 repurchased shares on 15 February 2027.

**Why it matters:** A 24.26% parent stake is being unwound through the subsidiary's own balance sheet, at a 10% discount, with the size set by a ¥20bn cash budget rather than by the stake itself.
