The two new orders add about 70 MW for delivery in Q2 2027, on top of the 9,000 GPUs committed in August for its first Hyderabad AI factory. AMI cites a global development pipeline of about 400 MW and plans a further 300 MW within 15 months, with more than $20bn of additional capex beyond the $6bn already committed to the first 100 MW (company release, 2026-11-21).

**Why it matters:** Committed Rubin capacity of about 29,000 GPUs and nearly 100 MW, a 400 MW development pipeline and more than $20bn of further capex planned make AMI a named buyer to watch in Nvidia's Rubin ramp across India and Malaysia.

**AM Intelligence追加订购20,000块英伟达Vera Rubin GPU，承诺的Rubin总规模增至约29,000块、近100兆瓦**



新增约70兆瓦容量将于2027年第二季度交付，部署于印度和马来西亚；加上8月为海得拉巴首座AI工厂承诺的9,000块GPU，合计约29,000块。公司称全球开发管线约400兆瓦，计划15个月内再投放300兆瓦，在首个100兆瓦已承诺的60亿美元之外再投入逾200亿美元资本开支（公司公告，2026-11-21）。



约29,000块GPU、近100兆瓦的已承诺Rubin规模，加上400兆瓦开发管线与逾200亿美元的后续资本开支计划，使AMI成为英伟达Rubin在印度与马来西亚放量中值得关注的具名买家。
