XPENG reported third-quarter revenue of RMB24.28bn, a record high, up 23.0% quarter on quarter, with record deliveries of 118,390 vehicles (up 15% quarter on quarter) and a cash position of RMB45.41bn. Overseas sales reached 13,000 units in October, with Q4 targeted above 40,000. Its first automated humanoid production line began operating in September with core-process automation above 80%; the first mass-produced IRON robots enter some XPENG stores for trial deployment in Q1 2027, with external commercial customers from Q2 2027. Source: XPENG Q3 release via GlobeNewswire, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** XPENG is funding its humanoid line from a record car quarter and has now dated the commercial sequence — store trials in Q1 2027, external customers in Q2 2027 — a concrete humanoid timeline from a listed Chinese maker.
