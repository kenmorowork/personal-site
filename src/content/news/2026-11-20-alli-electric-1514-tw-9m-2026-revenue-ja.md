At its 20 November briefing Alli said transformer revenue grew over 50% on semiconductor fab demand, with TSMC and UMC among its customers; AI/IDC power-equipment orders reached about NT$1.5bn this year versus NT$0.5–0.6bn a year previously, and sales at its US AI IDC UPS subsidiary roughly doubled (BigGo Finance Taiwan, 20 Nov 2026).

**Why it matters:** Transformers rising to 18.33% of revenue with gross margin at 19.71% shows fab power demand reaching Taiwan's mid-cap electrical names; AI/IDC orders rising to about NT$1.5bn from NT$0.5–0.6bn a year previously is the growth line to track.

**亚力电机（1514）：2026年前三季度营收79.94亿新台币、同比增长9.66%，EPS 2.44元、增长14%；变压器占比升至18.33%，毛利率升至19.71%**



亚力电机在11月20日的说明会上表示，受半导体晶圆厂需求拉动（客户包括台积电与联电），变压器营收增长逾50%；今年AI/IDC电力设备订单约15亿新台币，去年为5亿至6亿新台币；其美国AI IDC UPS子公司的销售额大致翻倍（BigGo Finance台湾，2026年11月20日）。



变压器占营收升至18.33%、毛利率达19.71%，显示晶圆厂电力需求正惠及台湾中型电机厂商；AI/IDC订单由去年的5亿至6亿新台币升至约15亿新台币，是需要跟踪的增长线。
