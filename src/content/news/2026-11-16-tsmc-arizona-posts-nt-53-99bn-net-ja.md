TSMC's Arizona subsidiary earned NT$53.99 billion in the first nine months of 2026, more than 11 times the NT$4.77 billion of a year earlier and the group's most profitable overseas manufacturing unit (H1 NT$36.07bn implies about NT$17.92bn in Q3). Japan's JASM recorded a NT$2.40 billion profit against an NT$8.38 billion loss a year earlier, while Germany's ESMC lost NT$1.08 billion; overseas operations received NT$167.59 billion in government grants across four countries through September. TSMC expects overseas fabs to cut 2026 gross margin by nearly 2 points, widening to 3–4 points in coming years; Arizona fab 2 is installing equipment for volume production in Q3 2027, slightly ahead of schedule.

**Why it matters:** Overseas fabs are no longer a pure margin drag: Arizona's 11x profit jump and JASM's swing to profit change the maths behind TSMC's 3–4 point long-run margin warning.

**台积电亚利桑那子公司前三季度净利润539.9亿新台币，同比增逾11倍；日本JASM实现扭亏**



台积电亚利桑那子公司2026年前三季度净利润达新台币539.9亿元，为上年同期47.7亿元的11倍以上，成为集团最赚钱的海外制造子公司（上半年360.7亿元推算三季度单季约179.2亿元）。日本JASM盈利24.0亿新台币，上年同期为亏损83.8亿元；德国ESMC亏损10.8亿元。海外业务截至9月在四国共获政府补助1675.9亿新台币。台积电预计海外工厂将使2026年毛利率下降近2个百分点，未来数年扩大至3–4个百分点；亚利桑那第二厂正安装设备，计划2027年三季度量产，略早于原计划。



海外工厂不再只是毛利率拖累：亚利桑那利润增逾11倍、JASM扭亏，正在改写台积电长期3–4个百分点毛利率拖累警告背后的算术。
