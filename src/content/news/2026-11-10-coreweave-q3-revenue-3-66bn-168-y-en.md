CoreWeave reported Q3 (Jul–Sep) after the US close on 10 Nov: revenue $3.661bn, +168% y/y, above the $3.54bn consensus; backlog ~$132.5bn, up from $104.2bn at end-Q2. GAAP net loss widened to $709m as interest expense hit $846m; adjusted EBITDA margin held at 62%. Active power passed 1.7GW (+200MW in the quarter). FY26 revenue guidance raised to $13.0–13.3bn; Q4 interest guided at $1.05–1.15bn. Figures: CoreWeave Q3 release, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Demand beat, but $846m of quarterly interest against a 62% EBITDA margin is the whole debate: growth versus the cost of the debt funding it.
