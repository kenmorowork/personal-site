Samsung Electronics acquired four US silicon photonics patents from UK-based Rockley Photonics in September 2026, South Korea's Shinailbo reported on 11 Nov, citing US Patent and Trademark Office records. Samsung had showcased a co-packaged optics (CPO) optical interconnect demonstration at SEDEX 2026. Figures: DigiTimes, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** CPO is the interconnect answer to AI rack power and bandwidth limits — a memory and logic giant buying photonics IP is a small but directional signal for Fujikura's optical chain.

**三星据报从Rockley购入四项硅光子学专利**



据韩国《新日报》11月11日援引美国专利商标局记录报道，三星电子已于2026年9月从英国Rockley Photonics购入四项美国硅光子学专利。三星曾在SEDEX 2026上展示共封装光学（CPO）光互连演示。数据来源：DigiTimes，2026年11月11日。



CPO是应对AI机架功耗与带宽限制的互连方案——存储与逻辑巨头购入光子学专利，对藤仓的光通信链条是一个虽小但有方向性的信号。
