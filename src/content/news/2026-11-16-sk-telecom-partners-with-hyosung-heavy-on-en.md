SK Telecom has formed a strategic partnership with Hyosung Heavy Industries to supply transformers and switchgear for 345kV substations powering its AI data centres, including joint testing of 800-volt DC systems. Through its subsidiary SK Hyper, SK Telecom has pledged AI data centres totalling 15GW of IT capacity by 2035, with the first 5GW available in phases from 2029, against a national plan for 18.4GW of AI data-centre capacity by 2035. Source: YT CIG, updated 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Korea's AIDC build-out now has a named grid-interface supplier: the 345kV substation — transformers and switchgear — is contracted before the campuses, making power equipment the pacing item again.
