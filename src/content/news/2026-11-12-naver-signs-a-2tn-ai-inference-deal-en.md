On its Q3 earnings call on 12 Nov, Naver CEO Choi Soo-yeon said data-centre capacity secured for its AI factory project with Nvidia and Brookfield already exceeds the target disclosed through 2028: service starts in H1 2027 at 55MW, scaling to 100MW by end-2027 and 200MW in 2028. CFO Kim Hee-cheul said a computing-services contract with a US AI inference company is worth about ₩2tn over five years for about 20MW, using a mix of B300 and Vera Rubin GPUs, with profit expected from 2027. Naver is also discussing a server purchase order with Nvidia — described as potentially Asia's largest — to secure Vera Rubin in advance, and is in talks with more than 10 other global companies. Figures: ChosunBiz, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A ₩2tn, 20MW, five-year inference contract is the first monetisation datapoint for Naver's AI factory — and a 200MW-by-2028 build keeps Korea in the same power-and-GPU race as the Honam chip cluster story.
