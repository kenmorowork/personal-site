Preliminary real GDP for July–September was flat quarter on quarter (−0.02%) and −0.1% annualised, below the QUICK median forecast of +0.2% annualised and the first contraction in four quarters (Q2 revised up to +1.7% annualised). Private consumption fell 0.3% q/q and net exports subtracted 0.6 point, but capital expenditure rose 1.6% q/q, ahead of the +1.5% forecast; the GDP deflator rose 2.4% y/y. The second preliminary estimate is due 8 December.

**Why it matters:** The miss was consumption and trade, not capex: AI and labour-saving investment holding up keeps the domestic equipment read-through intact into the 8 Dec revision.

**日本三季度GDP年化下降0.1%，低于预期的增长0.3%；设备投资增长1.6%**



日本内阁府公布的7–9月实际GDP初值环比基本持平（−0.02%），年化为−0.1%，低于QUICK调查中值预期的年化+0.2%，为四个季度以来首次收缩（二季度上修为年化+1.7%）。个人消费环比下降0.3%，外需拖累0.6个百分点；但设备投资环比增长1.6%，高于预期的+1.5%，AI与省力化投资形成支撑。第二次初值将于12月8日公布。



不及预期来自消费与外需而非设备投资：AI与省力化投资的韧性使国内设备链逻辑在12月8日二值公布前依然成立。
