Keisei Electric Railway raised its net profit forecast for the year to March 2027 by 63.4% to ¥64.2bn from ¥39.3bn after selling Oriental Land shares, while operating profit rises only ¥700mn to ¥31.7bn. The annual dividend becomes ¥30, including a ¥5 special dividend at year end funded from part of the sale gain.

**Why it matters:** Almost all of the 63.4% raise sits below the operating line: operating profit moves ¥700mn of the ¥24.9bn increase in net profit.

**京成电铁因出售东方乐园股份将净利润预期上调63.4%至642亿日元，并增设5日元特别股息**

京成电铁在出售东方乐园股份后，将截至2027年3月的财年净利润预期上调63.4%，至642亿日元，此前为393亿日元，而营业利润仅增加7亿日元至317亿日元。全年股息为30日元，其中包括以部分出售收益为来源的年末5日元特别股息。
