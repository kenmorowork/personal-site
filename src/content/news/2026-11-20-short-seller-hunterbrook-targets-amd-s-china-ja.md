The short report alleges entity-listed IEIT Systems built at least six AMD firmware updates after its March 2025 listing, and puts AMD's 2025 China + Hong Kong revenue at $7.75bn, 22% of the $34.6bn total; AMD denies any non-compliance. The claims are allegations from a disclosed short position, not a regulatory finding.

**Why it matters:** These are allegations from a disclosed short position, not a regulatory finding — but the size put on AMD's China exposure (22% of revenue) makes the entity-list compliance question one investors now have to price.
