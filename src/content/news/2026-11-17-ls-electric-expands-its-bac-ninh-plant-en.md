LS Electric said on 17 Nov it has completed a second production building at its Bac Ninh plant in Vietnam — four floors, 7,611 square metres — with dedicated lines for ring main units, battery control panels for energy storage systems and air circuit breakers. The company has already secured enough orders to run the new building at full capacity next year. Its Vietnam unit's revenue is expected to double from US$83m in 2022 to US$166m this year, with a target of US$350m by 2030. Source: Yonhap, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A power-equipment maker adding ASEAN capacity that is already sold out for next year is a demand signal that sits outside the US hyperscale order headlines — the same LS Electric that won the $93.8m US switchgear order in this dashboard.
