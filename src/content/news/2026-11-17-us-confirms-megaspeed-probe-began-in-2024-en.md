In a joint statement on 16 Nov, the US Commerce and Justice Departments said the investigation into Singapore cloud firm Megaspeed began in 2024 after proactive monitoring of possible semiconductor diversion. Investigators requested Nvidia's direct and indirect Megaspeed sales records in May 2025; in June 2025 Nvidia placed Megaspeed on its no-sell list and US agents, with Singapore law enforcement, carried out an on-site end-use check and interviewed then-CEO Alice Huang, after which the Justice Department took the lead criminal role. The statement makes no allegations against Nvidia; Megaspeed says Alice Huang left on 30 Aug 2025 and its business was lawful. Source: Business Times / Reuters, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The probe now has a dated timeline — opened in 2024, Nvidia records requested in May 2025, an on-site check in June 2025 — and the statement explicitly makes no allegations against Nvidia, narrowing the legal overhang on the AI supply chain to Megaspeed itself.
