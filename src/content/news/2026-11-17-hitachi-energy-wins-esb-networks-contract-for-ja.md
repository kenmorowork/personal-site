Hitachi Energy has signed a contract with ESB Networks to supply Thyristor Controlled Series Compensation (TCSC) systems for three projects on Ireland's 400kV transmission network, covering design, supply, installation, commissioning and long-term lifecycle support. The selected compensation levels can raise power transfer capability by up to two times on the Oldstreet and Knockkyle lines and three times on the Dunstown line. The projects sit in the priority transmission portfolio under the Price Review 6 investment programme for 2026-2030. Source: Transformer Magazine / Hitachi Energy, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Series compensation that lifts transfer capability up to two to three times on existing lines is grid capacity without new corridors — another dated order point for Hitachi Energy inside Ireland's 2026–2030 investment programme.

Source: Transformer Magazine / Hitachi Energy · 17 Nov 2026 — [original article](https://transformer-magazine.com/news/hitachi-energy-upgrades-irelands-400-kv-grid/)

**日立能源获ESB Networks合同，为爱尔兰三项400kV电网工程供应串联补偿系统**



日立能源已与ESB Networks签约，为爱尔兰400kV输电网的三项工程供应可控硅串联补偿（TCSC）系统，范围包括设计、供货、安装、调试与长期全生命周期支持。所选补偿水平可使Oldstreet与Knockkyle线路的输电能力最高提升至2倍，Dunstown线路最高提升至3倍。三项工程均属2026—2030年Price Review 6投资计划的优先输电组合。来源：Transformer Magazine／日立能源，2026年11月17日。



在既有线路上将输电能力提升至最高2—3倍的串联补偿，是无需新建走廊的电网扩容——这是日立能源在爱尔兰2026—2030投资计划中的又一个有日期的订单节点。
