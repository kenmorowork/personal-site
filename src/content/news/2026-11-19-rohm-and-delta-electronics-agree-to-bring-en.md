Delta Electronics and ROHM said on 18 Nov they signed a basic agreement to implement 800V high-voltage DC (800VDC) power solutions for AI data centres in Japan, as rack power rises from about 10kW toward over 100kW and eventually 1MW; they will jointly define system requirements, target an SST-based proof of concept in Japan in H2 2027 and feasibility confirmation during 2028, and ROHM targets over ¥100bn in server-related semiconductor sales by 2030.

**Why it matters:** 800VDC now has a Japan implementation path with named partners and dates — the SST proof of concept in H2 2027 is the checkpoint that separates agreement from deployment.
