An IEA report dated 19 Nov puts global data-centre electricity consumption at 485 TWh in 2025, rising to about 950 TWh by 2030 — roughly 3% of world power demand — while AI-specific data-centre consumption more than triples over the period. Korea's government has separately cited 11.9 GW of additional AI data-centre peak demand by 2040. Figures: IEA report, 19 Nov 2026, via eToday.

**Why it matters:** This is the demand premise underneath transformer and grid-equipment backlogs: the constraint on AI capacity is increasingly megawatts and grid connections, not chips.
