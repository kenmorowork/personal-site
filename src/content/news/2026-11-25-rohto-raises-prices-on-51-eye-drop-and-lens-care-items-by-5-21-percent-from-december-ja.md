Rohto Pharmaceutical said on 24 November that it will raise shipment prices on 51 items from 1 December, citing raw material, packaging, energy and logistics costs. The 45 eye-drop items rise 5-10% on shipment prices and the 6 contact-lens care and insertion items rise 15-21%.

**Why it matters:** Cost pass-through is reaching everyday consumer staples, with shipment-price rises of 5-21% across 51 items.

**乐敦制药（4527）12月1日起上调51款眼药水及隐形眼镜护理产品价格，涨幅5%至21%**

乐敦制药11月24日宣布，将从12月1日出货起上调51款产品价格，理由是原材料、包装、能源和物流成本上升。其中45款眼药水按出货价格上涨5%至10%，6款隐形眼镜护理及佩戴产品上涨15%至21%。
