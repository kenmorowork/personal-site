DIC said KJ005's tender offer for Taiyo Holdings is now expected to start around early February 2027, delayed from late November 2026, because competition and investment regulatory procedures in China and Japan remain incomplete. DIC also completed its buyback, acquiring a cumulative 2,033,500 shares for ¥9,999,664,700.

**Why it matters:** The buyback finished on schedule in the same week the acquisition it accompanies slipped by two months on regulators in two countries.

**DIC将太阳控股的要约收购推迟至2027年2月初，并完成100亿日元、共2033500股的回购**

DIC宣布，KJ005对太阳控股的要约收购因中国和日本的竞争与投资监管程序尚未完成，开始时间将从原定的2026年11月下旬推迟至2027年2月初。另一方面，DIC的股份回购已经完成，累计回购2033500股，总额9999664700日元，接近100亿日元的上限。
