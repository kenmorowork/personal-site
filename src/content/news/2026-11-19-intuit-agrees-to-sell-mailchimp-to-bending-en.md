The deal was signed on 19 November 2026 (Intuit 8-K and release) and is expected to close in early calendar 2027. Intuit bought Mailchimp in 2021 for roughly $12bn, per TechCrunch; the Friday share fall of 12.8% followed the cut to its annual revenue growth forecast (Reuters via Moneycontrol).

**Why it matters:** Selling Mailchimp for $3.68bn against a roughly $12bn purchase price in 2021 crystallises the write-down on Intuit's marketing-platform bet; the 12.8% Friday fall shows investors reacting to the guidance cut as much as to the disposal.
