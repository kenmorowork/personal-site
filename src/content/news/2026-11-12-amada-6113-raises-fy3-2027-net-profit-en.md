Amada raised FY3/2027 guidance on 12 Nov: net profit to ¥40.0bn (+¥6.0bn vs prior forecast, +31% y/y), above QUICK consensus of ¥39.7bn; revenue to ¥490bn (+12% y/y); operating profit to ¥58.0bn (+29% y/y). Nikkei attributes the raise to strong AI-related demand for sheet-metal machinery from data centres. Figures: Nikkei (TDnet disclosure), 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Sheet-metal machinery is the unglamorous end of data-centre construction — a guidance raise explicitly tied to AI data-centre demand extends the machine-tool cycle story beyond JMTBA orders.
