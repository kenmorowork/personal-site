At the opening panel of electronica 2026 in Munich on 10 Nov, industry leaders agreed that the primary bottleneck in AI is shifting from computing power to energy delivery, and the European supply chain sees power semiconductors as the next shortage, Digitimes reported on 12 Nov. Source: Digitimes, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** This is the framing behind Infineon's raised AI data-centre target and the onsemi capacity-diversion story — power devices are becoming the priced constraint.
