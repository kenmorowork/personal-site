Ecolab has launched an integrated AI data-centre cooling platform combining CoolIT's direct liquid cooling hardware with its 3D TRASAR real-time monitoring, integrated into the new CoolIT CHx 3000, a 3-megawatt coolant distribution unit. Ecolab's Global High-Tech business is growing more than 25% a year and is approaching $1.5bn in annualised 2026 sales including Ovivo and CoolIT; the company targets $4bn of annual High-Tech sales by 2030 at a 25% operating income margin. Source: Zacks, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A 3MW CDU wrapped with real-time monitoring, and a $4bn-by-2030 High-Tech sales target at a 25% operating margin, prices liquid cooling as a monitored platform business rather than one-off hardware.
