GE Vernova said on 12 Nov it has been selected by Power Grid Corporation of India (POWERGRID) to supply more than 70 extra-high-voltage transformers and shunt reactors, all 765kV class, for renewable-energy evacuation corridors under India's tariff-based competitive bidding framework, across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh. The order was booked through GE Vernova T&amp;D India; scope covers supply, erection, testing and commissioning. Units will be made at its Vadodara plant, which has built 765kV equipment since 2009 and supplied more than 700 units; deliveries run from 2026 through end-2027. Order value was not disclosed. Figures: GE Vernova announcement, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Another bulk, multi-year transformer award booked outside the data-centre channel — grid renewables, not AI, are filling the same 765kV order books Japanese and Indian makers compete for.
