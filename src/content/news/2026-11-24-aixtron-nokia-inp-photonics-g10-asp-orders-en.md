Nokia placed several repeat orders for AIXTRON's G10-AsP deposition systems for 150mm indium phosphide photonic integrated circuits, with deliveries scheduled throughout 2027. The release cites analyst expectations that the laser market for AI infrastructure grows from USD 2.3 billion in 2025 to USD 43.5 billion by 2031, a CAGR of about 63%. (AIXTRON via EQS News, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Repeat tool orders for InP photonics show optical interconnect capacity becoming a planned bottleneck in AI data-centre build-outs.
