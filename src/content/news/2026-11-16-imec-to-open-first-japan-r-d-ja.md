Belgian research institute imec announced on 16 Nov its first Japan R&D base, on the Hokkaido University campus in Sapporo, to support Rapidus (Chitose) on next-generation photonics and optical-wiring semiconductors, scaling to about 15 researchers and technicians over five years (Nikkei, 16 Nov 2026). It also renewed its collaboration with SCREEN Semiconductor Solutions.

**Why it matters:** imec planting its first Japan base next to Rapidus ties photonics and optical wiring into the Chitose project, with a five-year staffing build as the measurable commitment.

**imec将在札幌设立日本首个研发基地，以光子技术支持Rapidus**



比利时半导体研究机构imec于11月16日宣布，将在札幌的北海道大学校园内设立其在日本的首个研发基地，为千岁的Rapidus在下一代光子与光互连半导体方面提供技术支持，五年内扩充至约15名研究与技术人员（日经，2026年11月16日）。imec还更新了与SCREEN半导体解决方案公司的合作。



imec在Rapidus旁设立其日本首个基地，将光子与光互连纳入千岁项目，五年的人员扩充计划是可量化的承诺。
