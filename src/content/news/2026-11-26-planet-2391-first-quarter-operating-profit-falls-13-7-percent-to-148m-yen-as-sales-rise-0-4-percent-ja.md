Planet (2391), which runs EDI infrastructure for the daily-goods and cosmetics supply chain, reported first-quarter (August-October 2026) sales of ¥798m, up 0.4% year on year. Operating profit fell 13.7% to ¥148m, ordinary profit fell 10.0% to ¥161m and net profit fell 11.6% to ¥106m, with EPS of ¥16.32 against ¥18.25 a year earlier. The company guides full-year operating profit down 13.0% to ¥485m on upfront investment in EDI expansion and new services.

**Why it matters:** Sales are flat and the profit decline matches the company's own investment-year plan, so the fact set is plan versus execution.

**Planet（2391）第一季度营业利润下降13.7%至1.48亿日元，销售额增长0.4%至7.98亿日元**

运营日用品和化妆品供应链EDI基础设施的Planet（2391）公布第一季度（2026年8月至10月）销售额为7.98亿日元，同比增长0.4%。营业利润下降13.7%至1.48亿日元，经常利润下降10.0%至1.61亿日元，净利润下降11.6%至1.06亿日元，每股收益为16.32日元，上年同期为18.25日元。公司预计全年营业利润将下降13.0%至4.85亿日元，原因是EDI扩张和新服务的先行投资。
