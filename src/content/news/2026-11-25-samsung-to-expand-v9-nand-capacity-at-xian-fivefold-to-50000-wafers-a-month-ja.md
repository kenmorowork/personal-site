Samsung Electronics plans to expand ninth-generation V-NAND capacity at its Xi'an plant from about 10,000 300mm wafers a month to about 50,000, Seoul Economic Daily reported via Taiwan's Caixun. The X2 line's new capacity will be entirely V9, 20-30% of the existing V6 line will convert to V9, equipment is due around February 2027 and conversion is to finish by November 2027.

**Why it matters:** The expansion targets enterprise SSDs for AI data centres — the same NAND demand pool behind Kioxia's 37-fold profit increase in the first half.

**三星拟将西安工厂V9闪存产能扩大至每月约5万片，抢攻AI数据中心存储市场**

据首尔经济日报经台湾财讯快报报道，三星电子计划将西安工厂第九代V-NAND产能从每月约1万片300毫米晶圆扩大至约5万片。X2生产线新增产能将全部用于V9，现有V6生产线的20%至30%也将转产V9；设备预计2027年2月左右引进，生产线改造将于2027年11月前完成。
