Announced 20 Nov: FY ending May 2027 sales raised from ¥115.0bn to ¥124.0bn, operating profit from ¥2.35bn to ¥3.0bn, ordinary profit from ¥2.7bn to ¥3.4bn (+11.3% y/y) and net profit from ¥1.9bn to ¥2.3bn. The annual dividend was raised from ¥70 to ¥90 (¥45 interim + ¥45 year-end, vs ¥80 a year earlier). Drivers cited: auto-sector sales in Japan and overseas, industrial-equipment EMS orders, profitability measures and yen weakness (company disclosure via TDnet).

**Why it matters:** A second guidance raise to record profit, with the dividend lifted to ¥90, shows AI and auto-electronics demand reaching smaller listed suppliers, not just the large caps — with yen weakness an explicit driver.
