Citigroup and JPMorgan Chase have added language to the financing documents for a potential upsized $6.6bn loan sought by Bain Capital-owned Bridge Data Centres making it the borrower's responsibility to comply with US export controls and the BIS Entity List restrictions, Bloomberg Law reported on 18 Nov, citing people familiar with the talks. Bridge previously sold computing power to Megaspeed International, the Singapore neocloud under US investigation over alleged chip smuggling to China.

**Why it matters:** Export controls are now being priced into data-centre debt itself: lenders are shifting chip-curb liability to borrowers, which raises the cost and slows the financing of non-US neocloud capacity.
