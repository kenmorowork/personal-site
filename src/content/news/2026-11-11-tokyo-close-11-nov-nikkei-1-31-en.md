The Nikkei 225 closed up 789.01 points (+1.31%) at 61,248.59 and the Topix rose 0.92% to 3,929.39 on 11 Nov, a third consecutive gain as data and earnings eased AI-sector concerns. The largest gainers included Kokusai Electric (+10.17%), SUMCO (+9.31%) and Furukawa Electric (+8.01%); SoftBank Group fell about 3% after its first-half loss. In the US, Veterans Day (11 Nov) means stocks trade normal hours while the bond market is closed, and no major US data release was scheduled today. Figures: Reuters Tokyo close, 11 Nov 2026; market hours per NYSE / SIFMA calendars.

**Why it matters:** Tokyo is re-rating the AI chain on domestic evidence — orders and earnings — even while US yields near 5% cap the global multiple.
