Digitimes reported on 19 Nov that Lam Research announced on 18 Nov it had broken ground on its new Taiwan Technology Center in Zhunan, Miaoli County, an R&D expansion aimed at faster chip development in collaboration with TSMC; no investment figure was given in the report.

**Why it matters:** Another equipment maker co-locating R&D with TSMC in Taiwan — process-development proximity, not just installed-base service, is becoming the competitive position to hold.

**泛林集团在台湾苗栗竹南动工建设台湾技术中心，与台积电加速芯片开发**



Digitimes 11月19日报道，泛林集团（Lam Research）11月18日宣布其位于苗栗县竹南的新台湾技术中心破土动工，该研发扩张旨在与台积电合作加快芯片开发；报道未披露投资金额。



又一家设备厂商将研发与台积电在台湾共址——工艺开发的贴近程度，而不只是装机售后服务，正成为需要守住的竞争位置。
