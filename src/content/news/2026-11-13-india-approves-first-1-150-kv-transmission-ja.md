India's National Committee on Transmission approved the Raigarh–Kotra–Rourkela–Kolaghat 1,150 kV corridor worth ₹68,816 crore (about six years to complete), evacuating 12 GW of renewables at Bhadla-IV and Ramgarh-II plus storage. Power stocks rallied: Hitachi Energy India +12% to ₹32,043 on its Q2 beat, GE Vernova T&amp;D India +5%, Siemens Energy India +4%.

**Why it matters:** A 12 GW renewables evacuation corridor at 1,150 kV extends the ultra-high-voltage order cycle beyond data centres — the same equipment pool Hitachi Energy, GE Vernova and Siemens Energy are booking in India.

**印度批准首个1150千伏输电项目，价值6881.6亿卢比；日立能源印度股价大涨12%**



印度国家输电委员会批准了价值6881.6亿卢比的1150千伏输电走廊项目（工期约六年），将输送Bhadla-IV和Ramgarh-II共12GW可再生能源电力及配套储能。电力股上涨：日立能源印度因二季度业绩强劲上涨12%，GE Vernova输配电印度上涨5%，西门子能源印度上涨4%。



12GW可再生能源外送的1150千伏走廊把特高压订单周期延伸到数据中心之外——日立能源、GE Vernova和西门子能源在印度争夺的正是同一设备池。
