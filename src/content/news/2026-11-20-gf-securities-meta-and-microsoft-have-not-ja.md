GF Securities supply-chain checks reported on Friday evening (Zhitong / Phoenix, 20 Nov 22:39 JST) say Meta only shifted part of its x86 buying to Nvidia Grace/Vera and Microsoft's orders are unchanged. GF maintains a server CPU total addressable market of $211bn by 2030 (CAGR 47%) and forecasts 2027 shipments up 29% (general servers +15%, AI-inference CPUs +79%), with AMD Venice shipments of 400,000 in 2026 and 5.4 million in 2027.

**Why it matters:** A named broker's supply-chain check pushes back on order-cut fears for Meta and Microsoft and frames server CPUs — a $211bn market by 2030 on GF's numbers — as a second AI hardware leg alongside GPUs.

**广发证券：Meta与微软未削减服务器CPU订单，2030年服务器CPU市场规模料达2,110亿美元**



广发证券供应链核查（智通财经／凤凰网，11月20日22:39日本时间报道）称，Meta仅将部分x86采购转向英伟达Grace/Vera，微软订单未变。广发维持2030年服务器CPU可服务市场2,110亿美元（复合增长率47%）的预测，并预计2027年出货量增长29%（通用服务器+15%，AI推理CPU +79%），AMD Venice出货量2026年40万颗、2027年540万颗。



具名券商的供应链核查驳斥了Meta与微软砍单的担忧，并将服务器CPU——按广发测算2030年达2,110亿美元——定位为GPU之外AI硬件的第二条增长腿。
