Oracle said on Friday that the financing outlined at its investor day, including vendor financing, is in place for the five-year, roughly $300bn OpenAI computing agreement, after a final raise of about $20bn split roughly evenly between stock and debt. Oracle and OpenAI plan 4.5 GW of additional US data-centre capacity beyond the Abilene Stargate site, which Oracle says is nearly complete (Reuters, 2026-11-20).

**Why it matters:** With financing in place for the roughly $300bn OpenAI agreement and 4.5 GW of additional US capacity planned beyond Abilene, the funding question hanging over the Stargate build-out is, on Oracle's account, settled.
