Anthropic is due to set its final IPO price on the evening of 18 Nov US time and debut on Nasdaq as ANT on 19 Nov, offering 825 million shares at $70-78. Bloomberg reported orders of nearly seven times the shares available and the WSJ reported pricing could exceed $80, above a range valuing the company at up to about $1.4tn fully diluted; a concurrent $26bn private placement to seven strategic investors (Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC) is at the same price.

**Why it matters:** Tonight's pricing sets the reference for the whole AI complex after the chip selloff: a print above $80 would clear the marketed range, and the $26bn strategic placement at the same price ties Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, AMD, Broadcom, Micron and TSMC directly to the debut.

**Anthropic将于11月18日晚定价，市场传闻定价或高于70–78美元区间**



Anthropic定于美国时间11月18日晚确定IPO最终价格，11月19日以代码ANT在纳斯达克上市，发行8.25亿股，价格区间70–78美元。彭博报道认购约为可供股份的近七倍，《华尔街日报》报道定价可能超过80美元，高于对应全面摊薄估值最高约1.4万亿美元的区间；七家战略投资者（英伟达、谷歌、微软、AMD、博通、美光、台积电）另以同价参与260亿美元私募配售。



今晚的定价将为芯片股抛售后的整个AI板块设定参照：若定价高于80美元，将超出推介区间；而七家战略投资者以同价参与的260亿美元配售，使英伟达、谷歌、微软、AMD、博通、美光与台积电直接与此次上市绑定。
