The US Commerce Department is reportedly finalising a rule to stop Chinese AI firms from remotely accessing advanced Nvidia compute through overseas data centres, extending licensing/monitoring to seven countries including Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, possibly announced this month. The rule is still in inter-agency consultation and has not been formally announced; scope and enforcement are unconfirmed. Status: reported plan, NOT yet formally announced. Source: TechNews Taiwan / MoneyDJ, citing The Information, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Remote access is the loophole the Moonshot B300 rental story on this dashboard exposed — a rule closing it would hit Singapore and Malaysia data-centre demand, but it remains unannounced and unconfirmed.
