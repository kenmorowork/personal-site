Ordinary profit progress of 77.1% compares with a 32.9% progress rate a year earlier. Disclosure coverage named higher value-added products, digital transformation, expansion in the non-residential sector, reported up 118.1%, and stronger overseas initiatives as drivers. The shares closed at ¥383 on November 26, down 1.54%, before the after-close release.

**Why it matters:** Ordinary profit at 77.1% of plan after nine months, against 32.9% a year earlier, shows the recovery is in the cumulative numbers even though the standalone quarter is still loss-making at the ordinary line.

**Takasho（7590）前三季度经常利润增长69.9%至4.01亿日元，营业利润增至三倍以上达4.60亿日元，销售额159.24亿日元**

Takasho前三季度销售额为159.24亿日元，营业利润为4.60亿日元，同比增至三倍以上，经常利润增长69.9%至4.01亿日元，达到未调整的全年5.20亿日元计划的77.1%。第三季度单季经常亏损由1.13亿日元收窄至6200万日元，并计入4400万日元汇兑损失为营业外费用。
