Eblen's H1 FY3/2027 (Apr–Sep, announced 12 Nov) sales rose 37.1% y/y to ¥2.655bn and operating profit jumped 106.2% to ¥490m, with net profit up 85.8% to ¥316m (Fisco, 13 Nov). Its core measurement-and-control segment grew 58.9% to ¥1.814bn (68.3% of sales) on memory-related, especially HBM, capex in semiconductor equipment. The full-year guide was raised from ¥4.4bn to ¥5.6bn in sales (+40.2% y/y) and from ¥620m to ¥1.06bn in operating profit (+99.8%), with the dividend lifted from ¥48 to ¥58.

**Why it matters:** A small-cap equipment supplier doubling profit on HBM capex is the same demand chain Micronics Japan flagged yesterday — the HBM build-out is broadening from probe cards into measurement and control gear.

**Eblen（6599）上半年营业利润翻倍，同比+106%——受HBM带动的半导体设备需求推动，全年指引大幅上调**



Eblen 2027年3月期上半年（4–9月，11月12日发布）销售额同比+37.1%至26.55亿日元，营业利润同比+106.2%至4.90亿日元，净利润+85.8%至3.16亿日元（Fisco，11月13日）。核心的测量控制部门增长58.9%至18.14亿日元（占销售额68.3%），受益于半导体设备中存储、尤其是HBM相关的资本开支。全年指引上调：销售额从44亿日元上调至56亿日元（同比+40.2%），营业利润从6.2亿日元上调至10.6亿日元（+99.8%），股息从48日元提高至58日元。



一家小型设备供应商凭HBM资本开支实现利润翻倍，与昨天日本マイクロニクス所指为同一条需求链——HBM扩产正从探针卡扩散到测量控制设备。
