China's official manufacturing PMI for November is expected at 49.4 in a Reuters poll of 13 economists, slightly above October's 49.3 and a second straight month below the 50 mark that separates expansion from contraction. The private RatingDog manufacturing PMI, compiled by S&P Global, is forecast to dip to 51.5 from 52.0 in October; the official data is released on Monday and the RatingDog survey on Tuesday.

**Why it matters:** The same factory sector prints below 50 and above 51 in the same week, depending on which firms are surveyed — the level matters less than which sample a reader is holding.

Source: Reuters poll via WTBX · 27 Nov 2026 — [original article](https://wtbx.com/2026/11/27/chinas-factory-slump-likely-extends-to-november-despite-solid-exports/)

**中国11月官方制造业PMI预计为49.4，连续第二个月收缩，RatingDog预计为51.5**

路透对13位经济学家的调查显示，中国11月官方制造业PMI预计为49.4，略高于10月的49.3，连续第二个月低于50的荣枯线。由标普全球编制的民间RatingDog制造业PMI预计从10月的52.0降至51.5；官方数据将于周一公布，RatingDog调查将于周二公布。
