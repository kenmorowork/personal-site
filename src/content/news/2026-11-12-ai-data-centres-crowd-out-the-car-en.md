A 12 Nov Nocut News investigation reports the AI build-out squeezing autos: Gartner puts the 2026 global semiconductor market at $1,555.2bn, up 92% from $809bn in 2025, on AI data-centre investment and memory prices. Onsemi said on its Q3 call it deferred some auto customers' restocking orders and prioritised limited capacity for AI data centres, planning to resume the delayed auto volumes from Q1 next year; Nexperia's interim CEO warned AI data-centre and robotics demand exceeds capacity. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron focusing on HBM is also lifting commodity DRAM prices. Trafigura estimates copper demand from AI infrastructure could rise from about 1Mt in 2026 to 2.5–3.0Mt in 2028 if all announced plans are built. Micron signed long-term strategic supply agreements with GM and Ford in July. Figures: Nocut News, 12 Nov 2026, citing Gartner and Trafigura.

**Why it matters:** The first named case of a chipmaker explicitly deferring auto orders for AI data centres turns the 'crowding out' argument from theory into a supplier's own words — with copper as the second squeeze.
