Retail investors bought about $1.6bn of Nvidia shares over the three sessions to Wednesday 12 Nov (Mon $590m, Tue $730m — the largest single day since March — Wed $300m), the largest three-day total in at least 12 months, as the stock fell on Jensen Huang's resignation (The Coin Republic, 14 Nov 2026, citing The Kobeissi Letter / Vanda Research). Leveraged long ETFs NVDL and NVDU took in a further $465m combined, and Thursday's opening 30 minutes saw a record $111m net retail buy, with Nvidia taking 61% of all US retail buying that week.

**Why it matters:** Positioning, not fundamentals: the retail book is maximally long into the first earnings print under interim CEO Kress on 17 Nov, which cuts both ways for the post-earnings move.
