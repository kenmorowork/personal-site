WT Micro guided Q4 revenue to a midpoint of NT$750bn (up 26% quarter on quarter and 119% year on year) with gross margin of 3.24%, while WPG posted Q3 revenue of NT$510bn, up 108.6%, and net profit of NT$1.039bn, up 226.9%, with memory at 58% of sales. Morgan Stanley cut its targets from NT$296 to NT$174 for WT Micro and from NT$160 to NT$79 for WPG, citing late-cycle memory, working-capital strain and its US team's push of about 5 GW of data-centre capacity from 2028 to 2029 (Morgan Stanley report, 2026-11-20, via Phoenix).

**Why it matters:** Target prices halved for both distributors despite record results underlines the late-cycle memory call: with memory at 58% of WPG's sales and working capital under strain, earnings quality rather than growth is now the question for the channel.
