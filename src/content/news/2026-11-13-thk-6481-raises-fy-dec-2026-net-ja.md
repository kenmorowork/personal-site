THK lifted its net profit guide by ¥4bn and its operating profit guide by ¥4bn to ¥52bn, 3.6x the prior year (Nikkei, 13 Nov); January–September revenue was ¥235.9bn, +34% y/y, and net profit ¥46.9bn, 8.9x y/y, as orders for ball screws and linear guides into semiconductor equipment stayed strong.

**Why it matters:** THK is a clean read-through from semiconductor capex to Japanese machine components: an upward revision driven by industrial-machinery orders, not autos, supports the machine-tool and factory-automation order cycle Ken covers.

**THK（6481）将2026年12月期净利润预期上调至588亿日元——营收预期上调至3200亿日元、同比+33%，受半导体相关工业订单拉动**



THK将净利润预期上调40亿日元，营业利润预期上调40亿日元至520亿日元、为上年的3.6倍（日经，11月13日）；1—9月营收为2359亿日元、同比+34%，净利润469亿日元、同比8.9倍，半导体设备用滚珠丝杠和直线导轨订单保持强劲。



THK是半导体资本开支向日本机械零部件传导的清晰样本：此次上调由工业机械订单而非汽车驱动，印证了Ken覆盖的工作机械和工厂自动化订单周期。
