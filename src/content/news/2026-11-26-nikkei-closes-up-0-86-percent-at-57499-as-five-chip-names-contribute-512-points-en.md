The Nikkei 225 opened up 417.92 points at 57,429.54, closed the morning at 57,886.95 and reached an intraday high of 58,251.93 before fading to 57,499.16. Kioxia (285A) closed up 12.57% at ¥7,762, SoftBank Group rose 7.32% to ¥2,894 and Advantest rose 4.56%, while Fast Retailing subtracted 54 points. Trading volume was about 2.24 billion shares, and US markets were closed for Thanksgiving.

**Why it matters:** The top five names contributed +512 points against an index gain of +488 — the rebound was narrower at the close than the index level suggests.
