The proof of concept used 18 server nodes and 144 XD8 Series E1.S NVMe SSDs, supporting 512-byte accesses for large vector databases, retrieval-augmented generation and graph neural network workloads. Kioxia's GP2 drive was reported at more than 18 million random reads per second on 512-byte blocks, nearly twice its predecessor, with evaluation samples expected by the end of 2027. Kioxia shares closed up 12.57% at ¥7,762 on November 26.

**Why it matters:** A 1.1 PB SSD tier at 80 times the rack's HBM reframes the memory bottleneck as a capacity problem that storage, not only HBM, can address.
