The Nikkei closed up 163.85 points at 59,024.28 on 18 Nov, its first rise in three sessions, after Nvidia's earnings lifted AI and chip shares intraday by more than 700 points at the peak. Advantest rose 4.56% and was the biggest contributor, while the broader TOPIX fell 16.02 points to 3,824.00, with only about a quarter of prime-market stocks rising.

**Why it matters:** The close reversed the midday fade — the Nikkei finished higher for the first time in three sessions — but breadth was poor: TOPIX fell and only about a quarter of prime-market stocks rose, so the rebound rests on narrow AI and semiconductor leadership into tonight's US session.
