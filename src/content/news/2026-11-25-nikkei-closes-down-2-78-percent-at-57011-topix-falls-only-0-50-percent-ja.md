The Nikkei 225 closed at 57,011.62 on 25 November, down 1,633.01 points (-2.78%), while the TOPIX closed at 3,746.20, down 18.68 points (-0.50%). Advantest fell 12.43% and Tokyo Electron fell 10.86%, while Sony rose 4.42% and pharmaceutical shares each rose more than 6%. Trading volume was about 2.734 billion shares.

**Why it matters:** The gap between the Nikkei and the TOPIX shows the fall was concentrated in AI and semiconductor names rather than spread across the market.
