BusinessToday (21 Nov) reported, citing Jefferies' 19 Nov GREED & fear note, that the US Bureau of Industry and Security introduced export licensing requirements for AI chips shipped to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore and Brazil, except qualifying exports to US companies, to close overseas data-centre loopholes; Jefferies estimates up to 240,000 Nvidia AI chips may have been diverted to China since 2024.

**Why it matters:** Licensing AI chip shipments to five countries extends the export-control perimeter to overseas data-centre routes into China; Jefferies' estimate of up to 240,000 diverted Nvidia chips since 2024 is the scale the rule is aimed at.
