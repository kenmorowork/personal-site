Japan Tobacco shares climbed 2% in early Tokyo trading on Friday 27 November after Bloomberg reported the company is considering partnering on a takeover of Imperial Brands, whose shares had jumped 13.8% the previous day to a £25.9bn ($34bn) market value. Altria said it is considering a possible all-cash bid and discussing part of Imperial's business with another party, while Imperial said it has received no approach.

**Why it matters:** A £25.9bn target with a 24 December UK deadline — how the assets would be split geographically is the open question in any joint bid.
