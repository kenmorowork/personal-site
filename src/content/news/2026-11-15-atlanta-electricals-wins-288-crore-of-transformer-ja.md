Atlanta Electricals has secured five new power-transformer orders worth ₹288 crore from Karnataka Power Transmission Corporation (KPTCL) and an independent power producer executing NTPC-linked projects, all for delivery within 12 months (India Renewable Energy News, 15 Nov 2026). The wins lift the company's order book to ₹2,787 crore, above the record ₹2,451 crore at end-December 2025; the KPTCL portion (₹146 crore) covers 13 transformers of 100–150 MVA at 220kV, and the IPP portion (₹142 crore) covers 15 units of 100–125 MVA.

**Why it matters:** Another data point that India's transmission build-out is still converting into transformer order books at the mid-cap tier, not only at Hitachi Energy India and GE Vernova — the demand breadth matters for the whole 220kV supply chain.

**Atlanta Electricals新获₹288 crore变压器订单——在手订单升至创纪录的₹2,787 crore**



Atlanta Electricals新获5份电力变压器订单、合计₹288 crore，客户为卡纳塔克邦输电公司（KPTCL）及一家承接NTPC相关项目的独立发电商，全部在12个月内交付（India Renewable Energy News，2026年11月15日）。公司在手订单升至₹2,787 crore，高于2025年12月底创纪录的₹2,451 crore；其中KPTCL部分（₹146 crore）包括13台100–150 MVA、220kV变压器，IPP部分（₹142 crore）包括15台100–125 MVA机组。



这再次表明，印度的输电建设仍在转化为中型厂商的变压器订单，而不仅仅是日立能源印度和GE Vernova——需求的广度对整个220kV供应链都很重要。
