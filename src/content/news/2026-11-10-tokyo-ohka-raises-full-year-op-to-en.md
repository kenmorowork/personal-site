Jan–Sep 2026 sales ¥217.5bn (+25.9% y/y), operating profit ¥46.0bn (+44.4%), net income ¥32.1bn (+45.7%). Full-year guidance raised: sales ¥291bn→¥300bn, operating profit ¥60.6bn→¥63.5bn — the mid-term plan's final-year target now seen a year early, driven by AI-server data-centre demand. Figures: Tokyo Ohka Q3 filing via NEWSjp, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Watch: electronic functional materials (+27.3% y/y) vs high-purity chemicals (+23.3%) mix — materials are confirming the wafer-start strength behind the equipment names.
