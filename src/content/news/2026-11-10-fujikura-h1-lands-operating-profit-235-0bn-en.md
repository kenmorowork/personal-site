H1 (Apr–Sep 2026) sales ¥867.4bn (+55.2% y/y), operating profit ¥235.0bn (+160.6%), net income ¥180.3bn (+168.5%); Info & Comms operating profit ¥217.7bn on sales ¥581.5bn (+91.6%). Full-year sales guidance raised to ¥1,768bn, but operating profit cut from ¥432bn to ¥429bn on second-half procurement costs and a ¥160→¥156 H2 FX assumption; implied H2 OP is only ¥194.0bn. Dividend raised ¥38→¥80. Figures: Fujikura interim filing, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Watch: whether the H2 margin step-down (implied OP margin ~21.5% vs 27.1% in H1) is cost pass-through timing or the peak of the fibre cycle.
