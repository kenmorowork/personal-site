Keisei Electric Railway (9009) raised its fiscal March 2027 net profit forecast to ¥64.2bn from ¥39.3bn, an increase of 63%, mainly on gains from selling its Oriental Land shares. Revenue was raised to ¥364.3bn from ¥359.8bn, operating profit to ¥31.7bn from ¥31.0bn and ordinary profit to ¥55.7bn from ¥50.5bn.

**Why it matters:** A ¥24.9bn net-profit upgrade in which operating profit moved only ¥0.7bn — the quality of the revision sits in the asset column, not the timetable.

**京成电铁将净利润预期上调63%至642亿日元，主要来自出售东方乐园股份的收益**

京成电铁（9009）将2027年3月期净利润预期从393亿日元上调63%至642亿日元，主要来自出售东方乐园股份的收益。营业收入预期从3598亿日元上调至3643亿日元，营业利润从310亿日元上调至317亿日元，经常利润从505亿日元上调至557亿日元。
