Rebellions will integrate its next-generation R200 NPUs into Nvidia's MGX rack architecture via NVLink Fusion, for deployment in SK Telecom's AI data centres under Korea's government-backed sovereign-AI programme. R200-based systems are expected to be ready for deployment in 2028. Source: eeNews Europe, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Watch: NVLink Fusion licensing is Nvidia renting its moat — custom-silicon competition gets absorbed into Nvidia racks rather than displacing them.

**英伟达向韩国Rebellions开放NVLink Fusion——R200 NPU进入SK电讯的MGX机架**



Rebellions将通过NVLink Fusion把其下一代R200 NPU集成到英伟达MGX机架架构，部署于SK电讯的AI数据中心，属于韩国政府支持的主权AI计划。基于R200的系统预计2028年可部署。来源：eeNews Europe，2026年11月10日。



关注：NVLink Fusion授权是英伟达在“出租护城河”——定制芯片竞争被吸纳进英伟达机架，而非取而代之。
