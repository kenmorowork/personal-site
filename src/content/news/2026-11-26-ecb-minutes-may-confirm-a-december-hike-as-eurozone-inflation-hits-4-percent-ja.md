The ECB left rates unchanged in October after last raising the deposit rate by 25bp with effect from September 16. The euro short-term rate was 2.444% on November 25, and the euro edged up 0.04% to $1.0980 in thin Thanksgiving trading. Bundesbank President Joachim Nagel was reported as saying a December hike is very likely needed.

**Why it matters:** With inflation at 4.0% and the deposit rate at 2.50%, the ECB's real rate is −1.50% — the December question is the pace of hikes, priced at 91% for 25bp.
