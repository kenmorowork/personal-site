The Nikkei rebounded 322.99 points (+0.53%) to 61,142.35 after rising as much as ¥1,083 intraday, as buybacks in AI and chip names faded on profit-taking and a softer KOSPI; TOPIX rose just 0.11% to 3,941.57 (fifth straight gain, per Kabutan/Fisco). SoftBank Group fell 5.11% to ¥3,547, while Ibiden (-4.05%), Fujikura (-3.20%) and Kioxia (-2.25%) turned negative by the close.

**Why it matters:** The close confirms the midday rebound was positioning, not conviction — TOPIX breadth stayed narrow (+0.11%) while former leaders SoftBank Group, Ibiden, Fujikura and Kioxia all closed lower, so chip exposure into Nvidia's 17 Nov print remains concentrated.
