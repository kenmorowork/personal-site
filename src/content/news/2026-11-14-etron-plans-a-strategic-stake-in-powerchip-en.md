Etron Technology plans a strategic stake in Powerchip Innovation Investment, Digitimes reported (14 Nov 2026). The Taiwan memory-IC designer's Q3 2026 revenue was NT$6.792bn (about $206.6m), up 38.6% q/q and up 535% y/y — the extreme operating leverage of a niche DRAM/NOR designer into the memory price spike that has run through Taiwan's chip chain this quarter. (Digitimes preview; full text paywalled.)

**Why it matters:** A second-order memory-tightness gauge: when a small niche-memory designer prints +535% y/y, the pricing power sits with memory supply — consistent with the SMIC and TSIA warnings already on this board.
