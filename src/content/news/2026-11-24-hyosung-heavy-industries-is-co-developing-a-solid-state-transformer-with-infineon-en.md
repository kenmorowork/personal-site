Korea's Hyosung Heavy Industries is jointly developing a solid-state transformer (SST) for AI data centres with Infineon, pairing its grid-equipment experience with Infineon's power devices for the 800V DC shift. Hyosung says the SST remains at prototype stage and is not yet commercialised. Figures: company comments reported by The Elec, 23 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Another grid-equipment incumbent is buying its way into the SST race through a chip partner — the same 800V DC contest as Jereh's PowerVEGA and TBEA's mass production.
