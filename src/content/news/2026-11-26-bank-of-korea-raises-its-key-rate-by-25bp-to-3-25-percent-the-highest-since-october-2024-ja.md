The rate is the highest since October 2024. Two board members, Hwang Kun-il and Lee Soo-hyung, voted to hold at 3.00%. Third-quarter GDP grew 3.4% year on year, beating forecasts, and Reuters linked the decision to a chip-driven export boom keeping inflation and housing prices elevated. Inflation eased from 3.1% in August to 2.9% in September and 2.8% in October, but stayed above target.

**Why it matters:** Korea is tightening because chip exports are strong, so this is a boom problem rather than a response to weakness.

**韩国央行加息25个基点至3.25%，为2024年10月以来最高，并将2027年增长预期上调至4.1%**

韩国央行在11月26日以5比2的票决将基准利率从3.00%上调25个基点至3.25%，在暂停三个月后恢复紧缩。该行将2026年增长预期上调至3.9%，2027年上调至4.1%。10月消费者价格同比上涨2.8%，仍高于2%的目标。
