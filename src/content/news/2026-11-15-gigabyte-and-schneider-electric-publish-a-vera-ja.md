Giga Computing (Gigabyte) and Schneider Electric announced a joint AI-factory reference architecture built on Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 rack, combining Gigabyte's compute platforms and POD Manager software with Schneider's power, liquid and hybrid cooling and facility infrastructure (15 Nov 2026). The design organises AI factories into repeatable POD-scale units and will be showcased at Supercomputing 2026 (SC26). No order values or deployment volumes were disclosed.

**Why it matters:** Reference architectures are how Rubin-era demand gets standardised before it becomes orders — the named stack (compute plus power plus liquid cooling in one POD) is the unit Ken's equipment coverage will be quoted against.

**技嘉与施耐德电气发布Vera Rubin NVL72 AI工厂参考架构**



技嘉旗下Giga Computing与施耐德电气于2026年11月15日宣布，推出基于英伟达Vera Rubin NVL72机架的AI工厂联合参考架构：技嘉提供计算平台与POD Manager软件，施耐德提供电力、液冷与混合冷却及设施基础设施。该设计将AI工厂组织为可复制的POD级单元，将在Supercomputing 2026（SC26）上展示。双方未披露订单金额或部署规模。



参考架构是Rubin时代需求在变成订单之前先被标准化的方式——计算+电力+液冷打包成一个POD，正是Ken设备覆盖今后会被拿来比价的单位。
