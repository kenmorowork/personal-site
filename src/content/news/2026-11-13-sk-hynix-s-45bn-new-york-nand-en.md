Reuters reporting carried by Sina Finance on 13 Nov says Washington has not approved equipment-export licences for SK hynix's Dalian plant, whose cash flow was meant to help fund Solidigm's planned two NAND fabs in New York (about $45bn, potentially up to $100bn).

**Why it matters:** Export licensing for an existing China plant now sits inside the funding path for new US NAND capacity — the Dalian decision is a gating item for Solidigm's New York build.
