Reuters reported on 12 Nov that Nvidia's board was weighing ousting CEO Jensen Huang; Nvidia fell about 7% premarket and Nasdaq 100 futures were -0.79% at 04:11 ET, with Intel, Marvell and Micron down 2.6-3.4% and CoreWeave -7.2%. Asian suppliers are redrawing plans; TSMC fell 2% and SK hynix 1.6% in the session reported. This is an escalation of the earlier board-probe story already on this dashboard. Figures: Reuters, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** An escalation from board probe to reported ouster talks turns a governance story into a sector-wide derating risk — Asian suppliers redrawing plans is the transmission channel to watch into the 17 Nov print.

**英伟达风波升级：董事会据报考虑罢免CEO黄仁勋，股价盘前跌约7%**



路透11月12日报道，英伟达董事会正考虑罢免CEO黄仁勋；英伟达盘前跌约7%，纳斯达克100期货在美东04:11跌0.79%，英特尔、美光等跌2.6–3.4%，CoreWeave跌7.2%。亚洲供应商开始重拟计划，台积电跌2%、SK海力士跌1.6%。此为本看板此前董事会调查报道的升级。数据来源：路透，2026年11月12日。



从董事会调查升级到据报考虑罢免CEO，治理事件正演变为全板块的估值下调风险；亚洲供应商重拟计划是11月17日财报前最值得跟踪的传导渠道。
