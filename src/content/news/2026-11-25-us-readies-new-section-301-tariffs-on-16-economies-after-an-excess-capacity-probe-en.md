The Trump administration is poised to release the findings of a Section 301 probe into excess industrial capacity in 16 economies, with new duties expected to stack on top of existing tariffs. Agreed caps are expected to be honoured at 15% for the EU, Japan and South Korea and 20% for China, while proposed rates of 15-25% have been cited for economies deemed to have excess capacity; no proclamation had been published at the time of reporting.

**Why it matters:** For Japan the 15% cap is already agreed, so the market-relevant questions are whether the new duties stack on existing tariffs and which products they cover.
