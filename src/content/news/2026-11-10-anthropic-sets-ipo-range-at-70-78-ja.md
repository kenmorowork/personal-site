Anthropic's amended S-1 on 10 Nov set an estimated IPO price of $70–$78 per share for 825m Class A shares (801.4m from the company, 23.6m from selling holders), implying gross proceeds of about $57.8–$64.4bn before discounts, listing on Nasdaq as ANT. Seven strategic buyers — Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC Partners — have agreed to buy $26.0bn of non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price. The filing sets no pricing or trading date; CNBC reported pricing on 18 Nov and trading on 19 Nov, which is a press report, not a filing date. Figures: Anthropic S-1/A, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** At the $74 midpoint the placement arithmetic makes Google and Nvidia roughly $10bn buyers each — the IPO is now a supply-chain financing as much as a listing.

Source: Anthropic Amendment No. 2 to Form S-1 · filed 10 Nov 2026, via Reuters — [original article](https://edgeconsultancykw.com/anthropic-ipo-price-range-70-78-dollars-825-million-class-a-shares-nasdaq-ant/)

**Anthropic设定IPO价格区间70–78美元、发行8.25亿股——260亿美元私募配售同步进行**



Anthropic于11月10日提交的修订版S-1将IPO预估价格定为每股70–78美元，发行8.25亿股A类股（公司发行8.014亿股、售股股东0.236亿股），募资总额约578–644亿美元（未计承销折扣），将在纳斯达克以代码ANT上市。谷歌、英伟达、AMD、博通、美光、微软与TSMC Partners七家战略买家已同意以IPO价格认购260亿美元无投票权N类股。文件未设定定价或上市日期；CNBC报道定价为11月18日、交易为11月19日，此为媒体报道而非文件日期。数据来源：Anthropic S-1修订版，2026年11月10日。



按74美元中值计算，谷歌与英伟达各认购约100亿美元——此次IPO既是上市，也是供应链融资。
