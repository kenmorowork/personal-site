Global Times reports smartphone ODM Longcheer's embodied-robot data-collection equipment has entered mass production and is being delivered (still no more than 1% of revenue at end-October), Avonflow HVAC subsidiary Yunxin Robotics has signed a precision structural-parts supply deal with a leading North American humanoid maker with the first batch scheduled for production, and Dechang is making small-batch deliveries of joint motors for dexterous robot hands. Source: Global Times, 22 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Consumer-electronics capacity moving into embodied AI — data-collection rigs in mass production, precision structural parts for a leading North American humanoid maker, joint motors in small-batch delivery — shows where China's robotics supply chain is forming first, even though Longcheer's contribution is still no more than 1% of revenue.
