Sales rose in every product segment, led by collagen peptide on North American protein demand and gelatin for gummy candy in Japan. Sales were ¥1,487m below the initial plan, while operating and ordinary profit were broadly in line with plan. Total assets rose ¥1,587m to ¥44,457m. The full-year net profit forecast was revised to ¥1.4bn after the impairment, with operating and ordinary profit forecasts unchanged.

**Why it matters:** The operating business set a record while reported net profit fell, and the whole gap is one cancelled IT project at ¥1.5bn.
