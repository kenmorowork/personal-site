Horiba (6856) reported Jan-Sep 2026 sales of ¥281.659 billion, up 22.3% year on year, and operating profit of ¥50.673 billion, up 51.2%, both records (BigGo, 16 Nov 2026). The semiconductor segment posted sales of ¥161.189 billion, up 40.4%, and operating profit of ¥50.224 billion, up 53.1%. Full-year guidance was raised to sales of ¥392.5 billion and operating profit of ¥73.0 billion, with a 1-for-5 split effective 1 Jan 2027.

**Why it matters:** Semiconductor metrology is converting AI wafer demand into record profit: Horiba's raised full-year guide and 1-for-5 split make the segment's leverage explicit.
