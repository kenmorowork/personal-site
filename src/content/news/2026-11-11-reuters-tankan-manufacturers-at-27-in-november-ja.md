The Reuters Tankan manufacturers' sentiment index rose to +27 in November from +22 in October, its highest since August 2021, while non-manufacturers jumped to +35 from +23, the highest since June 2018. Electronics sentiment rose to +39 from +25 on strong memory-chip demand, and precision machinery — which includes chip-equipment makers — rose to +42 from +38. The poll ran 27 Oct–6 Nov with 214 of 508 firms responding; the February outlook is +28 for manufacturers and +29 for non-manufacturers. Source: Reuters Tankan, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Sentiment confirms what the order books say: the chip cycle, not consumption, is carrying Japanese business confidence into the BOJ's December meeting.
