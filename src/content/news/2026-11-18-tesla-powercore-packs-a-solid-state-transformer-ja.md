At RE+ 2026 in Las Vegas Tesla launched Powercore, a single enclosure combining an inverter, switchgear and a solid-state transformer, accepting 800-2,000V DC and delivering 10-34.5kV AC at up to 7.4MW (9MVA). First units are due in service in 2027 from US factories, aimed at solar, storage, data centres and EV charging, and Tesla is pursuing Nvidia DSX Ready certification.

**Why it matters:** Powercore folds the solid-state transformer into a single 7.4MW block aimed at data centres as well as solar, storage and EV charging — another entrant productising the SST layer, with first units in 2027 and Nvidia DSX Ready certification as the checkpoints.

**特斯拉Powercore将固态变压器、逆变器与开关设备集成于一台7.4MW设备**



特斯拉在拉斯维加斯RE+ 2026上发布Powercore，将逆变器、开关设备与固态变压器集成于单一机柜，输入800–2,000V直流，输出10–34.5kV交流，最高7.4MW（9MVA）。首批设备计划2027年由美国工厂投运，面向光伏、储能、数据中心与电动车充电，特斯拉正申请英伟达DSX Ready认证。



Powercore将固态变压器整合进一台7.4MW设备，目标市场涵盖数据中心以及光伏、储能与电动车充电——又一家厂商将固态变压器层产品化，2027年首批投运与英伟达DSX Ready认证是检验点。
