The Nikkei fell 1,934.20 points, or 3.30%, to 56,710.43 at the morning close on 25 November, after rising 3.91% the previous day, while the Topix fell 1.00% to 3,727.24. The reversal followed a 7.9% fall in the US Philadelphia Semiconductor Index, with Advantest down about 12% intraday.

**Why it matters:** The two-day round trip came with no change in the record first-half earnings reported the same morning: positioning and AI-profitability sentiment moved, not the profit figures.
