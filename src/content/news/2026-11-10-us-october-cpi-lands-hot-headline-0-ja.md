BLS released Oct CPI at 22:30 JST on 10 Nov: headline +0.4% m/m (consensus +0.3%, Sep +0.6%), core +0.3% (consensus +0.2%) — the third straight +0.3% core month, annualising to ~3.6%. No y/y rates were published: Oct 2025 survey data was not collected during the shutdown. Shelter +0.3% drove over 30% of the monthly rise; energy +0.7%, food +0.5%. Figures: BLS CPI release, 10 Nov 2026; consensus via Dow Jones/Bloomberg surveys.

**Why it matters:** A third straight +0.3% core month keeps December hike pricing live — funding costs for leveraged AIDC borrowers stay the pressure point.

Source: BLS · 10 Nov 22:30 JST — [original article](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)

**美国10月CPI超预期：总体环比+0.4%、核心+0.3%，均高于预期0.1个百分点，无同比数据**



美国劳工统计局于11月10日22:30 JST公布：总体CPI环比+0.4%（预期+0.3%，9月+0.6%），核心CPI环比+0.3%（预期+0.2%），核心已连续第三个月+0.3%，折年率约3.6%。因停摆期间未采集2025年10月数据，本月未公布同比。住房+0.3%，贡献月度涨幅三成以上；能源+0.7%，食品+0.5%。数据来源：BLS，2026年11月10日；预期为道琼斯/彭博调查。



核心连续第三个月+0.3%，12月加息预期仍在——杠杆型AIDC借款人的融资成本仍是压力点。
