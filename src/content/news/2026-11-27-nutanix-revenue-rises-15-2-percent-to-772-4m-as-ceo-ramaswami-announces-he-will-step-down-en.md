Nutanix's first-quarter revenue rose 15.2% year on year to $772.4m, GAAP net income rose to $143.6m from $62.1m, and annual recurring revenue reached $2.63bn, up 15%. The company guided full-year revenue to $3.18-3.23bn and announced that chief executive Rajiv Ramaswami will step down after a successor is appointed, by April 2027 at the latest. The shares closed down 15.2% at $46.30 on 25 November.

**Why it matters:** A beat on both lines still lost 15% of the share price — the verdict was delivered on guidance and succession, not on the quarter.
