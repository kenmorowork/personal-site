Intel announced an expanded Microsoft EMIB packaging partnership on 18 Nov — Microsoft is its first public external EMIB customer, for Azure in-house chips — but Google is reported to have re-approached TSMC for next-generation TPU advanced packaging after EMIB-T yields missed expectations; no order size was disclosed.

**Why it matters:** Advanced packaging is the contested layer of the AI stack: a Google return to TSMC would undercut Intel's EMIB foundry pitch just as its first public EMIB customer was announced.

**传谷歌因英特尔EMIB-T良率不及预期，下一代TPU封装重回台积电**



英特尔11月18日宣布扩大与微软的EMIB封装合作，微软成为其首个公开的外部EMIB客户（用于Azure自研芯片）；但据报道，因EMIB-T良率未达预期，谷歌已就下一代TPU先进封装重新接洽台积电，报道未披露订单规模。



先进封装是AI供应链中争夺最激烈的一环：若谷歌重回台积电，将在英特尔刚公布首个公开EMIB客户之际削弱其EMIB代工主张。
