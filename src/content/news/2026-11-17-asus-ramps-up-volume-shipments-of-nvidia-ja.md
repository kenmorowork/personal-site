ASUS announced on 17 Nov at SC26 in Chicago that it is ramping up volume shipments of NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems in Q4 to meet robust demand, supported by L10/L11 manufacturing and its AI Factory services. Its showcase AI POD, the XA VR721-E3, integrates 72 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs in a fully liquid-cooled rack-scale design, with peak rack power demand of 227kW. Source: ASUS release via PR Newswire, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Vera Rubin racks are now in volume shipment from a second-wave ODM while the market sells the AI chain — shipments and selloff are running in opposite directions into Nvidia's results.

**华硕第四季度扩大英伟达Vera Rubin NVL72系统的批量出货**



华硕11月17日在芝加哥SC26上宣布，第四季度将扩大英伟达Vera Rubin NVL72系统的批量出货，以满足强劲需求，并由L10/L11制造与AI Factory服务支持。其展示的AI POD XA VR721-E3在全液冷机架级设计中集成了72块英伟达Rubin GPU和36块Vera CPU，机架峰值功耗为227kW。来源：华硕新闻稿（经PR Newswire），2026年11月17日。



在市场抛售AI链之际，Vera Rubin机架已从二线ODM进入批量出货阶段——出货与股价正朝相反方向运行，市场今晚迎来英伟达业绩检验。
