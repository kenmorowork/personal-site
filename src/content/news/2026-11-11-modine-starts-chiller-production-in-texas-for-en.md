Modine announced on 11 Nov the start of commercial production at a new 682,000-square-foot Airedale chiller factory in Grand Prairie, Texas, dedicated to 2MW and 3MW chillers with free cooling for hyperscale and high-density AI computing. The first production line is operating, with a target of four lines by the end of the current fiscal year and a scale-up from one shift to three. The plant follows a $100m investment announced in July 2025; management targets total data-centre revenue approaching $2bn in fiscal 2028. Figures: Modine via Unite.AI, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Cooling is the other half of the AI power story — a new US chiller plant turning into operating capacity is a supply-side data point to set against the transformer and power bottlenecks elsewhere on this page.
