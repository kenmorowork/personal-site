The Nikkei ended the 13 Nov morning session at 61,816.72, up 997.36 points (+1.64%), and TOPIX rose 0.77% to 3,967.54, as AI and semiconductor names including Advantest and Tokyo Electron were bought back and good earnings supported the tape (Reuters, 13 Nov). November options SQ passed without disruption at an estimated 61,414.10; strategists said the next read on AI/chip names waits on Nvidia's earnings next week.

**Why it matters:** Tokyo treated the Nvidia shock as a one-day event: the rebound was led by the same chip names sold on 12 Nov, so positioning, not fundamentals, drove both days.
