The CleverHack summary of J.P. Morgan data puts Broadcom's custom chips at 23% of the roughly 44GW of frontier-lab compute agreements for 2026 and later, AMD at 18%, Amazon Trainium at 16%, Google TPU at 14% and Cerebras at 2%. Anthropic's 10 November filing showed about $900.9bn of compute and infrastructure commitments, the largest lines being Broadcom leases of $161.2bn and Google at at least $121.3bn, with no direct Nvidia compute deal (CleverHack, 21 Nov 2026).

**Why it matters:** An 83% share of AI chip revenue for 2023–2025 against only 28% of forward frontier-lab compute agreements is the quantified version of the custom-silicon shift — the forward order book is far more contested than the historical revenue split.

**摩根大通数据（经CleverHack）：英伟达拿下2023–2025年AI芯片收入的83%，但在2026年以后约44GW前沿实验室算力协议中仅占28%**



CleverHack汇总的摩根大通数据显示，在2026年以后约44GW的前沿实验室算力协议中，博通定制芯片占23%、AMD占18%、亚马逊Trainium占16%、谷歌TPU占14%、Cerebras占2%。Anthropic 11月10日的文件显示其算力与基础设施承诺约9,009亿美元，最大两项为博通租赁1,612亿美元与谷歌至少1,213亿美元，且没有与英伟达的直接算力交易（CleverHack，2026年11月21日）。



2023–2025年AI芯片收入份额83%、而前瞻算力协议仅占28%，量化了定制芯片的迁移趋势——未来的订单簿远比历史收入结构更具竞争性。
