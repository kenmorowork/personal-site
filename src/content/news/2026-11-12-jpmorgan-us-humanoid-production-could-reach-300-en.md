JPMorgan (12 Nov) sees US humanoid robot production of 300,000-400,000 units by 2030 and about 1.5 million by 2035; most US makers ex-Tesla target 10,000-30,000 units a year. Bottlenecks: hands, actuators, compute and capacity. The key-component market (actuators, motors, vision) could approach US$30bn by 2035. Figures: JPMorgan via Reuters / CNBC TV18, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A bank putting unit numbers and a ~US$30bn component market on US humanoids gives the actuator, motor and vision chain a sizing anchor — hands and actuators are named as the bottleneck.
