Announced 19 November in Munich: Megmeet's three-phase AI power platform will use Infineon's CoolSiC MOSFET G2 devices; the scope extends from server PSUs and intermediate bus converters to solid-state transformers for next-generation AI data centres (China AET / Infineon release, 19 Nov 2026).

**Why it matters:** Another named pairing around the 800V DC architecture — Infineon silicon inside Megmeet's platform across PSUs, IBCs and SSTs — as the AI rack power stack consolidates around a few supplier combinations.
