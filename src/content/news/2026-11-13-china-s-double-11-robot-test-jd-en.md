JD's Double 11 data show smart humanoid robot sales up 700% y/y and GMV in trend categories such as robotic arms and dexterous hands up more than 10x, with Songyan Power taking first place in both JD humanoid sales and GMV over 9 Oct–11 Nov (科创板日报/CLS, 13 Nov). On the industry side, RobotEra's embodied sorting robots ran at more than a dozen China Post and SF Express logistics centres at a peak of 1,500 items per hour during the sale — the report's point is that delivery capacity and production lines, not demand, are now the real test.

**Why it matters:** China's humanoid demand signal is now measured in sell-through and live sorting throughput, giving a demand-side benchmark against the supply-side capacity stories from UBTech and Schaeffler earlier this week.
