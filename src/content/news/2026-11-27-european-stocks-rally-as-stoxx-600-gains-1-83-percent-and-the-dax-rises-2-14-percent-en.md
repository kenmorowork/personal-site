European stocks rallied on Thursday 26 November, with the Stoxx 600 up 1.83% at 636.98, the DAX up 2.14%, the CAC 40 up 1.71% at 8,108.74, the FTSE 100 up 1.77% and Italy's FTSE MIB up 2.36%. Every major sector finished higher, with miners, automakers and defence shares each advancing more than 3%. Imperial Brands surged about 17.6% in London on talk of an Altria offer.

**Why it matters:** The whole rally printed while US cash markets were shut for Thanksgiving — it measures how much tariff fear had been priced, in thin holiday liquidity.
