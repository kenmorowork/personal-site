Nomura raised its 2027 global AI server revenue estimate by 32%, for growth of 143%, mainly on higher average selling prices, while cutting general-purpose server shipments by 15% for 2027. TSMC CoWoS output is assumed at 1.81 million wafers in 2027 and 2.34 million in 2028, with ASE and Amkor CoWoS-like demand seen at 5.5 times the 2026 level.

**Why it matters:** The revision rises on price rather than volume: AI server shipments were raised only 5% for 2027 while revenue was raised 32%.

**野村将2027年AI服务器收入预期上调32%，预计台积电CoWoS产能2027年增长约80%**

野村将2027年全球AI服务器收入预期上调32%，增速达143%，主要受平均售价上升推动，同时将2027年通用服务器出货量下调15%。台积电CoWoS产量预计2027年为181万片晶圆，2028年为234万片，日月光与安靠的类CoWoS需求预计为2026年的5.5倍。
