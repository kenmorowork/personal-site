Daifuku (6383) reported the nine months to September on 11 Nov: sales ¥553.78bn, +13.9% y/y; operating profit ¥83.49bn, +11.0%; net profit ¥63.28bn, +8.2%. It raised full-year guidance (Japan GAAP) — sales from ¥735.0bn to ¥770.0bn, operating profit from ¥113.0bn to ¥119.0bn, and the orders range from ¥860–900bn to ¥970bn–¥1,000bn — citing a favourable order environment and cleanroom systems revenue recognised earlier than expected; the IFRS reference operating profit is ¥117.0bn. The board also resolved on 11 Nov to repurchase up to ¥40.0bn (6.6m shares); the annual dividend is ¥90 (¥40 interim + ¥50 year-end). Figures: Daifuku Q3 materials, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Cleanroom systems are the semiconductor-fab end of material handling — the orders range approaching ¥1tn is a fab-capex read-across, not just logistics.

Source: Daifuku Q3 materials · 11 Nov — [original article](https://www.daifuku.com/ir/policy/message/)

**大福上调全年指引并推出400亿日元回购——前三季度销售额5538亿日元，同比+13.9%**



大福（6383）11月11日公布截至9月的九个月业绩：销售额5537.8亿日元，同比+13.9%；营业利润834.9亿日元，同比+11.0%；净利润632.8亿日元，同比+8.2%。公司上调全年指引（日本准则）：销售额从7350亿日元上调至7700亿日元，营业利润从1130亿日元上调至1190亿日元，订单区间从8600–9000亿日元上调至9700亿–1万亿日元，理由是订单环境良好且无尘室系统收入确认早于预期；IFRS参考营业利润为1170亿日元。董事会于11月11日决议回购至多400亿日元（660万股）；全年股息90日元（中期40日元+期末50日元）。数据来源：大福三季度资料，2026年11月11日。



无尘室系统是物料搬运中面向半导体工厂的一端——订单区间逼近1万亿日元，是晶圆厂资本开支的映射，而不只是物流景气。
