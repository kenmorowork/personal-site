Schaeffler has opened a lighthouse factory for humanoid robotics in Taicang, China, consolidating component production, robot training, data collection and digital-twin work on one site of about 7,500 square metres. Under its 'humanoids making humanoids' concept, humanoid robots assemble key joint modules, including linear and rotary actuators, for next-generation humanoids. Schaeffler said it won two new customers in Greater China in Q3 2026, taking its humanoid robotics order intake to about €400m. Figures: Schaeffler press release, Japanese edition published 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A named actuator supplier putting humanoids on its own production line, with a €400m order figure attached, is one of the few humanoid stories with both a factory and a number.

**舍弗勒太仓“灯塔工厂”启用：人形机器人组装人形机器人关节模组，订单达约4亿欧元**



舍弗勒在中国太仓启用人形机器人先进“灯塔工厂”，约7500平方米的场地集零部件制造、机器人训练、数据采集和数字孪生于一体。按照“人形机器人制造人形机器人”的构想，人形机器人负责组装下一代人形机器人的关键关节模组，包括直线和旋转执行器。舍弗勒表示，2026年三季度在大中华区新增两家客户，人形机器人业务订单额达约4亿欧元。数据来源：舍弗勒新闻稿，日文版2026年11月12日发布。



一家具名的执行器供应商把人形机器人放上自家产线，并给出4亿欧元订单数字——这是少数同时有工厂和数字的人形机器人消息。
