Larsen & Toubro's LTN Compute won a multi-year contract classed as a large order (L&T's category for INR 2,500-5,000 crore) to deploy additional Nvidia B300 GPUs in Chennai for an unnamed AI inference provider. The campus targets a 10,000-GPU cluster and 250 MW first-phase capacity, following a 52 MW data-centre start in October 2026. (MartechAI, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** India's AI factory build-out is moving from announcements to multi-year GPU deployment contracts with named power capacity.

**L&T获得多年期大额订单，在金奈AI数据中心部署更多英伟达B300 GPU，规划建设万卡集群**

拉森图博旗下LTN Compute获得一份多年期合同，属于其“大额订单”类别（25亿至50亿卢比），将在金奈为一家未具名的AI推理服务商部署更多英伟达B300 GPU。该园区规划建设10,000块GPU集群，一期容量目标为250兆瓦，此前52兆瓦数据中心已于2026年10月开工。
