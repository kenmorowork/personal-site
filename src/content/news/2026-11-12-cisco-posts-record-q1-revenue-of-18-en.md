Cisco's Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 24 Oct 2026, reported 12 Nov) revenue was a record $18.5bn, +24% y/y — its strongest growth in over 15 years — with GAAP EPS of $1.14 (+58%) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.40 (+40%) (Cisco Newsroom, 12 Nov). It raised FY27 revenue guidance to $74.8–75.8bn from $72.2–73.4bn and lifted expected hyperscaler AI infrastructure revenue to about $9bn, more than double FY26; Q2 guidance is $18.0–18.2bn.

**Why it matters:** Networking is converting AI capex into record revenue with a guide raise, not just orders — the demand signal extends from GPUs to switching and optics, relevant to Fujikura and Japan's fibre/component names.
