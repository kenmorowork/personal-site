Reuters reported on Friday, citing two people briefed on the matter, that SEC enforcement staff are scrutinising potential accounting issues at Nvidia — the accounting focus is newly reported; the existence of a probe was reported earlier by the WSJ. Nvidia closed at $125.84, down 13.80% on the day (Finnhub, 20 Nov 16:00 EST), down roughly 20% on the week — its worst week since 2018 (WSJ / Dow Jones Market Data) — and 47% below its 6 October high (Barron's), slipping behind Alphabet to fourth among US companies by market value.

**Why it matters:** An accounting-focused SEC probe adds a regulatory overhang to the AI bellwether on top of a 47% drawdown from its October high; any company response is the next checkpoint, and the shares now rank only fourth among US companies by market value.
