GS Group broke ground on 28 November on a 2.4 GW AI data centre campus in Donghae, Gangwon Province, in two phases of 1.2 GW each. The first 100 MW is permitted and targeted for the first half of 2028, and planned investment is ₩30tn, or up to ₩120tn (US$89.79bn) including GPU and memory costs.

**Why it matters:** The scale is in the phases, not the opening — 100 MW is permitted and dated; the remaining 2.3 GW rests on roadmap dates that differ by a year between reports.
