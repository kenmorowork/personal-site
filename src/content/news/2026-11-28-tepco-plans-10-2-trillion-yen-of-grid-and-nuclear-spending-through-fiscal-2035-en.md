Tokyo Electric Power Company Holdings plans to spend ¥10.2 trillion through fiscal 2035, including ¥4.9 trillion on transmission and distribution and ¥2.5 trillion on nuclear facilities, as it targets data-centre electricity sales of around 340 billion kWh in fiscal 2035, up from about 43 billion kWh in fiscal 2027. Cumulative investment through fiscal 2040 is expected to exceed ¥15 trillion, funded in part through capital alliances under negotiation with five investor groups including SoftBank, KKR and Blackstone.

**Why it matters:** The bottleneck for Japan's AI build-out is being priced by the utility itself: ¥10.2 trillion of grid and nuclear spending, funded partly by outside capital, against an eightfold rise in data-centre power sales.
