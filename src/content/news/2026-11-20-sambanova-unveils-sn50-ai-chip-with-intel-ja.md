SN50 claims up to 5x the compute per accelerator and 4x the network bandwidth of the prior generation, linking up to 256 accelerators, with customer shipments planned for the second half of 2026. The oversubscribed Series E was led by Vista Equity Partners and Cambium Capital, with Intel Capital participating; SambaNova and Intel plan a multi-year collaboration on cloud-scale AI inference.

**Why it matters:** A $350m-plus raise, a multi-year Intel collaboration and SoftBank as first customer put a named alternative-accelerator vendor into Japan's next-generation AI data-centre build-out — the H2 2026 shipment start is the checkpoint.

**SambaNova发布SN50 AI芯片并与英特尔结盟，融资超3.5亿美元；软银成为其日本下一代AI数据中心的首个客户**



SN50单加速器算力最高达上一代的5倍、网络带宽达4倍，最多可连接256个加速器，计划于2026年下半年开始向客户出货。超额认购的E轮由Vista Equity Partners和Cambium Capital领投，Intel Capital参投；SambaNova与英特尔计划在云规模AI推理领域开展多年合作。



逾3.5亿美元融资、与英特尔的多年合作以及软银成为首个客户，使一家具名的替代加速器厂商进入日本下一代AI数据中心建设——2026年下半年开始出货是检验点。
