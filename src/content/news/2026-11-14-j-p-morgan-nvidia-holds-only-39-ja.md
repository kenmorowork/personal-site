In Eye on the Market (14 Nov), J.P. Morgan's Michael Cembalest charts frontier labs' future compute agreements at roughly 41GW in total: Nvidia 39% (about 16GW), Broadcom custom chips 24%, Google TPUs 16%, AMD GPUs 15% and Amazon Trainium 6% — custom silicon together 46%, against Nvidia's 80%-plus market share in 2023–2025, when it earned 83% of AI chip revenues at a 75% gross margin and a 66% operating margin. The note says one unnamed lab now onboards models onto AMD GPUs in 3 days, versus 6–12 months a year ago, and that of the seven largest accelerator buyers only SpaceX is not building its own chips. Nvidia's filing-based commitments rose from $214bn in Q1 FY27 to $422bn in Q2 FY27. The note is Cembalest's own view and states it is not J.P. Morgan Research.

**Why it matters:** The share loss is to customers' own silicon, not to AMD alone: if the 41GW pipeline splits this way, merchant-GPU growth decouples from total AI capex growth.

**摩根大通：英伟达仅占未来前沿实验室算力协议的39%——博通、谷歌与亚马逊的定制芯片合计46%**



摩根大通Michael Cembalest在11月14日的《Eye on the Market》中测算，前沿实验室未来的算力协议总量约为41GW：英伟达占39%（约16GW）、博通定制芯片24%、谷歌TPU 16%、AMD GPU 15%、亚马逊Trainium 6%——定制芯片合计46%；而英伟达在2023–2025年的市场份额在80%以上，拿走了83%的AI芯片收入，毛利率75%、营业利润率66%。报告称，一家未具名实验室如今把模型迁移到AMD GPU只需3天，一年前需要6–12个月；七大加速器买家中只有SpaceX没有自研芯片。英伟达基于财报的承诺额从2027财年一季度的2,140亿美元升至二季度的4,220亿美元。该报告为Cembalest个人观点，声明不构成摩根大通研究。



份额流失主要流向客户自研芯片而非仅AMD：若41GW的在谈规模按此切分，商用GPU的增长将与AI总资本开支脱钩。
