Korea's exports rose 93.9% year on year to $115.41bn in October 2026, an October record and a second straight month above $110bn, Korea Customs' final data showed; imports rose 36.0% to $72.77bn, leaving a $42.65bn surplus, a 21st straight monthly surplus. January-October exports reached $930.20bn, up 60.6% year on year. Exports to Japan rose 44.0% to $3.20bn, led by memory semiconductors (+462.2%), computer peripherals (+206.2%) and semiconductor manufacturing equipment (+185.8%). Source: Korea Customs final October data via MT News, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A 93.9% export jump led by semiconductors and machinery — with memory exports to Japan up 462.2% — is a hard-data read on how far the memory upcycle is carrying Asia's trade, not just Korea's chip names.

**韩国10月出口同比增93.9%至1,154亿美元，半导体与机械领涨；顺差连续21个月**



韩国关税厅终值显示，2026年10月出口同比增93.9%至1,154.1亿美元，创10月纪录且连续第二个月超过1,100亿美元；进口增36.0%至727.7亿美元，顺差426.5亿美元，为连续第21个月顺差。1—10月累计出口9,302.0亿美元，同比增60.6%。对日本出口增44.0%至32.0亿美元，由存储半导体（+462.2%）、电脑外设（+206.2%）与半导体制造设备（+185.8%）领涨。来源：韩国关税厅10月终值，经MT News，2026年11月16日。



出口增93.9%、由半导体与机械领涨，对日存储半导体出口更增462.2%——这是硬数据，显示存储上行周期正带动整个亚洲贸易，而不只是韩国芯片股。
