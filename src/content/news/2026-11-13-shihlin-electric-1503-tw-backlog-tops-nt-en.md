Taiwan heavy-electrical maker Shihlin Electric reported Q3 2026 net income of NT$888m with EPS of NT$1.70, a record for the period, and first-three-quarter EPS of NT$6.74 versus NT$5.46 a year earlier — already above the NT$6.27 earned in all of last year (results released 12 Nov, via BigGo, 13 Nov). Nine-month net income rose 23.4% y/y to NT$3.513bn on revenue of NT$30.079bn. Taiwan Power's Grid Resilience Plan keeps releasing orders, lifting the backlog past NT$60bn; a fourth transformer plant completes in September 2027, lifting transformer capacity by about 30%, with revenue contribution from 2028.

**Why it matters:** Another Taiwan transformer name printing record EPS on grid-resilience orders — with capacity relief only in 2028, the same lead-time structure as the US and Indian order books on this board.
