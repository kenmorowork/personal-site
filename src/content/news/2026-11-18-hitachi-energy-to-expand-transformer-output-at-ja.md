Nikkei reported on 18 Nov 2026 that Hitachi Energy will move to an expanded production setup at its transformer plant in Samut Prakan, Thailand, from early 2027, responding to rising power demand in Asia as AI spreads.

**Why it matters:** An expanded setup at Samut Prakan from early 2027 adds transformer capacity inside Asia, where AI-driven power demand is tightening the supply of grid equipment — capacity location, not just volume, is the point.

**日立能源将自2027年初扩大泰国工厂变压器产量，应对AI带来的电力需求增长**



《日本经济新闻》2026年11月18日报道，日立能源将自2027年初在其位于泰国北榄府的变压器工厂转入增产体制，以应对AI普及带来的亚洲电力需求增长。



自2027年初在北榄府转入增产体制，为亚洲本地再添变压器产能——在AI电力需求使电网设备供给趋紧之际，产能的区位与规模同样重要。
