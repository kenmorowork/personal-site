GMO Internet Group forecast business profit up 9% to ¥67bn and revenue up 12% to ¥320bn for the year to December 2026, including about ¥3bn of hacking-related costs. For December 2027 it guided revenue of ¥330bn or more, business profit of ¥74bn or more and a dividend of ¥73 or more per share.

**Why it matters:** A nine-year silence on guidance ended because of a security incident, so the disclosure itself is the governance signal.
