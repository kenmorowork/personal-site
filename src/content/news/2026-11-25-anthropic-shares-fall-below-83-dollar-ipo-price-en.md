Anthropic's shares fell 6.54% to $81.91, below the $83 IPO price, after reaching $111 on the market debut on 19 November 2026. The report cited heightened scrutiny of AI-related firms and concerns over elevated capital expenditure. On the same day Dell fell 11.83% in the regular session and Hewlett Packard Enterprise fell 16.31%, despite both beating earnings estimates.

**Why it matters:** A recent AI listing trading below its IPO price within a week marks how quickly sentiment toward the AI complex has turned.
