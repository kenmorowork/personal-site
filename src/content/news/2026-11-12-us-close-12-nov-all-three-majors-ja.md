On 12 Nov the Dow closed at 52,297.09 (-0.36%), the S&amp;P 500 at 7,592.40 (-0.35%) and the Nasdaq at 25,366.09 (-0.39%), with the Russell 2000 down 1.7% (Swingfolio/EODHD data, 12 Nov). Nvidia pared an intraday -14% to close -4.34% at $166.19 after CEO Huang's resignation, while AMD (+5.86%) and Broadcom (+5.54%) led the S&amp;P 500; the 10-year Treasury yield rose 10.5bp to 5.10%.

**Why it matters:** The first full session after the resignation shows rotation inside the AI complex rather than an exit from it — the index damage came as much from the 5.10% yield as from Nvidia itself.

**美股11月12日收盘：三大指数均跌约0.4%，英伟达-4.3%被市场消化——AMD涨5.9%、博通涨5.5%领涨，10年期美债收益率升至5.10%**



11月12日，道指收于52,297.09点（-0.36%），标普500收于7,592.40点（-0.35%），纳指收于25,366.09点（-0.39%），罗素2000下跌1.7%（Swingfolio/EODHD数据，11月12日）。英伟达在CEO黄仁勋辞职后盘中一度跌14%，收盘收窄至-4.34%，报166.19美元；AMD（+5.86%）与博通（+5.54%）领涨标普500。10年期美债收益率上升10.5个基点至5.10%。



辞职后的首个完整交易日显示资金是在AI板块内部轮动，而非撤离——指数的压力同样来自5.10%的收益率，而不仅是英伟达本身。
