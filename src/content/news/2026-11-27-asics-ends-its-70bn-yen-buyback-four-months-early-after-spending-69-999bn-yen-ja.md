ASICS ended its share buyback after spending ¥69.999bn of the ¥70bn cash ceiling on 16,423,800 shares, fewer than the 20,000,000-share ceiling authorised on 9 September 2026. The final tranche, from 1 to 25 November, bought 12,224,400 shares for ¥53.0bn — about three-quarters of the programme's shares in a single month. The disclosure does not state why the programme ended or what will be done with the shares.

**Why it matters:** The buyback accelerated into November rather than running to its deadline — the cash cap, not the share cap, was the binding limit.
