The US Commerce Department added Singapore-based Megaspeed to its Entity List, barring purchases of sensitive US technology including advanced AI chips without a licence, over allegations it transferred Nvidia processors to China in breach of export controls. The move came hours after Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive. (Times Now News, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Entity List action against a data-centre operator tightens the enforcement net around Southeast Asian AI-chip flows.
