Keisei Electric Railway tendered 15,706,800 shares of Oriental Land at ¥3,820 per share into OLC's off-auction own-share repurchase (ToSTNeT-3) on 25 November, for up to ¥60bn. Keisei's voting stake in OLC falls from 20.10% to a minimum of 19.33%, and Keisei expects to book a gain on sale of affiliate shares and an equity-change loss in the third quarter of the year ending March 2027.

**Why it matters:** A ¥60bn policy-shareholding sale that takes the stake below 20% is both a governance step and a source of growth-investment funding for Keisei.
