SemiAnalysis estimates SpaceX's Memphis–Mississippi AI campus will grow from 0.4GW of IT capacity in January 2026 to 2.4GW by year-end across five sites (X thread, 14 Nov 2026; estimates from its paid datacentre model, satellite imagery and local filings — SpaceX does not publish site capacity). Build speed is the claim: Colossus 2 added 210MW in 91 days against 150MW in 122 days for Colossus 1's first retrofit. The constraint is power: the firm counts 1,377 Tesla Megapacks (nearly 6GWh) at Colossus 2 and puts the possible shortfall above 300MW once the Southaven turbine limit falls to 1.24GW nameplate in July 2027.

**Why it matters:** If the estimates are right, the binding constraint on the fastest AI build-out in the US is no longer chips or buildings but permitted power — the same bottleneck repricing transformers, turbines and now grid-scale batteries.

**SemiAnalysis：SpaceX孟菲斯园区到2026年底IT容量将达2.4GW——为1月的六倍，但仍有300MW以上的电力缺口**



SemiAnalysis估计，SpaceX位于孟菲斯—密西西比州界的AI园区IT容量将从2026年1月的0.4GW增至年底的2.4GW，分布在五个站点（X帖子，2026年11月14日；数字来自其付费数据中心模型、卫星图像和地方文件——SpaceX不公布站点容量）。其核心说法是建设速度：Colossus 2用91天增加了210MW，而Colossus 1首期改造用122天增加150MW。约束在电力：该机构在Colossus 2数到1,377个特斯拉Megapack（近6GWh），并认为2027年7月Southaven燃机限值降至1.24GW铭牌后，缺口可能超过300MW。



如果估计成立，美国最快的AI建设所受的约束已不再是芯片或厂房，而是获批的电力——这正是让变压器、燃机乃至电网级电池重新定价的同一个瓶颈。
