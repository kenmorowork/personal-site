The Week 47 robotics roundup also notes Hyundai completed its purchase of the rest of Boston Dynamics the same week, XPENG's IRON faces a live audience on 25 November ahead of mass production due 1 January 2027, and UBTECH's U1 companion humanoid has over 13,000 orders (Elia Robotics, 16–22 Nov 2026).

**Why it matters:** Atlas moving into chip factories — a high-value setting outside Hyundai's car plants — is the first named industrial deployment test for the platform; the early-2027 start at GlobalFoundries sites is the checkpoint.

**Atlas获汽车业外首份工作：格芯将于2027年初起在其芯片工厂测试波士顿动力Atlas人形机器人**



第47周机器人综述还提到：现代汽车同周完成了对波士顿动力剩余股权的收购；小鹏IRON将于11月25日面向现场观众展示，量产定于2027年1月1日；优必选U1陪伴人形机器人订单已超过13,000台（Elia Robotics，2026年11月16–22日）。



Atlas进入芯片工厂——现代汽车工厂之外的高价值环境——是该平台首个具名的工业部署测试；2027年初在格芯工厂的启动是检验点。
