At Ignite 2026 on 17 Nov, Microsoft said its second-generation Maia 200 inference accelerator is now in production and running its largest frontier models, alongside seven new MAI models. The chip is built on TSMC's 3nm process with 216GB of HBM3e and 272MB of SRAM, and Microsoft claims up to 1.7x more tokens per watt than leading GPU infrastructure in Teams, Copilot and Cowork. Maia 200 is deploying in the US, Europe and Australia, with GitHub Copilot and Foundry to follow, and Microsoft plans multi-gigawatt Azure Maia scale by 2027. Source: Microsoft via Thurrott / AIBase, 17-18 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A second hyperscaler custom-silicon programme reaching production — 3nm, 216GB HBM3e, a 1.7x tokens-per-watt claim — is another dated data point for merchant-GPU share pressure and for TSMC and HBM demand outside Nvidia.
