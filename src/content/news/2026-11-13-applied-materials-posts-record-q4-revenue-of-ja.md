Applied Materials' fiscal Q4 2026 (Aug–Oct, reported 12 Nov US) revenue hit a record $10.41bn, +53% y/y, with adjusted EPS of $4.16 (+92%) beating the $4.06 consensus and net income up 43% to $2.72bn (BigGo Finance, 13 Nov). Semiconductor Systems revenue surged 64% to $8.0bn and non-GAAP gross margin widened 250bp to 50.6%; full-year revenue was a record $34.45bn (+21%). Q1 FY27 guidance is $11.1bn ±$0.5bn vs the $10.89bn consensus, with adjusted EPS of $4.35.

**Why it matters:** The largest WFE maker says 2027 WFE demand grows more than 30% and demand exceeds supply in leading-edge logic and DRAM — a direct read-across for Tokyo Electron, SCREEN and Japan's equipment chain into Nvidia's 17 Nov print.
