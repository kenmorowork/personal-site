In evening announcements on 19 Nov, Changxin Technology (688825.SH, CXMT affiliate) raised its buyback plan to ¥2.5–5.0bn with a cap of ¥63.78/share; Hygon (688041.SH) made its first buyback, 1.3768m shares for ¥235m at ¥162.69–174.99; and Enflame (688801.SH) said orders in hand are ample and it is working with domestic partners on a domestic-supply-chain cloud AI chip, after a 20% limit-down move that day amid the Samsung foundry rumour (exchange announcements summarised by JRJ).

**Why it matters:** Buybacks stepped up on the same evening the TSMC halt landed: Changxin and Hygon are signalling balance-sheet support for domestic chip names just as the external supply constraint tightens.

**中国芯片企业晚间公告加码回购：长鑫科技上调至25亿至50亿元，海光信息首次回购斥资2.35亿元**



11月19日晚间公告中，长鑫科技（688825.SH，长鑫存储关联公司）将回购计划上调至25亿至50亿元，价格上限为每股63.78元；海光信息（688041.SH）实施首次回购，回购137.68万股、斥资2.35亿元，价格区间为每股162.69–174.99元；燧原科技（688801.SH）表示在手订单充足，正与国内伙伴推进国产供应链云端AI芯片——该股当日因三星代工传闻出现20%跌停（交易所公告，经金融界汇总）。



回购加码恰逢台积电停单落地的同一晚间：长鑫与海光正以资产负债表为国产芯片股提供支撑，而外部供给约束同时收紧。
