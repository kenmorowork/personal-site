Shochiku (9601) cut its consolidated net income forecast for the fiscal year ending February 2027 to ¥1.64bn from ¥2.20bn, a fall of 25.5%, and cut forecast earnings per share to ¥119.30 from ¥160.00. The sales forecast was left unchanged at ¥100.0bn, operating income at ¥3.70bn and ordinary income at ¥3.50bn; the revision followed a reassessment of gains and losses linked to a fixed-asset transfer.

**Why it matters:** The operating line did not move at all — a 25.5% net cut driven entirely below the operating line is an asset-transfer story, not a box-office story.

**松竹将净利润预期下调25.5%至16.4亿日元，源于固定资产转让的重新评估，销售额与营业利润预期不变**

松竹（9601）将2027年2月期合并净利润预期从22亿日元下调25.5%至16.4亿日元，每股收益预期从160.00日元下调至119.30日元。销售额预期维持1000亿日元不变，营业利润维持37亿日元，经常利润维持35亿日元；此次修正源于对固定资产转让相关特别损益的重新评估。
