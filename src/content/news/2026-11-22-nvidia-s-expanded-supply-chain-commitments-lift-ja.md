CNA reported on 22 Nov 2026 that Nvidia's expanded long-term commitments to partners including TSMC, Foxconn, SPIL, Kinsus, KYEC and Unimicron are stabilising confidence after the CEO transition, according to Taiwan Institute of Economic Research analyst Liu Pei-chen. With Vera Rubin NVL72 volume shipments under way, Nvidia's overall supply commitments have risen to US$390bn, giving Taiwanese suppliers order visibility over the next few quarters.

**Why it matters:** The read-across is order visibility: US$390bn of commitments underwrite capex across TSMC, Foxconn and the Taiwan packaging and test chain over the next few quarters, even as the market digests Nvidia's CEO transition.

**分析师称英伟达扩大供应链承诺提振台湾厂商信心，供应承诺总额达3,900亿美元**



中央社2026年11月22日报道，台经院分析师刘佩真表示，英伟达扩大对台积电、鸿海、日月光投控旗下矽品、欣兴、京元电、景硕等伙伴的长期承诺，正在稳定CEO交接后的市场信心。随着Vera Rubin NVL72开始量产出货，英伟达整体供应承诺已升至3,900亿美元，台湾供应商未来数季的订单能见度较高。



关键在于订单能见度：3,900亿美元的供应承诺，为台积电、鸿海以及台湾封装测试产业链未来数季的资本开支提供了支撑，即便市场仍在消化英伟达的CEO交接。
