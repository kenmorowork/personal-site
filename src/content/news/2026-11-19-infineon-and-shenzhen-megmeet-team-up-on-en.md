Megmeet will use Infineon's CoolSiC MOSFET G2 silicon-carbide devices in its three-phase AI power shelf; the collaboration also covers server power supply units (PSUs), intermediate bus converters (IBCs) and solid-state transformers (SSTs), building on the pair's earlier single-phase server power work.

**Why it matters:** 800VDC gains another named pairing — Infineon SiC devices inside Megmeet's three-phase shelf, extended across PSUs, IBCs and SSTs — as the power architecture for AI racks consolidates around a few supplier stacks.
