Rapidus reaffirmed on 14 Nov that it is targeting commercial 2nm wafer output by early 2028, after difficulties validating SRAM bitcells at its Chitose pilot line through the summer of 2026 slipped internal targets (East Asia Brief, 15 Nov 2026). METI convenes its next semiconductor project review in December 2026 to evaluate a planned ¥400bn equity injection. TSMC prices 2nm at $30,000 per 300mm wafer, 50% above the $20,000 3nm baseline, held a record 72.5% foundry revenue share in Q2 2026, and plans first commercial 2nm deliveries in H1 2027; Apple has reserved roughly half of initial 2nm runs.

**Why it matters:** This morning's price story (Rapidus about 30% cheaper) meets the schedule story: a cheaper wafer that arrives a year later does not contest TSMC's 2027 AI orders — the December METI review is the next dated checkpoint.

**Rapidus 2纳米再遇阻：SRAM位元验证延迟、量产推至2028年初，台积电2纳米晶圆守住3万美元**



Rapidus于11月14日重申目标2028年初实现2纳米晶圆商业化生产；其千岁试点线在2026年夏季验证SRAM位元时遇到困难，内部进度落后（East Asia Brief，2026年11月15日）。经济产业省将于2026年12月召开下一次半导体项目审查会，评估计划中的4,000亿日元注资。台积电2纳米定价为每片3万美元（300mm），较3纳米的2万美元高50%，2026年二季度代工收入份额达创纪录的72.5%，计划2027年上半年开始2纳米商业交付；苹果已预订约一半的初期2纳米产能。



今晨的价格故事（Rapidus便宜约三成）遇上进度故事：晚一年到货的便宜晶圆无法争夺台积电2027年的AI订单——12月的经产省审查是下一个有日期的节点。
