Korea's industry ministry unveiled an ₩800.2bn K-On-Device AI Semiconductor roadmap on 12 Nov: 10 chip and IP types are to be developed and verified by 2030 across autonomous vehicles (1), smart appliances (3), and collaborative, humanoid and farm robots and drones (1 each) (Press9, 13 Nov). Samsung Foundry will act as the core manufacturing partner, pre-surveying fabless demand and prioritising 21 MPW and single-run slots in 2027 — 2nm ×3, 4nm ×4, 5nm ×3, 8nm ×3, 14nm ×3 and 28nm ×5.

**Why it matters:** Seoul is funding the edge-AI layer — chips for robots, cars and appliances — with Samsung Foundry capacity reserved in advance; it is the demand counterpart to yesterday's Samsung HBM packaging pull-forward.
