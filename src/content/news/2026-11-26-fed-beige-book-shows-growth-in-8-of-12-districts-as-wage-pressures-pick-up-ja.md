The Federal Reserve's latest Beige Book found economic activity growing at a slight-to-moderate pace in 8 of its 12 districts, down from 9 in October, while four districts reported no change. Prices increased moderately overall, as in the prior period, and input costs from energy, transport, healthcare, insurance and tariffs outpaced selling-price increases. Employment rose slightly, mostly through replacement hiring, and wages and benefits rose moderately, up from modest increases in October. Consumer spending was little changed as households remained price sensitive.

**Why it matters:** The pressure point in this Beige Book is wages rather than goods prices, two weeks before the December FOMC meeting.

**美联储褐皮书显示12个地区中有8个增长，工资压力有所上升**

美联储最新褐皮书显示，12个地区中有8个经济活动以轻微至温和的速度增长，低于10月的9个，另有4个地区无变化。价格总体温和上涨，能源、运输、医疗、保险和关税相关的投入成本涨幅超过销售价格。就业小幅上升，主要为替代性招聘，工资和福利温和上涨，涨幅高于10月。消费者支出基本不变，家庭对价格仍然敏感。
