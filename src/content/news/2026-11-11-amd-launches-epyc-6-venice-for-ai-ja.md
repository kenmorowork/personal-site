AMD debuted its Epyc 6 server CPU line on 11 Nov: ten Zen 6 / Zen 6c parts, up to 256 cores and 512 threads, with the highest-frequency models carrying 96 cores and boosting to 5.3GHz. AMD says Zen 6 IPC is 31% higher than its previous design on AI workloads and that the cores are built on TSMC's 2nm node. AMD claims the chips can support up to 3.3 times more AI agents per watt than Nvidia's Vera CPU — a company claim, not independently verified. Major server suppliers have started shipping Epyc 6 systems, and the chips power two Microsoft Azure instances. Source: AMD announcement via SiliconANGLE, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The CPU is becoming contested ground inside AI racks: AMD is selling agent density per watt directly against Nvidia's Vera, with TSMC 2nm as the shared bottleneck.

**AMD发布面向AI智能体的EPYC 6「Venice」——Zen 6采用台积电2nm，称每瓦智能体数量为英伟达Vera的3.3倍**



AMD于11月11日发布EPYC 6服务器CPU系列：共十款Zen 6 / Zen 6c产品，最高256核、512线程，高频型号为96核、加速频率5.3GHz。AMD称Zen 6在AI工作负载上的IPC比上一代高31%，核心采用台积电2nm制程。AMD宣称其芯片每瓦可支持的AI智能体数量最高为英伟达Vera CPU的3.3倍——此为公司宣称，未经独立验证。主要服务器厂商已开始出货EPYC 6系统，该芯片亦用于两个Microsoft Azure实例。来源：AMD发布（SiliconANGLE），2026年11月11日。



CPU正在成为AI机架内的争夺地：AMD以每瓦智能体密度直接对标英伟达Vera，而台积电2nm是双方共同的瓶颈。
