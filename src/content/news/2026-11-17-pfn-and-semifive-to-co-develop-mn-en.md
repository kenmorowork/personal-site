Preferred Networks (PFN) and South Korea's SEMIFIVE announced on 17 Nov they will co-develop the MN-Core L2000 series, PFN's next-generation AI inference accelerator, targeting more than twice the memory bandwidth of the L1000 series (whose sales PFN plans to start in 2027) using 3D stacking. Tape-out is planned for Q1 2027 (Jan–Mar); SEMIFIVE provides 3D-IC design implementation and supply-chain management as a Samsung Foundry partner. The two will exhibit jointly at SC26 in Chicago, 17–19 Nov (booth 4606). Source: PFN / SEMIFIVE press release, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A dated Japan-designed inference-chip programme with a Samsung Foundry route: Q1 2027 tape-out is the checkpoint that separates a roadmap from silicon.
