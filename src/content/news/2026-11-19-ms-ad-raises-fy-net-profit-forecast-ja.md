MS&AD lifted its FY Mar-2027 net profit forecast from ¥425bn to ¥490bn and announced a buyback of up to 52m shares (3.7% of shares ex-treasury) or ¥135bn; insurers were among the only risers in the morning sell-off, and Tokio Marine separately set a ¥280bn buyback cap (Nikkei).

**Why it matters:** A forecast raise plus a ¥135bn buyback, with Tokio Marine's ¥280bn cap alongside, made insurers the defensive pocket of a −4.49% morning — capital return, not earnings momentum, is the story.

Source: Minkabu (19 Nov evening disclosure) · 20 Nov 2026, 10:11 JST — [original article](https://minkabu.jp/)

**MS&AD上调全年净利润预期至4,900亿日元并推出1,350亿日元回购**



MS&AD将2027年3月期净利润预期从4,250亿日元上调至4,900亿日元，并宣布最高5200万股（占已发行股本3.7%）或1,350亿日元的回购；保险股是午间暴跌中少数上涨的板块之一，东京海上另行设定了2,800亿日元的回购上限（日经）。



上调预期加上1,350亿日元回购、东京海上另设2,800亿日元上限，使保险股成为日经大跌4.49%的早盘中少数防御板块——看点在资本回报而非盈利动能。
