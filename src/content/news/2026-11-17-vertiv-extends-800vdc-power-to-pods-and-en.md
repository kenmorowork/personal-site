Vertiv on 17 Nov added the PowerDirect 9000, a 1.5MW 800VDC power centre converting 480VAC for a full row or pod and scaling in 60kW increments, and the PowerNexus MV SST, a 5MW solid-state transformer converting medium-voltage AC directly to 800VDC in one stage at up to 98% efficiency. The portfolio now spans rack, pod and data-hall conversion points and is being showcased at SC26 (17-22 Nov); the release cites pilot and validation programmes but no customer orders or revenue. Source: PR Newswire / Vertiv, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** 800VDC is moving from rack-level announcements to pod and data-hall products with named ratings — a 1.5MW centre and a 5MW SST at up to 98% efficiency — so the power-conversion layer of the AI build-out is getting productised, though Vertiv cites pilots, not orders.
