Infineon will supply Enphase Energy with what it calls the world's first 900V GaN monolithic bidirectional switch for Enphase IQ solid-state transformer power modules for AI data centres (eetrend, 16 Nov 2026). The device extends the working voltage range to 900V while keeping GaN high-frequency switching; testing is complete and integration into IQ SST development is under way.

**Why it matters:** A named supply deal moves the 900V GaN solid-state transformer from device announcement to integration: Enphase's IQ SST programme is now the dated checkpoint.

**英飞凌向Enphase供应全球首款面向AI数据中心固态变压器的900V GaN双向开关器件**



英飞凌将向Enphase Energy供应其所称的全球首款900V GaN单片双向开关器件，用于Enphase IQ固态变压器电源模块（电子创新网，2026年11月16日）。该器件将工作电压范围扩展至900V，同时保持GaN高频开关特性；测试已完成，正集成至IQ固态变压器的开发中。



具名供货协议使900V GaN固态变压器从器件发布走向集成阶段：Enphase的IQ固态变压器项目成为下一个有日期的检验点。
