Fujitsu chief technology officer Vivek Mahajan said initial demand for the Monaka 2-nanometre server CPU, which began commercial sales this month, is exceeding expectations, and the company is in talks with TSMC about securing production capacity. Most of the 45 companies that received samples in August are likely to place orders, against a current plan of about 500,000 units over two years.

**Why it matters:** ¥35bn of expected sales next year against a ¥500bn annual target for 2035 — capacity talks with TSMC have begun before the first full year of sales.

**富士通称Monaka芯片需求超预期，预计下一财年销售额350亿日元，2035年目标为每年5000亿日元**

富士通表示，其2纳米服务器CPU Monaka本月开始商业销售后，初期需求超出预期，8月收到样品的45家公司中大多数可能下单。公司预计下一财年Monaka销售额为350亿日元，并力争到2035年达到每年5000亿日元。
