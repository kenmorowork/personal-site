The coupon landed slightly above the midpoint of the tentative 1.700-2.700% range. On ¥1m held for three years, the after-tax interest difference against the retail JGB is about ¥7,700. The bond pays interest each June 9 and December 9, matures on December 7, 2029, and was reported sold out at SBI Securities on the morning of November 26, the first subscription day.

**Why it matters:** The gap to the retail JGB is only 0.326 points, yet the issue sold out on day one at one broker, so demand is the story.
