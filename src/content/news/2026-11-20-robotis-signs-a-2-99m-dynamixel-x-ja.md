ROBOTIS disclosed on 20 Nov a ₩3.98bn (about $2.99m) supply agreement for DYNAMIXEL-X components with open-source humanoid developer Pollen Robotics, equal to 10.22% of ROBOTIS's consolidated 2025 revenue; signed 19 Nov, deliveries to France run through 18 April 2027, with 50% paid upfront. Source: ROBOTIS regulatory filing via Robotico News, Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** At 10.22% of ROBOTIS's consolidated 2025 revenue, a single actuator contract is material: 50% paid upfront and deliveries to France through 18 April 2027 give the DYNAMIXEL-X line revenue visibility into the open-source humanoid build-out.

Source: Robotico News (ROBOTIS disclosure) · 20 Nov 2026, reported 21–22 Nov 2026 — [original article](https://robotico.market/news/robotis-signs-2-99m-dynamixel-x-supply-contract-with-pollen-robotics)

**ROBOTIS与法国Pollen Robotics签下299万美元DYNAMIXEL-X执行器供应合同**



ROBOTIS于11月20日披露，与开源人形机器人厂商Pollen Robotics签订39.8亿韩元（约299万美元）的DYNAMIXEL-X部件供应协议，相当于ROBOTIS 2025年合并营收的10.22%；合同于11月19日签署，向法国交付持续至2027年4月18日，付款条件为50%预付。来源：ROBOTIS监管披露，经Robotico News报道，2026年11月。



单笔执行器合同相当于ROBOTIS 2025年合并营收的10.22%，规模已具实质意义：50%预付、向法国交付持续至2027年4月18日，为DYNAMIXEL-X产品线在开源人形机器人扩张中提供了收入能见度。
