SoftBank Group's H1 (Apr–Sep) net loss was ¥768.0bn, vs a ¥2,924.1bn profit a year earlier; sales rose 13.3% to ¥4.235tn. Q2 alone lost ¥1,115.3bn — its first quarterly loss in about two years. The driver was a ¥597.2bn loss from revising power-generation equipment procurement for US AI data-centre projects (incl. Ohio), plus a ¥270.1bn loss linked to Intel shares and derivatives. Figures: SoftBank Group H1 results via Nikkei Asia, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The writedown sits in power procurement, not demand — AIDC returns hinge on power execution, echoing the transformer bottleneck.
