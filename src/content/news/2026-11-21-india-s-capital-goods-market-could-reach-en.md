A CII-BCG report released 21 Nov at the CII Capital Goods Summit projects India's capital goods market at $1-1.2 trillion by 2047; annual capex in electronics, semiconductors and data centres is projected to rise nearly fourfold, from Rs 30,000 crore in FY23-25 to Rs 1.2 lakh crore in FY27-29, driving demand for transformers, switchgear and cooling, while imports still supply about 36% of domestic demand.

**Why it matters:** A near-fourfold rise in annual electronics, semiconductor and data-centre capex by FY27-29 is the demand line for transformers, switchgear and cooling in India; imports at about 36% of domestic demand mark the local-content opportunity.
