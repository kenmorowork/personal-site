Infineon now expects data-centre revenue of close to €4bn ($4.49bn) in the year to Sep 2027, up from prior guidance of ~€2.5bn, and total FY27 revenue of ~€20bn vs €16.35bn in FY26. Jul–Sep quarter revenue rose 19% y/y to €4.71bn, in line with Vara consensus. Figures: Infineon results via Dow Jones, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Read-across: power semis are raising numbers while GPU names derate — the bottleneck (and pricing) sits in power delivery, supportive for transformer / power-equipment names.
