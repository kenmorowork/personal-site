Samsung Electronics promoted three Device Solutions executives to president on 20 November: Hwang Sang-jun (Memory), Cho Sang-yeun (System LSI) and Lee Jong-myeong (DS CTO and head of the Semiconductor R&D Center), while co-CEOs Jun and Roh step back from concurrent business-head roles. The DS division posted a record operating profit of 106.5 trillion won in Q3 (Newspim, 2026-11-20).

**Why it matters:** Putting the HBM architect in charge of Memory, with System LSI and the DS CTO also changing hands, signals Samsung is organising its chip leadership around memory execution after the DS division's record 106.5 trillion won operating profit in Q3.

**三星任命HBM负责人黄相准为社长兼存储业务负责人，半导体部门主导高层人事调整**



三星电子11月20日将三名半导体（DS）部门高管晋升为社长：黄相准任存储业务负责人，赵尚延任系统LSI负责人，李钟明任DS部门首席技术官兼半导体研究所所长；两位联席CEO不再兼任业务负责人。DS部门第三季度营业利润达106.5万亿韩元，创历史新高（Newspim，2026-11-20）。



让HBM负责人执掌存储业务、系统LSI与DS首席技术官同步换人，显示三星在DS部门第三季度营业利润创106.5万亿韩元纪录之后，正围绕存储执行力重组半导体领导层。
