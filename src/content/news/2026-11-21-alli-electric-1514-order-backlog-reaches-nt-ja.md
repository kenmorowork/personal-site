At its 20 Nov investor conference, Alli Electric said its order backlog has reached NT$13 billion, helped by AI data-centre builds, semiconductor fab expansions at home and overseas, and Taipower's grid-resilience programme; AI/IDC power-equipment orders of about NT$1.5 billion have been booked this year versus roughly NT$0.5-0.6 billion a year previously, and its second transformer plant lifts transformer capacity to NT$1.5 billion.

**Why it matters:** A NT$13bn backlog, with AI/IDC orders of about NT$1.5bn this year against NT$0.5–0.6bn a year previously, gives Alli multi-year visibility; the second transformer plant lifting capacity to NT$1.5bn is the supply side of the same story.

**亚力（1514）在手订单达130亿新台币，受惠AI数据中心与半导体扩厂**



亚力11月20日法说会表示，在手订单达130亿新台币，AI数据中心与半导体扩厂带动电力设备需求；今年AI及IDC订单约15亿新台币，远高于以往每年约5亿至6亿新台币。



在手订单达130亿新台币、今年AI及IDC订单约15亿新台币（以往每年仅约5亿至6亿新台币），使亚力获得多年期的订单能见度；第二座变压器厂将产能提升至15亿新台币，是同一故事的供给侧。
