Nvidia said licensed Hopper data-centre sales to China were $2.4bn in Q3, but it is no longer shipping those products and its Q4 guidance includes none. Washington still bans Blackwell and Rubin exports to China.

**Why it matters:** Licensed Hopper was the last legal data-centre channel into China; Nvidia confirming shipments have stopped — and that the Q4 guide carries none of it — closes that revenue line and leaves the China question resting on Blackwell and Rubin policy, not on Hopper.

**英伟达称已停止向中国出货获许可的Hopper数据中心产品**



英伟达表示，第三季度获许可的Hopper数据中心产品对华销售额为24亿美元，但公司已停止出货，且第四季度指引未包含此类销售。美国仍禁止Blackwell与Rubin对华出口。



获许可的Hopper曾是对华数据中心销售的最后合法通道；英伟达确认已停止出货、且第四季度指引不含此类销售，意味着这条收入线关闭，中国问题回到Blackwell与Rubin的政策本身。
