Hitachi, Ltd. (6501) and Bechtel announced an enterprise agreement for earlier joint planning, engineering and procurement of distribution and power equipment for Bechtel's US projects, aimed at long lead times for power equipment; no order value was disclosed (Bechtel, 19 Nov 2026).

**Why it matters:** Another framework aimed squarely at power-equipment lead times: for Hitachi (6501) the value is in earlier visibility over Bechtel's US project pipeline rather than a single booked order.

**日立与Bechtel签署企业协议，加快美国电力基础设施交付**



日立制作所（6501）与Bechtel宣布签署一项企业协议，就Bechtel美国项目的配电与电力设备开展更早期的联合规划、工程与采购，旨在应对电力设备交期漫长的问题；公告未披露订单金额（Bechtel，2026年11月19日）。



又一项直指电力设备交期的框架协议：对日立（6501）而言，价值在于更早掌握Bechtel美国项目管线的可见度，而非单笔已确认订单。
