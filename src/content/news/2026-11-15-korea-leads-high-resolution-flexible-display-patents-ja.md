Korea filed the world's most high-resolution flexible-display patent applications over 2015–2024, with 4,207 filings ahead of China's 3,901, Japan's 1,089 and the US's 876, according to the Korean Intellectual Property Office's November report on global technology trends in national strategic technologies, released 15 Nov (via BigGo). By filer, Samsung Display leads with 2,070, followed by BOE (1,023), LG Display (851), TCL (796) and Samsung Electronics (597).

**Why it matters:** A decade of patent stock is the quiet side of the display competition tracked here in electronic components — Korea's lead is concentrated in one filer, Samsung Display.

**韩国以4,207件高分辨率柔性显示专利居全球首位——三星显示独占2,070件**



韩国知识产权局11月15日发布的《国家战略技术全球技术动向与专利》显示，2015–2024年韩国高分辨率柔性显示专利申请达4,207件、居全球首位，其后为中国3,901件、日本1,089件、美国876件（经BigGo报道）。按申请人计，三星显示以2,070件居首，其后为京东方1,023件、LG显示851件、TCL 796件、三星电子597件。



十年专利存量是本看板在电子零部件领域追踪的显示竞争的另一面——韩国的领先高度集中于三星显示一家。
