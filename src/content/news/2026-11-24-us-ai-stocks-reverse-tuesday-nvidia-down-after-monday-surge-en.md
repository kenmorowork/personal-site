Micron and AMD fell roughly 8%, Intel, Marvell and SK Hynix each dropped more than 5%, and the SOXX semiconductor ETF fell more than 6% in early trading on 24 Nov (Investopedia). Dell fell 9% ahead of its earnings after the close, and Nvidia remained more than 30% below its 6 October record close despite adding more than $900bn of market value on Monday.

**Why it matters:** The round trip after Monday's surge shows positioning, not fundamentals, driving the tape into Dell's earnings after the close.
