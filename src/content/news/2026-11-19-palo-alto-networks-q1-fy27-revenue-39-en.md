For the quarter ended 31 Oct 2026, Palo Alto Networks reported revenue of $3.44bn, up 39% year on year, Next-Generation Security ARR of $9.73bn, up 66%, and RPO of $21.0bn, up 35% (company release via PR Newswire, 18 Nov 16:05 ET). Non-GAAP EPS was $1.05 against guidance of $0.96–0.98, while GAAP operating income fell to $96m from $309m; the company raised FY27 guidance to revenue of $14.52–14.57bn and non-GAAP EPS of $4.26–4.30, and announced its intent to acquire Applied Compute.

**Why it matters:** AI security spend is still accelerating — NGS ARR growth of 66% with a guidance raise — but the GAAP profit drop shows acquisition and stock-compensation costs are the offset to watch.
