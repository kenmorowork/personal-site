DIGITIMES reported on 13 Nov that TSMC is in talks over Elon Musk's Texas 'Terafab' project but regards control of fab operations as non-negotiable, as Musk's do-it-alone plan stalls. TSMC is also advancing its CoPoS panel-level packaging work at the Taoyuan Longtan site. (Headline-level report; financial terms not disclosed.)

**Why it matters:** Control, not capacity, is TSMC's stated red line on Terafab — the same project Japanese equipment makers were asked to supply on a 'within months' timetable — while CoPoS progress keeps panel-level packaging on the roadmap.
