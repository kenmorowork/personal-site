US initial jobless claims fell 13,000 to 189,000 in the week ended 21 November, below forecasts of about 200,000 and down from a revised 202,000 the previous week. The four-week average dipped to 196,000 and continuing claims fell to 1.697 million. Durable goods orders rose 0.2% month on month in October to $346.7bn, below the 0.6% forecast, after an upwardly revised 2.0% rise in September; orders excluding transport rose 0.5%.

**Why it matters:** Claims at 189,000 say layoffs are not rising, while the orders miss sits in the headline rather than the core capital-goods line.
