US retail and food services sales were $748.2bn in October 2026, up 0.2% from September (±0.4pp, so the 90% confidence interval includes zero) and up 6.8% year on year, the Census Bureau's advance estimate on 17 Nov showed; September's +0.8% was unrevised. Motor vehicle and parts dealers fell 1.6%, so sales excluding autos rose 0.6%, and core sales excluding autos, gasoline, building materials and food services also rose 0.6%; furniture fell 2.3% and clothing 0.7%. The November advance estimate is due 16 Dec. Source: US Census Bureau, Advance Monthly Retail Trade Survey, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The headline +0.2% is statistically indistinguishable from zero, but ex-autos and core sales both rose 0.6% — the US consumer slowed in October without stalling, with autos doing most of the drag.

Source: US Census Bureau · 17 Nov 2026, 08:30 ET — [original article](https://www.census.gov/retail/marts/www/marts_current.pdf?os)

**美国10月零售销售仅增0.2%至7,482亿美元；剔除汽车后增0.6%**



美国人口普查局11月17日公布的预估显示，2026年10月零售与餐饮销售为7,482亿美元，环比增0.2%（误差±0.4个百分点，90%置信区间包含零），同比增6.8%；9月的+0.8%未作修正。汽车及零部件经销商下降1.6%，剔除汽车后销售增0.6%，剔除汽车、汽油、建材与餐饮的核心销售同样增0.6%；家具降2.3%，服装降0.7%。11月预估将于12月16日公布。来源：美国人口普查局，2026年11月17日。



标题增幅+0.2%在统计上与零无异，但剔除汽车与核心销售均增0.6%——美国消费者10月放缓但未停滞，拖累主要来自汽车。
