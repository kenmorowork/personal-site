Second report dated 20 Nov (TDnet): the intrusion is estimated at about 01:30 on 15 Nov, presumed via a VPN-device authentication breach involving an account without multi-factor authentication. Production-related systems remain stopped while recovery proceeds; backups are confirmed intact and a phased restart will follow safety checks. Restart timing and the earnings impact are still undetermined, and a possible leak of technical information is under investigation (first report 18 Nov).

**Why it matters:** Production and shipments remain halted with restart timing undetermined, so the smart-meter supply impact is still open; the entry route — a VPN account without multi-factor authentication — is the detail utilities and vendors will be asked about.

**大崎电气（6644）勒索软件第二报：智能电表生产与出货仍停滞，重启时间未定**



11月20日第二份报告（TDnet）：入侵估计发生在11月15日01:30左右，推定经由未设多因素认证的账户突破VPN设备认证。生产相关系统仍处于停止状态，恢复工作进行中；备份已确认完好，安全检查后将分阶段重启。重启时间与业绩影响仍未确定，技术信息可能外泄一事正在调查（第一报为11月18日）。



生产与出货仍停滞、重启时间未定，智能电表供应影响仍未明朗；入侵路径——未设多因素认证的VPN账户——是公用事业公司与供应商将被追问的细节。
