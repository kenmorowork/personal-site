TOPIX fell 148.25 points to 3,674.22 as the US AI sell-off spilled into Tokyo; AI and semiconductor names led a near-universal decline, with decliners outnumbering advancers by more than 90% of TSE Prime issues. The Nikkei closed at the day's low.

**Why it matters:** A −5.06% close at the day's low, with more than 90% of Prime issues down, turns the morning's chip-led sell-off into a broad market break — the fifth-largest one-day drop on record frames the risk into the US session and Monday's open.

**东京收盘：日经指数暴跌5.06%（下跌3,007.56点）至56,436.65点，为史上第五大单日跌幅，七个月来首次收于57,000点下方**



东证股价指数下跌148.25点至3,674.22点，美国AI股抛售蔓延至东京市场；AI与半导体股领跌，东证Prime市场下跌家数占比超过九成。日经指数以全天最低点收盘。



日经以全天最低点收跌5.06%、Prime市场逾九成个股下跌，使早盘由芯片股主导的抛售演变为全面的市场下挫——史上第五大单日跌幅为美股时段与周一开盘定下了风险基调。
