At a White House news conference on Friday, President Trump said the US would not allow its companies to build data centres outside the country and would impose a 'tremendous tax' on those that do (Reuters, 20 Nov). No rate, mechanism or legislation was specified — this is a statement / pledge only. Morningstar / Dow Jones noted Alphabet's $15bn Malaysia data-centre plan for 2027–28 among the overseas projects potentially exposed.

**Why it matters:** A threatened 'tremendous tax' — a statement only, with no rate or mechanism — puts hyperscalers' overseas data-centre plans under a policy overhang; Alphabet's $15bn Malaysia build for 2027–28 is the named exposure.

**特朗普威胁对在海外建设数据中心的美国企业征收“巨额税”**



特朗普总统周五在白宫记者会上表示，美国不会允许本国企业在国外建设数据中心，并将对这样做的企业征收“巨额税”（路透社，11月20日）。税率、机制与立法均未说明，现阶段仅为表态。Morningstar／道琼斯指出，Alphabet计划于2027–28年在马来西亚投资150亿美元建设数据中心，可能受到影响。



“巨额税”的威胁目前仅为表态——无税率、无机制——使大型云厂商的海外数据中心计划面临政策不确定性；Alphabet 2027–28年150亿美元的马来西亚项目是点名的敞口。
