Anthropic (ANT) closed at $88.30 on Friday, down 9.13% (Finnhub, 20 Nov), after trading as low as $83.05 — essentially at its $83 IPO price — versus Thursday's $97.17 close. Anthropic said on Friday that the underwriter tasked with stabilising the shares had closed the position meant to do so (full greenshoe exercised at the IPO price), removing one source of buying support (Reuters).

**Why it matters:** With the stabilisation position closed, ANT trades without its IPO safety net: holding the $83 issue price is now the test, two sessions after a debut that closed +17%.
