Fisco's outlook (Zaikei, 14 Nov) notes the Nikkei closed the week at 61,142.35, up ¥3,707.58 (+6.5%), as the post-election AI-regulation selling faded and CoreWeave's results lifted AI names, while Nvidia — down about 13% on the week despite a weekend close about 9% above its 12 Nov low — reports on 17 Nov and Anthropic's IPO follows on 19 Nov. Japan's data week: Q3 GDP on 16 Nov, October trade and visitor arrivals on 18 Nov, September machinery orders on 19 Nov, and October CPI on 20 Nov.

**Why it matters:** Next week compresses the two questions hanging over Ken's coverage into five days: whether Nvidia's first post-Huang report validates AI capex, and whether Japan's machinery orders confirm the order cycle that THK, SMC and Takasago revisions pointed to this week.
