The Japanese heavy-electrical trio's H1 results, out by 13 November, showed transmission and distribution gear, UPS, FA equipment and GTCC turbine enquiries strong on data-centre demand. Hitachi and Mitsubishi Electric raised their FY Mar-2027 guidance, while the report flags US midterm and Nvidia leadership uncertainty (NewsPicks / NewsSwitch via Yahoo News Japan).

**Why it matters:** Hitachi Energy revenue up 32.0% to ¥1.8723tn and Toshiba operating profit doubling to ¥234.7bn confirm the grid-equipment cycle is broadening across all three Japanese majors, with two of the three raising full-year guidance.

**日立、三菱电机与东芝2026年4–9月均实现营收与营业利润增长，受数据中心需求拉动；日立能源营收增长32.0%至1.8723万亿日元，东芝营业利润翻倍至2,347亿日元**



日本重电三巨头上半年业绩（11月13日前已公布）显示，受数据中心需求拉动，输配电设备、UPS、工厂自动化设备与GTCC燃气轮机问询强劲。日立与三菱电机上调了2027年3月期指引；报道同时提示美国中期选举与英伟达领导层的不确定性（NewsPicks/NewsSwitch，经雅虎新闻日本）。



日立能源营收增长32.0%至1.8723万亿日元、东芝营业利润翻倍至2,347亿日元，印证电网设备景气正扩散至日本三大重电厂商，且其中两家已上调全年指引。
