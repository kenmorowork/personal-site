Walmart US comparable sales rose 2.9% excluding fuel, US eCommerce grew 23% with marketplace sales up nearly 50%, and Walmart Connect advertising grew 36% excluding VIZIO and Vibe (Walmart release, 19 Nov). Operating cash flow was $29.4bn and free cash flow $6.8bn; the shares were up 0.90% premarket at $128.50 (Finnhub, 07:08 ET).

**Why it matters:** A constant-currency sales beat against the guide, with eCommerce up 23% and advertising compounding, is a clean read on the US consumer heading into the holiday quarter.
