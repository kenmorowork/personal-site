A Nikkei analysis of 1,028 Prime-listed companies put combined April-September net profit at ¥35.75tn ($225bn), up 26% year on year and a record for a sixth straight interim half. Manufacturing profit rose 73%, electrical equipment profit rose 96% with a ¥3.28tn increase, Kioxia posted ¥2.19tn (37 times the year-earlier figure) and Fujikura nearly tripled to ¥180bn.

**Why it matters:** AI-linked demand and the soft yen lifted the whole manufacturing complex in the first half, even as the July-September quarter alone fell 3%.
