Chinese technology outlet Kuai Keji, via InvestingLive on 18 Nov, reported that TSMC has begun setting per-customer credit limits based on balance sheets, payment history and default risk, requiring orders above the limit to be paid in cash and some custom AI chip customers to prepay to secure capacity, and has sent staff to the US to check data-centre construction. TSMC has not announced any such policy and the outlet said it could not confirm it independently; TSMC already held about NT$234bn of customer prepayments at end-June, described as standard accounting prepayments. Treat as an unconfirmed report. Source: Kuai Keji via InvestingLive, 18 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Unconfirmed, but if TSMC is rationing credit and demanding prepayment from AI chip clients, it would be the first sign the foundry itself is pricing circular-financing risk — one to track for confirmation before treating it as fact.

**据报道台积电对部分AI芯片客户设定信用额度并要求预付现金，源于对循环融资的担忧——该报道未经证实**



中国科技媒体快科技经InvestingLive于11月18日报道称，台积电已开始根据资产负债表、付款记录与违约风险为每位客户设定信用额度，超额订单须以现金支付，部分定制AI芯片客户须预付以锁定产能，并已派员赴美核查数据中心建设。台积电尚未宣布任何此类政策，该媒体称无法独立证实；台积电截至6月底已有约2,340亿新台币客户预付款，被称为标准会计预收款。此为未经证实的报道。来源：快科技（经InvestingLive），2026年11月18日。



虽未经证实，但若台积电确在限制信用并要求AI芯片客户预付，将是代工厂自身开始为循环融资风险定价的首个信号——在证实之前应持续跟踪，不宜当作事实。
