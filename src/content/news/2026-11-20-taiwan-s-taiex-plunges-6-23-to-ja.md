The Taiex closed on Friday 20 Nov down 2,393.60 points (−6.23%) at 36,033.29, its third-largest single-day point drop on record, led by TSMC and AI suppliers (Digitimes). TSMC closed at NT$2,000, down NT$100 (−4.76%) on the day (ETtoday cross-check).

**Why it matters:** The Taiex's third-largest point drop on record, led by TSMC, puts Taiwan at the centre of the AI supply-chain sell-off — the region where the chips are made is now where the drawdown is deepest.

**台湾加权指数重挫6.23%至36,033.29点，为史上第三大单日跌点，台积电下跌4.76%至2,000新台币**



台湾加权指数11月20日周五收盘下跌2,393.60点（−6.23%）至36,033.29点，为史上第三大单日跌点，由台积电与AI供应链领跌（Digitimes）。台积电收于2,000新台币，单日下跌100新台币（−4.76%）（ETtoday交叉核对）。



台股录得史上第三大单日跌点、由台积电领跌，使台湾成为AI供应链抛售的中心——芯片生产之地如今也是跌幅最深之地。
