Ming-Chi Kuo's latest survey puts OpenAI at about 12 million TPU 8t chips (MediaTek-designed, bought outright) and Anthropic at about 14 million TPU 8i chips (Broadcom-designed, lease/purchase mix) across 2028–29, against his total TPU shipment estimates of 14–15 million in 2028 and 16–18 million in 2029. Visibility now extends to 2029, ahead of Nvidia GPUs and other ASICs (mostly 4Q27–2Q28); MediaTek becomes sole design supplier from v9, with its supply share rising to 55–60% by 2029. All figures are Kuo's estimates, not company disclosures.

**Why it matters:** Custom-silicon visibility to 2029 lengthens the TPU runway against Nvidia: MediaTek's sole-supplier step-up from v9 is the dated share shift to track.

**郭明錤：OpenAI与Anthropic将在2028–29年采购约2600万颗Google TPU，约占其出货预估的八成**



郭明錤最新产业调查显示，OpenAI将采购约1200万颗TPU 8t（联发科设计），Anthropic将以租赁与采购混合方式取得约1400万颗TPU 8i（博通设计），两者合计约占其2028年1400–1500万颗、2029年1600–1800万颗TPU出货预估的约八成。出货能见度已延伸至2029年，长于英伟达GPU及其他ASIC；联发科自v9起成为独家设计供应商。以上均为郭明錤的预估，并非公司披露。



定制芯片能见度延伸至2029年，TPU对英伟达的竞争窗口拉长：联发科自v9起独家供应是值得跟踪的份额变化节点。
