After more than 90 minutes of talks, the joint statement said the three countries will increase oil production and supply and will hold talks on reducing nuclear risk and preventing proliferation (Xinhua via Nikkei Asia, 19 Nov). The earlier Xi-Trump bilateral readout made no mention of extending the trade truce, which expires on 10 Jan 2027.

**Why it matters:** Energy supply and nuclear-risk language gives the trilateral a concrete deliverable, but with no Ukraine breakthrough and no trade-truce extension mentioned, the policy overhang for chips and energy stays in place.
