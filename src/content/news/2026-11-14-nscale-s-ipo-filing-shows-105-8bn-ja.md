UK AI cloud builder Nscale's IPO filing, summarised in Sina Finance's WhiteLine Daily (14 Nov 2026), shows about 50,000 GPUs operating — double the roughly 25,000 at end-August — against operating-plus-contracted capacity of about 461,000 GPUs (about 1.37GW), with take-or-pay contracts totalling $105.8bn. The same daily confirms Applied Materials' record Q4 revenue of $10.41bn, up 53% y/y, already carried on this board on 13 Nov.

**Why it matters:** The neocloud question in one ratio: contracted GPUs are about nine times operating GPUs, so delivery pace — power, buildings, chips — is the whole investment case, as with CoreWeave and Nebius on this board.

**Nscale的IPO文件显示照付不议合同总额1,058亿美元——而实际运营的GPU只有约5万块**



据新浪财经《白线日报》（2026年11月14日）对英国AI云厂商Nscale的IPO文件的梳理，该公司实际运营的GPU约5万块，较8月底的约2.5万块翻倍；而运营加已签约容量约为46.1万块GPU（约1.37GW），照付不议合同总额达1,058亿美元。同一日报确认应用材料第四季度营收创纪录达104.1亿美元、同比增长53%，该消息已于11月13日载于本看板。



新云厂商的问题浓缩为一个比率：已签约GPU约为实际运营的九倍，因此交付速度——电力、厂房、芯片——就是全部投资逻辑，与本看板的CoreWeave和Nebius相同。
