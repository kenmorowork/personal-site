At SC26 (15-20 Nov 2026, Chicago), Fujitsu showed production-intent MONAKA hardware: 144 Armv9.3-A cores on TSMC's 2nm N2P process in a 3D chiplet SiP using TSMC SoIC hybrid copper bonding, with dual 256-bit SVE2 pipelines and FP8/INT8 support, positioned for sovereign AI and HPC centres facing power constraints.

**Why it matters:** Production-intent hardware at SC26 moves MONAKA from roadmap to shippable system: a 144-core, 2nm Arm CPU in a 3D chiplet package is Fujitsu's bid for sovereign AI and HPC demand where power, not compute, is the binding constraint.
