Asian markets surged on Tuesday 24 November after Nvidia rose 31.5% in the US, restoring nearly $1 trillion in market value. Samsung, SK hynix and TSMC each rose more than 8%, Japan's Advantest rose more than 14%, and the MSCI Asia-Pacific index outside Japan climbed 4.6%. (Moneycontrol, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** The rebound shows how tightly Asian chip supply-chain valuations still move with Nvidia's leadership and earnings outlook.

**亚洲AI股票强劲反弹：韩国综合指数涨7.5%，日经涨5%，台湾股市涨9.1%**

11月24日，受英伟达在美国大涨31.5%、市值恢复近1万亿美元带动，亚洲股市普遍上涨。三星、SK海力士和台积电均涨逾8%，日本爱德万测试涨逾14%。
