At 05:08 ET, S&P 500 futures fell 0.36% and Nasdaq 100 futures lost 0.97% (Reuters, 16 Nov 2026). Nvidia slipped 2.2% premarket after its longest weekly losing streak since October 2012; Intel, AMD, Broadcom and Micron lost 3.5% to 5%, CoreWeave fell 5.4% and Nebius 5.5%.

**Why it matters:** De-risking is broad across chips and neoclouds into Tuesday's Nvidia print and Anthropic's debut: the premarket losses set the bar the earnings call has to clear.
