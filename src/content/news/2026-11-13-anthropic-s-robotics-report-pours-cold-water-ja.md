The report, covered on 13 Nov, finds robots will not replace human workers at scale for years because total deployment cost — hardware plus maintenance, training and integration — remains far above the cost of human labour.

**Why it matters:** Set against this week's production forecasts and channel orders, the report prices the other side of the humanoid trade: deployment cost, not unit demand, is the constraint it names.

**Anthropic机器人报告给人形机器人经济学泼冷水：单机5万–10万美元以上，部署成本仍高于人力**



11月13日报道的该报告认为，由于硬件加维护、培训与集成的总部署成本仍远高于人力成本，机器人多年内不会大规模替代人类劳动者。



与本周的产量预测和渠道订单相对照，该报告为机器人交易的另一面定价：它点名的约束是部署成本，而非单位需求。
