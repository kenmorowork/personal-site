Nikkei reported on 9 Nov that Terafab, the Tesla–SpaceX chip project in Texas, sent a procurement team to Japan in early November and asked some equipment makers for delivery dates only a few months away, to hold a target of volume production in H2 2028 (first test chip at the Austin research site in May–June 2027). Terafab has informally told suppliers it targets 1m wafers a month after 2032 — about 700,000 for memory and over 300,000 for logic — with Phase 1 investment in Grimes County put at $16.8bn. The targets are supplier briefings reported by Nikkei, not a Terafab confirmation. Figures: Nikkei via BigGo Finance, 9 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A 1m-wafer ambition is about 40 times Rapidus's 25,000-a-month Chitose plan — even a fraction of it reshuffles order books for Japanese toolmakers.
