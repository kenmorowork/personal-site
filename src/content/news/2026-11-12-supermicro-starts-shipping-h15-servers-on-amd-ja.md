Super Micro Computer (NASDAQ: SMCI) said on 12 Nov its H15 server portfolio, powered by 6th-generation AMD EPYC 9006 processors, is now shipping. The H15 generation delivers up to 33% more cores, twice the PCIe bandwidth and 1.6TB/s of memory bandwidth per socket versus the prior generation, across more than 20 systems for AI inference, HPC, cloud and enterprise workloads. Its Helios and Helion rack-scale solutions, built on Supermicro's Data Center Building Block Solutions and powered by AMD EPYC CPUs and Instinct GPUs (Helios uses MI455X), target large-scale and agentic AI deployments. Figures: Supermicro press release, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Shipping — not announcing — AMD's EPYC 9006 platform matters for the non-Nvidia server lane Ken tracks through Pegatron and Foxconn monthly sales.

**Supermicro开始出货搭载AMD EPYC 9006的H15服务器——核心数最多+33%，单插槽内存带宽1.6TB/s**



超微电脑（NASDAQ: SMCI）11月12日宣布，搭载第六代AMD EPYC 9006处理器的H15服务器产品线开始出货。H15一代较上一代核心数最多增加33%，PCIe带宽翻倍，单插槽内存带宽达1.6TB/s，产品线涵盖20多款系统，面向AI推理、HPC、云和企业负载。其Helios与Helion机架级方案基于Supermicro的数据中心构建模块方案，采用AMD EPYC CPU与Instinct GPU（Helios采用MI455X），面向大规模与智能体AI部署。数据来源：Supermicro新闻稿，2026年11月12日。



AMD EPYC 9006平台是“开始出货”而非“发布”，对Ken通过和硕、鸿海月度销售追踪的非英伟达服务器路线意义不同。
