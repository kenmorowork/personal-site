Profits rose at 12 of 15 major Japanese life insurers in the April-September half, Nikkei's survey showed. Premium income totalled about ¥22.8tn ($143bn), up 11% year on year, and core operating profit jumped 22% to ¥2.9tn; Sumitomo Life's profit rose 54% and Meiji Yasuda's operating profit rose 32%.

**Why it matters:** Insurers raised promised yields to customers — Sumitomo Life to the highest since 1998 — and investment returns still outpaced the higher payments.
