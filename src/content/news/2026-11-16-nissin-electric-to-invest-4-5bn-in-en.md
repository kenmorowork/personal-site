Nissin Electric held a ground-breaking on 16 Nov at its Maebashi works, investing ¥4.5 billion to refurbish a parts plant and build a welding plant (about 4,930 sq m) for GIS pressure vessels for substations, for completion in December 2027 (Nikkei, 16 Nov 2026). The expansion is explicitly aimed at power demand from AI data centres.

**Why it matters:** A mid-tier Japanese electrical maker is committing capex explicitly to AI power demand, with a December 2027 completion date for GIS capacity.
