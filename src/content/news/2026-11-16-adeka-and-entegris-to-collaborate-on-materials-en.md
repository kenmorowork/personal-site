ADEKA and Entegris agreed on 16 Nov to collaborate on domestic production in Japan of advanced semiconductor materials and related products for 3D semiconductor devices, aimed at strengthening the supply chain for AI infrastructure and data centres. ADEKA is building production facilities at its Kashima chemical plant in Kamisu, Ibaraki, with completion planned for June 2027 and commercial operation from October 2027. Source: ADEKA / Entegris press release via Nikkei, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Domestic production of 3D-device materials in Japan ties ADEKA's Kashima build to the AI supply chain, with June 2027 completion and October 2027 operation as the checkpoints.
