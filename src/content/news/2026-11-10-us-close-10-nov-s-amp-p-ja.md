US stocks closed lower on 10 Nov after the hot CPI print, though well off session lows: S&amp;P 500 −0.4%, Nasdaq Composite −0.7%, Dow −0.3%. The 10-year Treasury yield was around 4.98%, up from 4.90% at Monday's close, and Nvidia fell 5.5%, pacing Dow decliners. Figures: market close reporting via Investopedia, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Yields near 5% are the transmission channel from hot CPI to AI multiples — watch Nvidia into its 17 Nov print.
