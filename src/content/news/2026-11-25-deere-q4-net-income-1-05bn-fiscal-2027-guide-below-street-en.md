Deere & Company reported fourth-quarter net income of $1.051 billion, down 1% year on year, with worldwide net sales and revenues of $12.115 billion, down 2%. For fiscal 2027 the company forecast net income of $4.50 to $5.25 billion, including a pension settlement charge of $0.25 to $0.40 billion, against a Wall Street projection of about $5.91 billion. Full-year fiscal 2026 net income was $4.860 billion on revenues of $47.704 billion, up 4%.

**Why it matters:** Deere’s North America outlook (large agriculture up 5 to 15%, construction equipment flat to up 5%) is a direct read-across for Japanese machinery makers.
