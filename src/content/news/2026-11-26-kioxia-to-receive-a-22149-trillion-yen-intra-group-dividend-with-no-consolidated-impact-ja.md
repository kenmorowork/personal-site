title: "Kioxia to receive a ¥2.2149 trillion intra-group dividend with no consolidated impact, and its shares hit limit-up"
date: 2026-11-26
sector: "Semiconductors"
summary: "Kioxia Holdings (285A) will receive a ¥2.2149 trillion dividend from its consolidated subsidiary Kioxia Corporation, with receipt scheduled for November 27. The dividend is an internal transfer, will be recorded as operating revenue in the parent's standalone results for the year to March 2027, and has no impact on consolidated earnings. Its shares hit the daily limit-up price of ¥7,895 on November 26."
externalUrl: "https://japan.co.jp/e/japan-market-desk/"
lang: ja
---

The stock ended the morning of November 26 at ¥7,732, up 12.14%, on volume of more than 73.45 million shares, after falling 14.2% the previous day. No shareholder-return plan funded by the dividend has been officially announced. In the April-September half, Kioxia's consolidated net profit was ¥2,197.8bn, and a Nikkei ranking put its half-year profit improvement first at about ¥2.1 trillion.

**Why it matters:** A ¥2.2149 trillion internal dividend moves no consolidated profit, but it changes the parent's standalone capacity to return cash.

**铠侠将收到2.2149万亿日元集团内分红，对合并业绩无影响，股价触及涨停**

铠侠控股（285A）将从其合并子公司铠侠株式会社收到2.2149万亿日元分红，定于11月27日收取。该分红属于集团内部转移，将计入母公司截至2027年3月财年单体业绩的营业收入，对合并业绩没有影响。其股价在11月26日触及7,895日元的涨停价。
