Jereh Group, via GenSystems Power Solutions, unveiled the PowerVEGA SST at RE+ 2026 in Las Vegas on 20 Nov (PR Newswire, 22:44 JST): 3 MW rated, 13.8 kV AC input and 800V DC output, 98.5% peak efficiency, power density of 434 kW/m² (claimed 36% above mainstream competitors), 2300V SiC devices cutting component count by 51%, direct gas-turbine-to-SST coupling, on-site commissioning within 8 hours and 300-second hot-swap. It integrates transformer, distribution, UPS and power-quality functions.

**Why it matters:** Another named entrant in solid-state transformers for AI data centres, with 800V DC output and 98.5% peak efficiency, extends the SST field beyond the established names — direct gas-turbine coupling targets the grid-connection bottleneck.
