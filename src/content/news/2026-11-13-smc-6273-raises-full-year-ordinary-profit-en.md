SMC raised its FY ordinary profit forecast by 36.0% on 13 Nov, taking it above consensus, and announced a buyback of up to ¥50bn. The move adds factory-automation weight to a week of upward revisions across Japanese machinery and electronics names.

**Why it matters:** A 36.0% guide raise with a ¥50bn buyback from a core factory-automation name confirms the revision cycle is broadening from semiconductors into factory automation — directly relevant to the machinery coverage.
