US stocks closed sharply lower on Monday 16 Nov: Dow -1.26% to 51,688.96, S&P 500 -1.78% to 7,427.40, Nasdaq Composite -2.30% to 24,556.89, leaving the Nasdaq more than 10% below its 6 Oct record close and in correction territory; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 4.6%, with Intel and Micron each down more than 6%, as the AI selloff extended ahead of Nvidia's results on Tuesday and Anthropic's debut later in the week. Source: Reuters via The Korea Times, 16 Nov 2026 (published 17 Nov 03:02 JST).

**Why it matters:** A correction in the Nasdaq with the SOX down 4.6% resets positioning into Nvidia's Tuesday print: the earnings call now has to arrest a broad AI de-rating, not just beat consensus.
