TensorWave announced on 16 Nov a six-year agreement to provide OpenAI with 457 megawatts of dedicated IT capacity across multiple US data-centre sites, powered by AMD Instinct MI455X GPUs in purpose-built AMD Helios rack-scale systems and operated as single-tenant capacity. Capacity is expected to begin coming online in H1 2027, with full deployment by early 2028; no contract value was disclosed. TensorWave says it is the first neocloud publicly announced as an infrastructure provider under the AMD–OpenAI strategic partnership (6GW, announced Oct 2025). Source: TensorWave announcement via Business Wire, 16 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** 457MW is the first named, dated tranche of the 6GW AMD–OpenAI programme: H1 2027 first capacity gives the non-Nvidia rack build-out a concrete schedule to track.

**TensorWave签六年协议，为OpenAI提供457兆瓦AMD算力**



TensorWave于11月16日宣布一项六年协议：将为OpenAI在美国多个数据中心提供457兆瓦的专用IT容量，采用AMD Instinct MI455X GPU与专为AMD Helios机架级系统打造的基础设施，以单租户方式运营。容量预计于2027年上半年开始上线，2028年初完成全部部署；合同金额未披露。TensorWave称其为AMD与OpenAI战略合作（6吉瓦，2025年10月宣布）下首家公开宣布的新云基础设施提供商。来源：TensorWave公告（经Business Wire），2026年11月16日。



457兆瓦是6吉瓦AMD–OpenAI计划中第一个具名且有日期的批次：2027年上半年首批容量上线，为非英伟达机架建设给出了可跟踪的具体时间表。
