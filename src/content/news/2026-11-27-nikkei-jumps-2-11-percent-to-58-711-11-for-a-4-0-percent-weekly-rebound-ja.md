The Nikkei 225 closed up 1,211.95 points, or 2.11%, at 58,711.11 and the TOPIX gained 1.73% to 3,815.20, a second straight daily gain and a weekly rise of 4.0% after a 7.7% slide last week. Socionext rose 12.56%, Mitsui Kinzoku 11.53% and Lasertec 9.98%, with 160 advancers against 64 decliners.

**Why it matters:** The rebound broadened from battered AI names into domestic and defensive sectors, with advancers outnumbering decliners by 160 to 64.
