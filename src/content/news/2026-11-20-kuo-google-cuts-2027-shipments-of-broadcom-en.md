TF International analyst Ming-Chi Kuo's industry survey, posted on X on 20 November, puts the cut mainly in the higher-priced 8i Max (Whalefish); the size of the offsetting 8t increase is not final. Broadcom fell about 11% on Friday; neither Google nor Broadcom has commented, and Broadcom reports fiscal Q4 results on 9 December (Barron's, 2026-11-20; analyst survey, not a company disclosure).

**Why it matters:** A roughly 20% cut to 2027 TPU 8i shipments, concentrated in the higher-priced 8i Max and offset by an unquantified 8t increase, shifts the Google ASIC mix between Broadcom and MediaTek; Broadcom's fiscal Q4 results on 9 December are the next checkpoint.
