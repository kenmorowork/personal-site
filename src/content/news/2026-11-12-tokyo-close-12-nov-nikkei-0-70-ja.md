The Nikkei closed on 12 Nov at 60,819.36, down 429.23 points (-0.70%), its first fall in four sessions, after trading 465 points higher in the morning — an afternoon swing of about 895 points as semiconductor and AI names were sold on the Nvidia board reports. TOPIX rose 7.94 points (+0.20%) to 3,937.33, a fourth straight gain, with 62% of Prime names advancing; turnover was ¥9.09tn. Advantest was the largest single drag on the Nikkei. Figures: Jiji / Kyodo close data via Yahoo! Finance Japan, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A -0.70% index day with TOPIX positive is a positioning story, not a Japan story — the semi concentration of the Nikkei is the exposure to size.
