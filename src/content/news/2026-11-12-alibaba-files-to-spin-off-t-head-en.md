Alibaba announced on the evening of 10 Nov that it has filed to spin off its T-Head chip unit in a separate Hong Kong listing; T-Head's Zhenwu-series AI chips had shipped 560,000 units cumulatively as of April 2026, serving 650-plus enterprise customers across 20-plus industries. On 12 Nov China's semiconductor equipment names rallied — Cambricon rose over 11% back above ¥1,000, Hygon +6.90%, NAURA +6.31%, Piotech +5.89% and AMEC +5.39% in morning trade — against SEMI's forecast of $149bn in 2026 global 300mm front-end equipment spending, +31% y/y, with China the largest buyer. Sina also cites AMEC's record new orders in 2026 and ACM Research Shanghai's ¥17.073bn backlog, +88.2% y/y, with Q1-Q3 new orders +147%. Source: Sina Finance, 12 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A listed T-Head gives China's domestic AI silicon a valuation anchor — and the equipment order numbers are the direct read-across to Japanese SPE export exposure.
