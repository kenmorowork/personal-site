The US core PCE price index rose 0.3% month on month in October and 3.3% year on year, up from 3.1% in September and above the 3.2% forecast, the largest year-on-year advance since November 2023. Headline PCE rose 3.8% year on year. The Bureau of Economic Analysis also revised third-quarter GDP growth up to 4.3% annualised from the 3.4% first estimate. Personal income rose 0.7% in October and personal spending rose 0.3%.

**Why it matters:** Growth at 4.3% with core inflation at a three-year high is the combination the Federal Reserve's December meeting now prices against.
