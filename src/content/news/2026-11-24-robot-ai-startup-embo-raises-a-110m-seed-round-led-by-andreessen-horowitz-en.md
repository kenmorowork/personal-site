Embo announced on 23 Nov that it raised a $110m seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz to build AI models for robots, learning physical skills from internet video and simulated practice, then fine-tuning on its own humanoid fleet. The team says it has built training infrastructure across 70,000 GPUs. Figures: company announcement, 23 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A seed round above $100m for robot foundation models shows the humanoid contest shifting toward capital-intensive training infrastructure, not only hardware.
