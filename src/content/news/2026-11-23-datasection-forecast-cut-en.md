Datasection (3905) cut its FY3/27 revenue forecast from ¥162.2bn to ¥17.3bn — a 89.3% reduction — and now expects a net loss of ¥1.48bn. A ¥79.9bn AI infrastructure project was cancelled, with possible knock-on effects for Taiwanese server makers' B200/B300 orders.
