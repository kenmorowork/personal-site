Morgan Stanley's second AlphaWise survey of 94 Chinese manufacturing, industrial and services executives (CLS via Sina Finance, 11 Nov) raised the 2026 China humanoid shipment forecast to 50,000 units from an initial 14,000, with 100,000 seen for 2027 and 446,000 for 2030; 64% of firms may adopt within three years (2025 survey: 62%), but 72% say the price must fall below RMB 100,000 (2025: 53%) and 82% require payback within three years, at an average of 26 months.

**Why it matters:** Shipments are being revised up while deployment is revised later — the same tension as Anthropic's humanoid-economics report in the morning run, now quantified from the buyer side in China, the largest early market.
