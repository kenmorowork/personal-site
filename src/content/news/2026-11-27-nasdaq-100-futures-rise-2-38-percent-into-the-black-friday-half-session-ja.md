US stock futures rose ahead of a shortened Black Friday session, with Nasdaq-100 futures up 2.38%, S&P 500 futures up 1.74% and Dow futures up 1.30% at 05:41 ET. Stocks close at 1 p.m. ET and bonds at 2 p.m. ET, while Brent crude rose 0.72% to $97.27 a barrel and WTI rose 2.60% to $85.80.

**Why it matters:** The futures gains were printed in holiday-thin trade, in a session lasting three and a half hours against a normal six and a half.
