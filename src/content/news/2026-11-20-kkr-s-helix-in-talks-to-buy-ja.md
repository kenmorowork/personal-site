Helix Digital Infrastructure, launched by KKR this year with about $11bn raised from backers including Nvidia and the Kuwait Investment Authority, is negotiating to buy Partners Group's majority stake in EdgeCore, valuing the operator at $8–10bn. EdgeCore has more than 2.27 GW leased and under development, including a Louisa County, Virginia campus of more than 1.1 GW; talks may still end without a deal (Bloomberg via Moneycontrol, 2026-11-20).

**Why it matters:** A change of control at EdgeCore at an $8–10bn valuation, covering more than 2.27 GW leased and under development, would rank among the largest data-centre platform deals of the year — if the talks conclude.

**据彭博报道，KKR旗下Helix洽谈以最高100亿美元估值收购EdgeCore多数股权**



KKR今年设立、已从英伟达和科威特投资局等出资方募集约110亿美元的Helix Digital Infrastructure，正洽谈收购Partners Group持有的EdgeCore多数股权，对该运营商估值80亿至100亿美元。EdgeCore已租赁及在建容量超过2.27吉瓦，其中弗吉尼亚州路易莎县园区超过1.1吉瓦；谈判仍可能无果而终（彭博经Moneycontrol报道，2026-11-20）。



EdgeCore若以80亿至100亿美元估值易主，涉及逾2.27吉瓦已租赁及在建容量，将成为今年最大的数据中心平台交易之一——前提是谈判能够达成。
