The S&P Global flash Japan manufacturing PMI rose to 53.7 in November from 53.3 in October, above the 53.5 consensus, with output at 53.8 and new orders at 53.1 on semiconductor and electronics demand. The services PMI slipped to 50.5 from 51.7 and the composite eased to 51.5, a six-month low.

**Why it matters:** Manufacturing and services are moving in opposite directions: chip demand is carrying factories while domestic services activity cools.
