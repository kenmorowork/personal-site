TSMC has told affected Chinese designers that orders at 16nm and below are halted unless they obtain a US export licence, after new US Commerce rules took effect Thu 19 Nov; affected customers are generally limited to 28nm/40nm, with no HBM or advanced packaging (Reuters, citing TSMC guidance). The FT reported TSMC and Samsung Electronics suspended some shipments to Chinese customers; the US approved-designer list includes Nvidia, AMD, Apple, MediaTek and Realtek, and no mainland Chinese firms.

**Why it matters:** The manufacturing loophole is now closed at the foundry gate: Chinese designers' US licence applications are the next checkpoint, and 28nm/40nm is the ceiling until they clear.

**台积电收紧对华AI芯片代工：16纳米及以下无美国许可停单，新规堵住制造环节漏洞**



在美国商务部新规于11月19日（周四）生效后，台积电已告知受影响的中国设计公司，16纳米及以下订单除非取得美国出口许可否则暂停；受影响客户一般被限制在28纳米/40纳米，且不涉及HBM或先进封装（路透，引台积电指引）。英国《金融时报》报道台积电与三星电子已暂停向部分中国客户出货；美国批准的设计公司名单包括英伟达、AMD、苹果、联发科和瑞昱，不含中国大陆企业。



制造环节的漏洞如今已在代工厂门口堵住：中国设计公司的美国许可申请是下一个检验点，在获批之前28纳米/40纳米就是上限。
