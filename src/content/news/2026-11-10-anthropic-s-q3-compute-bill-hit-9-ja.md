Anthropic's amended S-1 (carried by CLS on 10 Nov) shows Q3 revenue of $16.96bn and compute/infrastructure expense of $9.9bn, 58% of revenue vs 48% in Q2, including $5.0bn bought on short-term commitments priced well above multi-year deals. Total purchase and lease commitments reached ~$895.4bn, substantially all compute-related. Figures: Anthropic S-1/A via CLS, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Compute at 58% of revenue, with $5.0bn bought short-term at premium prices — scarcity pricing flows to whoever owns capacity.

Source: CLS · 财联社 / Anthropic S-1/A · 10 Nov — [original article](https://www.cls.cn/subject/9086)

**Anthropic三季度算力支出达99亿美元，占收入58%——短期算力采购成本高企**



Anthropic修订版S-1（财联社11月10日报道）显示：三季度收入169.6亿美元，计算与基础设施支出99亿美元，占收入58%（二季度为48%），其中50亿美元为短期采购，价格显著高于多年期合约。采购与租赁承诺总额约8954亿美元，绝大部分与算力相关。数据来源：Anthropic S-1/A（财联社），2026年11月10日。



算力占收入58%，其中50亿美元以高价短期采购——稀缺定价正流向拥有产能的一方。
