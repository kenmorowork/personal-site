Nvidia reported Q3 FY27 (quarter ended 25 Oct 2026) revenue of $115.8bn, up 103% year on year and 20% quarter on quarter, above the LSEG consensus of about $109.1bn; data-centre revenue was $107.6bn, up 110%. GAAP gross margin was 74.8% (non-GAAP 74.9%), GAAP EPS $2.70, net income $65.3bn. Q4 guidance is $130.0bn plus or minus 3%, assuming no data-centre compute revenue from China, with gross margin guided to 71-72%. Vera Rubin NVL72 has begun shipping in what the company called its fastest product ramp; the board approved a further $150bn of buyback authorisation and the company returned $28.1bn in the quarter. Interim CEO Colette Kress presented the results; separate reports said the board does not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang. Source: GlobeNewswire via Manila Times, 18 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The print clears the bar the whole AI chain was sold into: revenue $115.8bn vs about $109.1bn consensus, data-centre +110%, and a $130bn Q4 guide — but the guide assumes zero China data-centre compute revenue and a 71-72% gross margin, so the FY28 growth comment and the margin path are what the US session will trade on.

**英伟达第三季度营收翻倍至1,158亿美元，数据中心收入增长110%至1,076亿美元；第四季度指引为1,300亿美元**



英伟达公布2027财年第三季度（截至2026年10月25日）业绩：营收1,158亿美元，同比增长103%、环比增长20%，高于LSEG一致预期的约1,091亿美元；数据中心收入1,076亿美元，同比增长110%。GAAP毛利率74.8%（非GAAP 74.9%），GAAP每股收益2.70美元，净利润653亿美元。第四季度指引为1,300亿美元±3%，假设不含来自中国的数据中心计算收入，毛利率指引为71–72%。Vera Rubin NVL72已开始出货，公司称其为史上最快的产品爬坡；董事会再批准1,500亿美元回购授权，本季度回购281亿美元。业绩由临时CEO Colette Kress发布；另有报道称董事会无意让黄仁勋复职。来源：GlobeNewswire（经Manila Times），2026年11月18日。



这份业绩清除了整个AI链被抛售时所面对的门槛：营收1,158亿美元对一致预期约1,091亿美元，数据中心增长110%，四季度指引1,300亿美元——但指引假设中国数据中心计算收入为零、毛利率71–72%，因此美股时段将交易的是FY28增长评论与毛利率路径。
