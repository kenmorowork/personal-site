TSMC's board on 10 Nov approved buying up to US$500m of Anthropic common shares at its IPO price and up to US$500m of OpenAI Group PBC preferred stock — ceilings, not completed purchases, with no share count or timetable disclosed. The same meeting approved about US$40.93bn of capital appropriations for advanced-process and advanced-packaging capacity, fab construction and 2027 R&amp;D, up to US$500m to set up a strategic investment company, and a Q3 cash dividend of NT$9.0 per share, up from NT$7.0. Figures: TSMC board resolutions, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** The foundry that makes most AI accelerators is becoming a shareholder in its two largest model customers — and the $40.9bn appropriation sets the 2027 wafer and packaging supply pace.
