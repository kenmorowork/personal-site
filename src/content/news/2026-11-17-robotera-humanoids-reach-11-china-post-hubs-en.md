ROBOTERA said on 17 Nov its humanoid robots have been deployed across China Post Group Guangdong Branch's network, reaching 11 postal hubs in 8 cities including Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Dongguan — described as the largest-scale deployment of embodied-intelligence robots in logistics to date. Since the pilot began at the Guangzhou Postal District Center in March 2026, the robots have achieved an average induction rate of more than 1,500 parcels per hour, comparable to experienced human workers. Source: ROBOTERA release via PR Newswire / AsiaOne, 17 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** This is a deployment metric, not a demo: a named operator, 11 sites and a parcels-per-hour rate at human parity give the humanoid coverage a dated, measurable proof point outside the US names.
