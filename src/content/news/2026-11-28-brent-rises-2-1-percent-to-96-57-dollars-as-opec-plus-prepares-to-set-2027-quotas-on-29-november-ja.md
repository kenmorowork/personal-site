Brent crude rose 2.1% to $96.57 a barrel, up about 8% in three sessions, with WTI close to $86, as OPEC+ ministers prepare to set 2027 quotas on 29 November with about 2 million barrels a day of cuts in place. MUFG forecasts Brent to average $88.75 in 2027, down from $93 in 2026.

**Why it matters:** Brent is up 8% in three sessions into a quota meeting whose bank forecasts point lower for 2027, so the premium is event risk.

**布伦特原油上涨2.1%至每桶96.57美元，OPEC+将于11月29日制定2027年配额**

布伦特原油上涨2.1%至每桶96.57美元，三个交易日累计上涨约8%，WTI接近每桶86美元。OPEC+部长将于11月29日制定2027年配额，目前约200万桶/日的减产仍在实施。三菱日联预计2027年布伦特均价为88.75美元，低于2026年的93美元。
