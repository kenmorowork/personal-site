The Bank of Japan's Corporate Services Price Index rose to 116.9 in October, up 4.2% year on year, the fastest pace since March 1990 and up from 3.8% in September. Ocean freight transport prices rose 120.4% year on year, and labour-intensive services prices rose 2.7%. The index also rose 1.1% month on month.

**Why it matters:** Services inflation at a 36-year high is a domestic price signal that feeds directly into the Bank of Japan’s rate decisions.
