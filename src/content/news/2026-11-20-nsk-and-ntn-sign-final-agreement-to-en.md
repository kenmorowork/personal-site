Disclosed on TDnet at 16:30 JST on 20 Nov: a joint holding company will be established by share transfer on 1 October 2027 (NSK 1 share → 1 holding-company share; NTN 1 share → 0.353 shares), with both companies delisting on 29 September 2027. Reported synergy target is ¥50–60bn on consolidated operating profit, with ROE above 10% targeted around the year ending March 2031 and holding-company capital of ¥60bn. Combined world bearing share is about 24% on a simple sum (NSK 13.3%, world No.3; NTN 10.6%, No.4), ahead of SKF's 16.4% (Nikkei).

**Why it matters:** The merger creates a bearing maker with about 24% world share, ahead of SKF, with a ¥50–60bn operating-profit synergy target and ROE above 10% eyed around the year ending March 2031 — the largest consolidation in Japan's bearing industry, with the holding company set for 1 October 2027.
