A 13 Nov market snapshot shows investors rotating out of Nvidia after Jensen Huang's departure — NVDA down about 11% week-to-date — into AMD (about +30%), Broadcom (about +24%) and TSMC (about +11%). AMD's Q3 revenue rose 45% to $13.4bn with data-centre revenue +97% to $8.5bn (3 Nov results); Nvidia reports on 17 Nov US time.

**Why it matters:** Rotation, not exit, is now quantified for the week — AMD and Broadcom gains against Nvidia's fall set the relative-value frame going into Nvidia's 17 Nov report.
