Segment sales were Domestic Beverage ¥102.81bn (down 6.6%), Overseas Beverage ¥54.8bn (up 13.4%), Food ¥14.52bn (down 9.3%) and Pharmaceutical-related ¥9.5bn (down 5.7%). The operating margin rose to 6.1% from 2.8%, EPS was ¥179.07 against ¥14.80 a year earlier, and the equity ratio was 44.5%. Net profit attributable to the parent was ¥5.68bn, against ¥0.47bn a year earlier.

**Why it matters:** Profit more than doubled on lower sales — the entire gain came from the domestic turnaround and a ¥3.75bn SG&A cut, not from demand.

**DyDo（2590）前三季度营业利润翻倍至112.1亿日元，销售额下降1.4%至1823.6亿日元，国内饮料业务扭亏为盈**

DyDo集团前三季度销售额下降1.4%至1823.6亿日元，营业利润增长114.8%至112.1亿日元，经常利润增长164.6%至82.2亿日元。海外饮料业务盈利72.4亿日元，国内饮料业务由亏损12.1亿日元转为盈利50.9亿日元，销售及管理费用削减37.5亿日元，全年预期维持不变，营业利润进度达91.1%。
