Japan's semiconductor manufacturing equipment sales rose 48.2% year on year in the July-September quarter to ¥1.89tn, about $11.9bn, according to Nikkei Asia reporting. Tokyo Electron said customer demand for expedited delivery and increased purchases continues, and Advantest plans to raise annual tester production from over 5,000 units to 10,000 units by the end of 2028.

**Why it matters:** A 48.2% sales increase with the largest tester maker doubling capacity — equipment supply is still expanding into AI-driven demand.
