Taiwan's Allis Electric told its 20 Nov investor conference that its order backlog has reached NT$13bn, driven by AI data-centre construction, semiconductor fab expansion at home and abroad, and Taipower's grid-resilience plan. AI/IDC orders of about NT$1.5bn have already been booked this year, versus the NT$0.5–0.6bn a year such demand used to contribute; a second transformer plant lifts transformer capacity to about NT$1.5bn. (MoneyDJ, 23 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** Even Taiwan's local electrical-equipment makers are stacking record backlogs — the grid-equipment squeeze is broadening beyond the global majors.

**亚力电机在手订单达130亿新台币，AIDC建设与半导体扩厂带动需求**

亚力电机（1514）在11月20日法说会表示，受AI数据中心建设、半导体海内外扩厂及台电强韧电网计划推动，在手订单已达130亿新台币。今年已接获电信及电子大厂约15亿新台币的AI/IDC订单，远高于过去每年约5亿至6亿的水平；变压器二厂投产后，变压器产能可提高至15亿新台币。（MoneyDJ，2026年11月23日）
