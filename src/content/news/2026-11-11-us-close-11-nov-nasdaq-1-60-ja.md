US stocks rose on 11 Nov: the S&amp;P 500 +1.31% to 7,619.38, the Nasdaq +1.60% to 25,466.19 and the Dow +0.49% to 52,486.49. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 5.66% to 10,564.11, with all 30 constituents higher — Arm +12.05%, Micron +11.89%, Intel +7.46%, AMD +7.32%, Lam Research +6.07% and Applied Materials +6.05%. CoreWeave surged 24.71% to $60.77 after Q3 revenue of $3.661bn beat the ~$3.54bn consensus and it raised 2026 revenue guidance to $13.0–13.3bn. Nvidia rose 2.87% to $173.73 after falling nearly 10% over the first two days of the week. The VIX fell about 10% to 17.49. Figures: TradingKey US close, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A broad chip rebound, not a single-name bounce — all 30 SOX names rose — after CoreWeave's beat-and-raise eased the debt-funded AI capex fear that drove last week's selloff. Tonight's test is whether Nebius, Cisco and Applied Materials confirm the same demand.
