The planner interprets spoken instructions, generates machining tasks and coordinates both systems — talking to the CNC via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and to the robot via ROS — using Mitsubishi Electric's Maisart AI. Actions affecting the physical world require human approval; the company is in validation research and sees practical use around 2030.

**Why it matters:** A humanoid tending a CNC cell under an AI planner — human approval required for physical actions, practical use eyed around 2030 — gives the machine-tool automation story a dated, named demo at JIMTOF rather than a concept claim.

**三菱电机在JIMTOF 2026演示人形机器人操作CNC机床，Galaxea机器人与AI加工任务规划器协同作业**



该规划器可理解语音指令、生成加工任务并协调两套系统——通过模型上下文协议（MCP）与CNC通信、通过ROS与机器人通信，并采用三菱电机的Maisart AI。涉及物理世界的操作需经人工批准；公司目前处于验证研究阶段，预计2030年前后实现实用化。



在AI规划器调度下由人形机器人照料CNC单元——涉及物理世界的操作需经人工批准、实用化目标约在2030年——使工作机械自动化在JIMTOF有了一个有日期、具名的演示，而非概念层面的宣称。
