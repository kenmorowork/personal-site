Data-centre developer Applied Digital has asked tenants including Oracle and Meta for billions of dollars in guarantees securing grid-connection power before signing leases, Bloomberg reported on 15 November, and is seeking case-by-case guarantees from Nvidia and AMD. It has capped capacity leased to junk-rated neoclouds, with CEO Wes Cummins stating a preferred mix of 70% investment-grade to 30% non-investment-grade tenants. Hyperscalers are expected to issue $300–320bn of debt this year, per Barclays estimates cited in the report.

**Why it matters:** Power access is being underwritten like credit: guarantee demands shift grid-connection risk onto tenants and favour investment-grade balance sheets over neocloud growth.
