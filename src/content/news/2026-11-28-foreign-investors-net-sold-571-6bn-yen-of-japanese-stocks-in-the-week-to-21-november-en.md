Foreign investors withdrew a net ¥571.6 billion ($3.59bn) from Japanese stocks in the week to 21 November, the largest weekly net sale since the week ended 19 September and more than double the prior week's ¥238.0 billion, according to Ministry of Finance data. Japanese investors also turned net sellers of overseas equities, offloading ¥350.8 billion and reversing nearly all of the previous week's ¥363.1 billion of net purchases.

**Why it matters:** Both sides of the border sold at once — foreigners cut Japan by ¥571.6bn while Japanese investors cut overseas equities by ¥350.8bn; this was de-risking, not rotation.
