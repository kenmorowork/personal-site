Mainland venture funding rotated back into semiconductors in October–November as embodied-intelligence deals cooled, according to CLS Chuangtou Tong data reported by UDN (15 Nov 2026). Integrated circuits drew RMB 11.1bn in the week of 31 Oct–6 Nov — over half of all disclosed mainland financing that week — and Q3 semiconductor financing reached 631 deals worth RMB 109.243bn, up 52.42% in deal count and 91.20% in value y/y (Tochung Research Institute). Embodied intelligence, by contrast, slowed to 33 deals worth RMB 10.105bn in October from 60 deals worth RMB 11.028bn in September; at least 10 AI-chip firms closed rounds in October, and advanced-packaging names closed multi-billion-yuan rounds in early November.

**Why it matters:** Funding momentum is a leading indicator for China's chip-equipment order pipeline — and the humanoid funding cooldown cross-checks Morgan Stanley's slower adoption timeline already on this board.

**中国创投资金重回半导体：集成电路一周吸金111亿元、占全市场过半，具身智能降温**



据联合新闻网11月15日引述财联社创投通数据，中国大陆创投资金在10至11月从具身智能回流半导体。10月31日至11月6日一周，集成电路融资111亿元，占当周全市场披露融资额的一半以上；投中研究院数据显示，三季度半导体融资达631起、1,092.43亿元，交易数同比+52.42%、金额同比+91.20%。相比之下，具身智能10月降至33起、101.05亿元（9月为60起、110.28亿元）；10月至少10家AI芯片公司完成新一轮融资，11月初先进封装企业亦完成数十亿元级融资。



融资动能是中国芯片设备订单管线的前瞻指标；而具身智能融资降温，也与本看板已有的摩根士丹利放缓采用时间表相互印证。
