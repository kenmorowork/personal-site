GlobalFoundries' Q3 2026 results (released 4 Nov; detailed recap 14 Nov) show revenue of $1.899bn and a record non-IFRS gross margin of 31.4%, up 540bp y/y, with communications infrastructure and data-centre revenue up 81% y/y to about 17% of total revenue on silicon photonics and SiGe demand for AI data centres. The company raised its 2026 silicon photonics revenue outlook to $500m and expects an annualised run rate of about $1bn by end-2027; Q4 guidance is revenue of about $2.0bn at a non-IFRS gross margin of about 31.5%.

**Why it matters:** The Malta groundbreaking in the morning run is the capacity story; this is the margin story — optical interconnect demand inside AI data centres is what is repricing GlobalFoundries' mix, and the $1bn photonics run-rate target for end-2027 is the number to monitor.
