US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick faces public criticism from Treasury Secretary Bessent — who said on CNBC on 13 Nov that he backed adding Singapore's Megaspeed, suspected of diverting advanced Nvidia AI chips to China, to the Entity List — and prediction market Kalshi now prices a 58% probability that Lutnick leaves office by year-end (BigGo Finance, 15 Nov 2026). BigGo also notes Cantor Fitzgerald cut its Nvidia holding from about 3.5m shares ($652m, Sep 2025) to 1.78m shares ($310m, end-Mar 2026), and that US government equity deals since January 2025 total 49 deals worth $30.8bn, including about 10% of Intel (CFR tally).

**Why it matters:** Export-control enforcement is now a personnel risk at Commerce — who runs the Entity List process matters for Nvidia and equipment shipments into Asia, and this follows the Megaspeed item already on this board.

**Kalshi定价商务部长卢特尼克年底前离任概率58%——英伟达芯片出口争议发酵**



美国商务部长卢特尼克因未将涉嫌把英伟达先进AI芯片转运至中国的新加坡企业Megaspeed列入实体清单，遭到财政部长贝森特公开批评（贝森特11月13日在CNBC表示支持列入）；预测市场Kalshi现定价卢特尼克年底前离任的概率为58%（BigGo Finance，2026年11月15日）。BigGo还指出，Cantor Fitzgerald的英伟达持股已从约350万股（2025年9月、6.52亿美元）降至178万股（2026年3月底、3.10亿美元）；自2025年1月以来美国政府股权交易共49笔、308亿美元，包括约10%的英特尔股份（CFR统计）。



出口管制执行如今已演变为商务部的人事风险——谁主导实体清单流程，关系到英伟达及设备对亚洲的出货；此事承接本看板已有的Megaspeed条目。
