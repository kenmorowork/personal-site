Japan's state-backed chipmaker Rapidus is reportedly facing mounting technological challenges that could delay its plan to begin mass-producing 2nm semiconductors by March 2028, potentially undermining Tokyo's efforts to rebuild domestic advanced semiconductor manufacturing. The report gives no revised date. Figures: DigiTimes, 11 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** Rapidus is the centrepiece of Japan's advanced-logic bet; a slip at 2nm matters for the equipment and materials order book and for the Terafab comparison, where the test-chip target is mid-2027.
