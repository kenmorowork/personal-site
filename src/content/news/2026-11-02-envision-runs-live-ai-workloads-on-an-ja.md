Envision said on 2 Nov that an integrated 800V DC power system — solid-state transformers converting 10kV AC directly to an 800V DC bus, plus battery storage and coordinated controls — is in commercial operation powering live workloads at its Galaxy AI campus in Ulanqab, which began operating in August 2026. The campus is planned at 3GW, designed for up to one million AI accelerators, with over 80% of power planned from direct renewable connections. Envision has not disclosed the live installation's capacity, measured efficiency, or cost. Figures: Envision announcement, 2 Nov 2026; planned campus figures are company targets, not operating results.

**Why it matters:** 800V DC moves from Nvidia's roadmap to a live site — the reference case for solid-state transformers versus the conventional transformer chain Ken covers.

Source: Envision announcement · 2 Nov 2026, via Data Centre Magazine · 9 Nov — [original article](https://datacentremagazine.com/news/envisions-800v-dc-data-centre-world-first-in-ulanqab-china)

**远景在内蒙古以一体化800V直流系统运行真实AI负载——称全球首次商业部署**



远景11月2日称，一套一体化800V直流供电系统——以固态变压器将10kV交流直接转换为800V直流母线，配合储能与协同控制——已在其乌兰察布Galaxy AI园区投入商业运行并承载真实负载，该园区于2026年8月投运。园区规划容量3GW，设计支持至多100万颗AI加速器，80%以上电力计划来自可再生能源直连。远景未披露在运系统的容量、实测效率或成本。数据来源：远景公告，2026年11月2日；园区规划数字为公司目标，非运营结果。



800V直流从英伟达路线图走向真实站点——成为固态变压器对传统变压器链的参考案例。
