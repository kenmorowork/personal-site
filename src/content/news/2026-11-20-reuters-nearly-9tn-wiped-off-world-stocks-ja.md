Reuters' global-markets wrap (20 Nov): nearly $9 trillion has been wiped off the value of world stocks since they peaked in August, and Taiwan's 17% plunge this week is its biggest since the early 1990s. The VIX reached 30 on Friday, its highest since late March; the US 10-year Treasury yield is on track for its biggest weekly fall since April 2025, down 17bp on the week; copper is on course for its steepest weekly drop since April 2025; and the dollar index has risen to a 19-month high this week. Gold fell about 1% on Friday to its lowest since September 2025.

**Why it matters:** Nearly $9tn erased since August, the VIX at 30 and the dollar at a 19-month high describe a global de-risking, not a single-stock story — the weekly falls in Treasury yields and copper show haven and industrial flows moving together.

**路透：全球股市自8月高点以来已蒸发近9万亿美元，台湾股市本周下跌17%**



路透社全球市场综述（11月20日）：全球股市市值自8月见顶以来已减少近9万亿美元，台湾股市本周下跌17%，为1990年代初以来最大单周跌幅。VIX周五升至30，为3月下旬以来最高；美国10年期国债收益率本周下跌17个基点，有望创2025年4月以来最大单周跌幅；铜价有望创2025年4月以来最大单周跌幅；美元指数本周升至19个月高位。黄金周五下跌约1%，创2025年9月以来最低。



自8月以来蒸发近9万亿美元、VIX升至30、美元创19个月新高，描绘的是一场全球性的去风险而非个股故事——国债收益率与铜价的周度跌幅显示避险与工业资金正在同步移动。
