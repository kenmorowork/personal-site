TSMC reported October consolidated revenue of NT$586.89bn on 10 Nov — its highest monthly total in records back to 1999, up 59.7% y/y and 14.7% m/m, topping August's previous record of NT$514.81bn. Jan–Oct revenue reached NT$4,485.62bn, +43.3% y/y. The monthly release gives no node or customer breakdown. Figures: TSMC October revenue report, 10 Nov 2026.

**Why it matters:** A record month with no slowdown signal — wafer starts underpin the equipment and materials names reporting today.

**台积电10月营收创纪录：5868.9亿新台币，同比+59.7%**



台积电11月10日公布10月合并营收5868.9亿新台币，为1999年有记录以来最高单月，同比+59.7%、环比+14.7%，超过8月5148.1亿新台币的前纪录。1–10月累计营收44856.2亿新台币，同比+43.3%。月度公告未披露制程或客户结构。数据来源：台积电10月营收公告，2026年11月10日。
