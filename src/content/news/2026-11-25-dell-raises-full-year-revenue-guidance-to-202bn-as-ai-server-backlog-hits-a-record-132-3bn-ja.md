Dell reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $54.94bn, up 103% year on year and above the $49.32bn consensus, with AI-optimised server revenue up 291% to $22bn and $60.2bn of new AI orders lifting the backlog to a record $132.3bn. Fiscal 2027 revenue guidance was raised to $202bn from $192bn, AI server revenue to $78bn from $74bn, and adjusted EPS to $29.68 from $25.50.

**Why it matters:** A $132.3bn backlog that management says is vetted for capital, land and power is the demand anchor for the whole AI data-centre supply chain.

**戴尔将全年营收指引上调至2020亿美元，AI服务器订单积压创纪录达1323亿美元**
