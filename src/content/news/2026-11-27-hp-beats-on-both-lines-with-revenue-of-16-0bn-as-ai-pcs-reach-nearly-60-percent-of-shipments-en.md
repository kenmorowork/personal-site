HP's fiscal fourth-quarter revenue of $16.0bn beat the $15.4bn consensus, and adjusted earnings of $0.83 per share beat the $0.75 expected. Personal Systems revenue was $11.8bn, AI PCs reached nearly 60% of shipments, and the quarterly dividend was raised to $0.31 per share. Fiscal 2027 non-GAAP earnings were guided to $3.15-3.45 per share, with free cash flow of $2.9-3.1bn.

**Why it matters:** On the same earnings night, hardware names were rewarded and software names fell — HP's quarter was carried by AI PC mix and cash returns.
