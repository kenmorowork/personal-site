Major server vendors including Dell and HPE are reported to be seeking additional price increases even on already-contracted deals, as Nvidia GPU server component costs rise faster than expected. The report frames AI infrastructure build-out costs as a growing burden on enterprise AI plans. (Digitimes via Amiko Consulting, 24 Nov 2026)

**Why it matters:** If server prices rise after contracts are signed, the unit economics of AI data-centre build-outs shift for every buyer downstream.
