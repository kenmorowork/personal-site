Nvidia is funding multibillion-dollar advanced-packaging expansion at SPIL in Taiwan and supporting Amkor in Arizona and South Korea, while Vera Rubin NVL72 and Vera CPUs began volume shipments in Q3 on TSMC 5nm/3nm and CoWoS (BigGo Finance, 19 Nov). Chinese A-share advanced-packaging stocks rallied on the news, and TSMC is reported to be considering a 10-20% CoWoS price increase.

**Why it matters:** Packaging and substrates, not wafers, are the binding constraint for Rubin-era supply: $390bn of commitments and a possible CoWoS price rise flow straight to TSMC, Amkor and the ABF substrate names.
