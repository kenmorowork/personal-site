Preliminary closes (Reuters, 20 Nov): S&P 500 −3.59% to 6,918.45, Nasdaq Composite −5.13% to 22,654.15, Dow −1.85% to 48,188.18. The S&P 500 and Dow closed more than 10% below their record closing highs, confirming corrections; the S&P 500's seventh straight loss is its longest streak since February 2020, and the PHLX Semiconductor Index sank 9.2%. Nikkei futures changed hands at 54,600 on Saturday morning in Tokyo, 1,820 points below Friday's cash close.

**Why it matters:** A confirmed correction in both the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, a seventh straight S&P 500 loss and a 9.2% one-day fall in the semiconductor index turn the AI unwind into a broad market event; Nikkei futures at 54,600 set up a sharply lower Tokyo reopen on Tuesday.

标普500与纳斯达克双双确认进入调整区间、标普七连跌、半导体指数单日重挫9.2%，使AI交易的回调演变为全面的市场事件；日经期货54,600点预示周二东京市场将大幅低开。
